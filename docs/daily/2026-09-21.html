Identifying functional TRs is essential for understanding development, tissue organization, and disease. Emerging spatial transcriptomics and epigenomics technologies now provide near-single-cell resolution mapping of genomic features while preserving information of each cell's physical location and microenvironment which influence TR activity. These findings support the adoption of a unified haplotype nomenclature to clarify population genetic structure and enhance the consistency of genetic assessments for conservation applications.
