Benchmarking demonstrated a consistent advantage of properly designed deep learning models over traditional positional weight matrices and other machine learning methods. Here we describe the detailed organization of the specialized brain of Xandarella spectaculum, a member of the subphylum Trilobitomorpha which went extinct at the end Permian. With reference to existing neurological attributes of living pancrustaceans major divergences of the trilobitomorph brain from the ancestral cerebral ground pattern may have weakened its resilience to geo-ecological disruptions and hence the final extinction of the clade to which trilobitomorphs belong.
