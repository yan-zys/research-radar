Structural Evolution of Neural Networks during Zebrafish Early Development
斑马鱼早期发育中神经网络的结构演化
为揭示神经网络早期建立的动态过程，作者利用转基因斑马鱼的长期共聚焦活细胞成像技术，在单细胞分辨率下解析脑、卵黄囊和脊髓中的神经网络发育，发现生长锥反复分叉是网络节点形成的核心细胞机制，尽管起源不同，三个区域的网络均遵循Strahler层级排序和Hack幂律标度，且节点数和聚类系数随发育增加而网络密度降低，反映了功能效率与布线成本平衡的结构优化策略。

推荐理由：论文聚焦斑马鱼早期发育中神经网络结构的物理拓扑变化，属于发育神经生物学范畴，缺乏演化或跨物种比较视角，未涉及细胞类型鉴定、同源性比较或基因调控网络演化。
背景：神经网络的空间架构是神经系统功能的基础，但关于其在体动态早期建立过程的证据仍然稀缺。理解神经网络如何从少数神经元逐步发育为具有复杂拓扑结构的系统，对于揭示神经系统自组织原则具有重要意义。斑马鱼作为模式生物，其胚胎透明且发育迅速，为长期活体成像研究神经网络形成提供了理想系统。
研究方法：研究者利用转基因斑马鱼进行长期共聚焦活细胞成像，在单细胞分辨率下追踪脑、卵黄囊和脊髓区域的神经网络发育过程。研究采用Strahler层级排序和Hack幂律标度等拓扑分析方法，对网络结构的动态变化进行定量表征，包括节点数、聚类系数和网络密度等参数。
研究结果：研究发现生长锥反复分叉是神经网络节点形成的基本细胞机制。尽管脑、卵黄囊和脊髓具有不同的发育起源，三个区域的神经网络均表现出高度保守的动态特征，严格遵循Strahler层级排序和Hack幂律标度。定量拓扑分析显示，随着发育进行，节点数量和聚类系数逐渐增加，而网络密度降低，这一模式反映了神经系统在功能效率与布线成本之间寻求平衡的结构优化策略。
研究意义：该研究揭示了物理几何约束与简单局部规则如何共同驱动复杂神经网络的自组织，为理解神经系统发育的普遍原则提供了重要见解。研究结果对于人工神经网络的设计具有借鉴意义，但在细胞类型多样性、跨物种比较及演化机制方面未作探讨，对演化神经生物学研究的直接参考价值有限。
中文全摘要：神经网络的空间架构是神经系统功能的基础，但其在体动态建立的证据仍然匮乏。本研究利用转基因斑马鱼的长期共聚焦活细胞成像，在单细胞分辨率下解析脑、卵黄囊和脊髓的神经网络发育，揭示生长锥反复分叉是网络节点形成的基本细胞机制。尽管发育起源不同，三个区域的网络均表现出高度保守的动态特征，严格遵循Strahler层级排序和Hack幂律标度。定量拓扑分析显示，随着发育进行，节点数和聚类系数增加而网络密度降低，反映了平衡高功能效率与低布线成本的结构优化策略。这些发现表明物理几何约束结合简单局部规则驱动复杂神经网络的自组织。
英文摘要（原文）：The spatial architecture of neural networks underpins nervous system function, yet dynamic in vivo evidence for their early establishment process remains scarce. Here, using long-term confocal live-cell imaging of transgenic zebrafish, we dissected neural network development across the brain, yolk sac, and spinal cord at single-cell resolution. We reveal that repeated growth cone bifurcation is the fundamental cellular mechanism for network node formation. Despite distinct developmental origins, networks in all three regions exhibit highly conserved dynamics, strictly following Strahler hierarchical ordering and Hack's power-law scaling. Quantitative topological analysis shows that as development progresses, node numbers and clustering coefficients increase, while network density decreases. This reflects a structural optimization strategy balancing high functional efficiency with low wiring cost. These findings suggest that physical and geometric constraints, combined with simple local rules, drive the self-organization of complex neural networks, offering valuable principles for artificial network design.

英文摘要（原文）：In the face of rising global rates of age-related cognitive decline, identifying accessible, non-pharmacological interventions is a critical public health priority. This narrative review synthesizes contemporary evidence to elucidate the molecular and systemic mechanisms by which voluntary exercise enhances hippocampal-dependent memory. We detail how physical activity initiates a coordinated cascade, beginning with the release of systemic factors like FNDC5/irisin, lactate, and IGF-1. These signals converge to robustly upregulate hippocampal brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) and its TrkB receptor, activating a master regulatory network that promotes neuronal survival, synaptogenesis, and adult neurogenesis. Translational human evidence confirms these mechanisms, demonstrating that regular aerobic exercise increases hippocampal volume, strengthens functional connectivity, and elevates serum BDNF, correlating with measurable improvements in episodic and spatial memory across populations from healthy older adults to those with mild cognitive impairment. The review concludes by bridging this mechanistic insight to therapeutic applications, discussing optimal exercise prescriptions, the synergy of exercise with pharmacological and other lifestyle interventions, and the future potential of "exercise mimetics." Ultimately, this synthesis posits voluntary exercise as a potent, plasticity-enhancing therapy whose decoded molecular blueprint provides a scientific foundation for strategies aimed at preserving cognitive resilience throughout the lifespan.

英文摘要（原文）：Electroencephalography (EEG) provides a direct measure of neural activity and offers an objective basis for emotion recognition. Existing graph neural network-based methods, however, often fail to capture both spatial topology and adaptive connectivity of EEG emotion signals, and typically focus on single-scale feature extraction. This study proposes a progressive dual-branch graph convolutional network (PDGCN) for EEG-based emotion recognition. The proposed framework jointly integrates progressive multi-scale temporal feature extraction with dual-branch graph convolution, enabling spatial modeling over temporally contextualized EEG representations. Specifically, PDGCN first extracts progressive multi-scale temporal features hierarchically from local to global scales. It then employs a dual-branch graph convolution strategy to extract and fuse spatial features: one branch encodes the intrinsic spatial topology of electrodes, while the other captures adaptive interactions between EEG channels by a learnable adjacency matrix. Cross-subject experiments on the SEED, SEED-IV, and DEAP datasets show that PDGCN achieves consistently competitive performance compared with representative existing methods. The proposed method offers an effective solution for EEG-based emotion recognition and introduces a new perspective for multi-scale and multi-view brain signal processing. Our codes are publicly available at https://github.com/wuminjin/PDGCN.

This study aimed to investigate the neurodevelopmental harms of fluoride and evaluate the multi-target neuroprotective efficacy of naringin using behavioural, biochemical, antioxidant, proteomic, and gene expression approaches in rats. Naringin attenuated many behavioural, biochemical, and molecular alterations, indicating a protective effect across multiple targets. These findings support further investigation of naringin as a potential protective compound against fluoride-induced neurodevelopmental alterations.

研究结果：分子与表型演化速率在埃迪卡拉纪持续上升，于埃迪卡拉纪晚期至寒武纪最早时期达峰值，此后下降。发育性状在后生动物干系中呈现不成比例的高速率贡献。神经系统演化速率峰值早于底质革命和信息革命假说所预测的时间，显示时间滞后。这些模式在不同时间尺度下基本稳健。
研究意义：该研究支持动物体型构型创新的主要阶段发生在埃迪卡拉纪，为寒武纪大爆发的深时演化背景提供时间框架。然而，研究完全依赖宏观演化速率推断和化石形态学，未涉及基因调控网络、细胞类型或单细胞水平的机制解析，对理解神经和细胞类型演化的具体分子基础贡献有限。
中文全摘要：为检验动物体型构型起源的因果假说，研究分析后鞭毛生物的分子与表型演化速率，聚焦托宁期-奥陶纪分歧事件。分子与表型速率在埃迪卡拉纪持续升高，于埃迪卡拉纪晚期至寒武纪最早时期达到峰值后下降。将表型性状划分为发育、取食、化石保存潜力、运动和神经系统等类别后，发现发育性状在后生动物干系中贡献不成比例。速率模式显示生态变化与表型演化之间存在时间滞后。
英文摘要（原文）：To test causal hypotheses for the emergence of animal body plans, we analyze rates of molecular and phenotypic evolution across Opisthokonta, focusing on divergences within the Tonian-Ordovician interval. Both molecular and phenotypic rates increase through the Ediacaran, peaking in an Ediacaran and earliest Cambrian interval before declining thereafter. Partitioning phenotypic characters into categories relating to development, feeding, fossilization potential, locomotion, and nervous systems reveals distinct temporal patterns, with developmental traits contributing disproportionately to elevated rates in the metazoan stem lineage. Rate patterns show a temporal lag between the ecological changes integral to hypotheses of developmental evolution and the Cambrian agronomic, substrate, and information revolutions and earlier peaks in the evolution of the associated phenotypic characters. These patterns are largely independent of the timescale used. Overall, our results indicate that the primary phase of elevated evolutionary novelty occurred during the Ediacaran and culminated in the late Ediacaran-earliest Cambrian, providing the foundation for later ecological and taxonomic innovation.

AnnelidaDB：环节动物分类学与多组学资源的综合平台
为整合分散的环节动物组学资源，作者构建AnnelidaDB数据库，基于更新系统发育框架整合1121物种的基因组、线粒体基因组、转录组、蛋白质组、表观组、单细胞数据集、共生菌基因组及比较共线性数据，涵盖152个核基因组和5个单细胞数据集共209,802细胞，提供10个导航目录和交互式单细胞图谱浏览、BLAST查询、Oxford图探索等功能，为环节动物基因组演化、适应性、再生及共生研究提供可及的多组学平台。

命中关键词：single-cell atlas, Annelida, Animal evolution, Genome evolution, Cell atlas
推荐理由：该数据库整合环节动物多组学资源，包含5个物种的单细胞数据集（209,802细胞），可作为研究者进行环节动物神经系统单细胞比较演化的数据基础设施，但本身为数据库工具论文，无直接演化分析。
背景：环节动物广泛分布于海洋、淡水和陆地生态系统，是研究动物演化、再生和宿主-微生物互作的重要模式生物。近年来环节动物的组学资源快速增长，但这些资源分散于不同数据库和出版物中，缺乏系统整合。构建专门的数据库平台对于促进环节动物比较基因组学和功能演化研究具有重要意义。
研究方法：作者基于更新的系统发育框架构建物种为中心的数据库，整合基因组、线粒体基因组、转录组、蛋白质组、表观组、单细胞数据集、宿主相关共生菌基因组及比较共线性数据。数据库通过10个导航目录连接物种、基因组、基因、功能注释和基因家族记录，提供交互式单细胞图谱浏览、12,200个预计算Oxford图、BLAST查询及染色体水平共线性分析功能。
研究结果：当前版本覆盖1121物种，包括152个核基因组、177个线粒体基因组、591个转录组、5个单细胞数据集共209,802细胞，以及30个细菌共生基因组。数据库提供序列、注释和私有结果下载功能，支持交互式单细胞图谱探索、基因搜索和共线性分析。
研究意义：作为专门面向环节动物的综合多组学资源，AnnelidaDB为环节动物基因组演化、适应性、再生和共生研究提供了可及的平台。该数据库特别对开展环节动物神经系统的单细胞比较演化研究具有数据基础设施价值，但本身不直接提供演化分析或生物学发现。
中文全摘要：环节动物是海洋、淡水和陆地生态系统的重要组成部分，是研究动物演化、再生和宿主-微生物互作的重要模式生物，但其快速增长的组学资源分散于各数据库和文献中。作者开发AnnelidaDB数据库，基于更新系统发育框架整合基因组、线粒体基因组、转录组、蛋白质组、表观组、单细胞数据集、共生菌基因组及比较共线性数据，当前版本覆盖1121物种，包括152个核基因组、177个线粒体基因组、591个转录组、5个单细胞数据集（209,802细胞）及30个细菌共生基因组，通过10个导航目录组织资源并提供交互式单细胞图谱、Oxford图、BLAST查询及共线性分析工具。
英文摘要（原文）：Annelids inhabit marine, freshwater, and terrestrial ecosystems and are important models for studying animal evolution, regeneration, and host-microbe interactions, yet their rapidly growing omics resources remain dispersed across repositories and publications. We developed AnnelidaDB (www.annelidadb.com), a species-centred database built on an updated phylogenetic framework that integrates genomes, mitochondrial genomes, transcriptomes, proteomes, epigenomes, single-cell datasets, host-associated symbiont genomes, and comparative synteny for Annelida. The current release covers 1121 species, including 152 species with nuclear genomes, 177 with mitochondrial genomes, 591 with transcriptome data, 5 with single-cell datasets comprising 209 802 cells, and 30 bacterial symbiont genomes. AnnelidaDB organizes these resources through 10 navigation directories and connects species, genome, gene, functional annotation, and gene-family records. Users can browse genomic assemblies, explore interactive single-cell atlases and 12 200 precomputed Oxford plots, run BLAST queries and search genes against the annelid-specific database, and submit chromosome-level data for syntenic analysis against 20 Annelida linkage groups. Sequences, annotations, and private results are available for download. As a comprehensive multi-omics resource dedicated to annelids, AnnelidaDB provides an accessible platform for comparative investigations of annelid genome evolution, adaptation, regeneration, and symbiosis.
