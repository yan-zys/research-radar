Birth position and tissue context influence bipolar cell translocation mode in the developing retina.
出生位置和组织环境影响发育中视网膜双极细胞的迁移模式
为探究神经元迁移策略如何适应动态发育组织变化，作者以斑马鱼视网膜双极细胞为模型，利用活细胞成像和组织操作实验，发现早期出生的双极细胞依赖微管介导的定向迁移，而晚期出生的双极细胞则更多依赖集体组织运动的被动位移，揭示了组织层化状态对细胞运动模式的调控作用，为理解神经组织发育中的细胞定位机制提供了新视角。

作者：Cruz MR, Paixão T, Coelho J, Norden C
DOI: 10.1242/dev.206189 · PubMed 链接: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43036847/
命中关键词：neuron, neural, zebrafish, Tissue architecture
推荐理由：该论文聚焦脊椎动物视网膜发育中的细胞迁移机制，缺乏演化视角、跨物种比较及单细胞组学内容，与该研究者核心关注的大脑演化、细胞类型同源性及跨物种比较研究无关。
背景：神经元迁移是中枢神经系统发育中建立功能性组织结构的关键过程。尽管细胞内在迁移机制已有较多研究，但发育中的组织环境如何动态调节这些迁移策略仍是未解之谜。视网膜作为结构高度有序的神经网络，为研究这一问题提供了理想模型。
研究方法：研究者以斑马鱼为模型，利用视网膜双极细胞在不同发育阶段（未层化期和层化期）产生的特点，采用活细胞延时成像技术追踪细胞迁移动态；通过药物干扰或遗传操作破坏微管功能、组织整体运动或光感受器层形成，以解析不同迁移模式的分子和力学基础。
研究结果：研究发现双极细胞迁移模式随组织层化状态而切换：早期出生的细胞采用微管依赖的定向胞体迁移；晚期出生的细胞则主要依赖集体组织运动的被动位移，定向性较低。干扰组织整体运动会削弱晚期细胞的位移，而破坏光感受器层形成则使晚期细胞转向定向迁移模式。无论采用何种策略，细胞均能精确到达目标层。
研究意义：该研究揭示了组织环境背景对神经元迁移模式的决定性影响，提出了一种发育可塑性的细胞运动调控原则。这一发现可能适用于其他神经组织及更广泛发育系统中的细胞定位过程，为理解组织形态发生机制提供了重要参考。
中文全摘要：神经元迁移对中枢神经系统功能性组织结构的建立至关重要。虽然驱动迁移的细胞内在机制已被部分揭示，但这些策略如何适应动态发育组织变化尚不清楚。本研究以斑马鱼视网膜双极细胞为模型，发现细胞运动模式随组织层化状态而变化：早期出生的双极细胞进行微管依赖的定向胞体迁移，而晚期出生的细胞则表现为被动、低定向性的集体组织运动驱动位移。干扰组织整体运动可减少后者位移，而破坏光感受器层形成则使晚期细胞也采用定向迁移。两种策略均能精确实现层状定位。
英文摘要（原文）：Neuronal translocation is essential for establishing functional tissue architecture in the developing central nervous system. While many of the cell-intrinsic mechanisms driving translocation have been identified, how these strategies adapt to dynamic developmental tissue changes remains less understood. Here, we address this question using zebrafish retinal bipolar cells as a model, as they are generated across unlaminated and laminated stages, which enables direct comparison of neuronal translocation modes and dynamics across development. We show that the mode of movement of bipolar cells changes depending on tissue lamination state. Bipolar cells born before photoreceptor layer formation undergo directed, microtubule-dependent somal translocation, as seen for other early born neurons. In contrast, later-born bipolar cells exhibit more passive and less directed displacement that seems to be mostly driven by collective tissue movements. Interference with tissue-wide movements reduces this displacement, while disrupting photoreceptor layer formation also leads to directed translocation at later developmental stages. Independent of strategy, cells achieve accurate laminar positioning. Together, this shows that tissue context can influence cell movement modes, a principle likely relevant across developing neural and other tissues.

Beyond humans, high levels of innovation have evolved independently across several animal lineages, including birds. Birds provide a particularly striking example, with highly innovative species distributed across multiple clades, creating an opportunity to investigate whether independent origins of this phenotype involve recurring evolutionary mechanisms. To investigate the genomic basis of avian innovativeness, we analyzed high-quality long-read genomes from a broad sampling of avian species, leveraging recent genomic resources from the Vertebrate Genomes Project. 英文摘要（原文）：Artificial intelligence (AI) compute demands outstrip electronic hardware scaling, creating a critical "AI compute gap." Photonic computing promises speed, low latency, and parallelism yet faces bottlenecks in core array scalability and optoelectronic bandwidth efficiency. We propose a time-domain parallelism (TDP) computing architecture that temporally multiplexes compact cores, enabling large-scale convolutional operations while optimizing bandwidth efficiency. Supporting this framework, we develop indium tin oxide (ITO)-based photonic devices demonstrating a record 9-bit dynamic reconfigurability at 60 kilohertz and a 3-decibel bandwidth of 311 kilohertz. Notably, the system maintains this high-fidelity performance while operating at 1694 frames per second. This work establishes a scalable pathway toward energy-efficient photonic computing for AI workloads.

推荐理由：该论文提出整合谱系追踪与多组学推断祖先GRN的方法框架，虽含斑马鱼应用但无跨物种演化比较，与研究者核心演化视角匹配度有限。
背景：理解不同细胞身份如何从共同祖细胞分化而来是发育生物学的核心问题。传统单细胞组学仅提供分子快照，无法直接追溯细胞命运决定的历史轨迹。基因调控网络（GRN）的重建需要整合时间维度、谱系信息和多模态数据，但现有方法在推断未观察祖先状态方面存在局限。
研究方法：作者开发RegArch概率计算框架，整合单细胞转录组、染色质可及性数据（ATAC-seq等）和时序细胞谱系追踪数据。采用概率图模型推断终端及祖先节点的GRN拓扑结构，结合调控因子表达预测靶基因表达特征，并通过谱系约束优化网络推断。应用于秀丽隐杆线虫EMS细胞谱系和斑马鱼心脏损伤再生模型进行验证。
研究结果：RegArch在模拟数据中成功恢复祖先网络并改善终端GRN推断准确性。在线虫EMS谱系中，推断的祖先GRN与实测祖细胞谱系特征匹配，鉴定出共享调控模块以及具有不同祖先表征的分支富集模块。在斑马鱼心脏损伤模型中，发育谱系信息辅助优先排序内皮相关和心外膜相关的候选调控因子。
研究意义：RegArch为从单细胞谱系和多组学数据重建细胞命运决定的调控历史提供了通用计算框架，理论上可扩展应用于跨物种祖先细胞类型调控网络的比较推断。但当前研究仅聚焦于单一物种内的发育谱系分析，未涉及研究者核心关注的跨物种细胞类型同源性或神经系统演化问题。
中文全摘要：传统分子快照无法直接揭示细胞身份从共同祖细胞分化出的调控历史。作者提出RegArch概率框架，整合转录组、染色质证据和时序细胞谱系以重建终端及未观察祖先的基因调控网络，可表征调控重编程、优先排序分化相关调控因子、识别组成型与谱系/状态限制模块。模拟验证祖先网络恢复能力；线虫EMS谱系分析发现共享模块和分支富集模块；斑马鱼心脏损伤后发育谱系分析辅助内皮和心外膜候选调控因子排序。
英文摘要（原文）：Understanding how distinct cell identities emerge from common progenitors requires access to regulatory histories that molecular snapshots cannot directly reveal. We introduce RegArch, a probabilistic framework integrating transcriptomic profiles, chromatin-derived evidence, and time-scaled cell lineages to reconstruct terminal and unobserved ancestral gene regulatory networks (GRNs). These lineage-resolved networks enable characterization of regulatory reorganization, prioritization of differentiation-associated and branch-biased regulators, and identification of constitutive, lineage-associated, and state-restricted modules. Simulations demonstrate ancestral network recovery and improved terminal GRN inference. In Caenorhabditis elegans, inferred ancestral GRNs combined with measured regulator expression generated target-expression signatures preferentially matching their corresponding progenitor profiles. EMS lineage analyses identified a shared regulatory module and branch-enriched modules with distinct ancestral representation. In zebrafish hearts sampled after injury, developmental ancestry informed endocardial- and epicardial-associated candidate regulator prioritization. RegArch thus generates testable hypotheses about the regulatory origins of cellular diversity.
