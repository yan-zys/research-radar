Development of Circadian Circuitry Underlying Feeding-Fasting Rhythms in Drosophila Larvae
果蝇幼虫摄食-禁食节律下昼夜节律回路的发育
为揭示早期发育中摄食-禁食节律形成的神经机制，作者以果蝇三龄幼虫为模型，通过遗传学手段特异性失活s-LNv昼夜节律起搏神经元和SIFa神经元，发现两类神经元之间的突触连接在L3期形成是摄食节律涌现的关键，并证明Pdf和SIFa信号通路对该行为节律的正常发育至关重要，为理解发育中昼夜节律行为的神经回路机制提供了新见解。

命中关键词：neural development, neuron, neural, RNA interference, Drosophila
推荐理由：该论文为果蝇幼虫昼夜节律神经回路的发育机制研究，缺乏演化或跨物种比较视角，且非单细胞图谱类工作。
背景：昼夜节律行为的形成是动物适应环境周期性变化的重要机制，但许多生物在发育早期并不表现出成体样的昼夜行为节律。睡眠和摄食行为的昼夜整合如何随发育逐步建立，其背后的神经回路发育机制尚不清楚。早期生活中昼夜节律的紊乱与神经发育异常和代谢疾病相关，因此理解这一发育过程具有重要的生理和临床意义。
研究方法：研究者以果蝇三龄幼虫为模型，利用遗传学手段（如UAS-GAL4系统）特异性失活或调控s-LNv昼夜节律起搏神经元和pars intercerebralis中的SIFa神经元，通过行为学监测分析摄食-禁食节律和睡眠节律，并采用RNA干扰技术降低Pdf和SIFa信号分子的表达，评估其对行为节律发育的影响。
研究结果：研究发现s-LNv神经元和SIFa神经元的共同失活会破坏幼虫摄食节律的发育，提示两者均为节律形成所必需。行为学和神经解剖学数据表明，s-LNvs与SIFa神经元之间的回路连接在L3阶段形成，是摄食节律涌现的结构基础。此外，通过RNA干扰降低Pdf或SIFa信号水平，可破坏昼夜摄食和睡眠差异，导致行为节律丧失。
研究意义：该研究首次鉴定了发育中生物体摄食节律调控的生物钟及其输出回路，揭示了s-LNv-SIFa神经回路在节律行为发育中的关键作用，为理解昼夜节律回路的功能发育提供了重要框架。然而，该研究局限于单一物种（果蝇）的行为神经机制，未涉及跨物种比较、细胞类型演化或基因调控网络演化等演化生物学问题。
中文全摘要：许多生物在生命早期不表现出强烈的日常行为节律。睡眠和摄食的昼夜节律整合直到发育后期才出现。早期生命中昼夜节律行为的紊乱可导致神经发育和代谢稳态失调，但调控摄食节律发育的回路机制未知。本研究鉴定了果蝇三龄幼虫中驱动摄食-禁食节律发育的神经回路，发现s-LNv昼夜节律起搏神经元和SIFa神经元的失活均破坏摄食节律发育，两类神经元间在L3期形成的回路连接是节律涌现的原因，且Pdf和SIFa信号减少会干扰正常行为节律发育。
英文摘要（原文）：Many organisms do not exhibit strong daily behavioral rhythms early in life. The consolidation of sleep and feeding to specific times of the day does not emerge until later in development. Disruptions to circadian behaviors in early life can lead to dysregulation of both neural development and metabolic homeostasis. However, the circuit mechanisms regulating feeding rhythm development are unknown. Here, we characterize the neural circuitry responsible for driving the development of feeding-fasting rhythms in early 3rd instar (L3) Drosophila larvae. We find that inactivation of both larval s-LNv circadian pacemaker neurons and SIFamide (SIFa) neurons in the pars intercerebralis disrupts feeding rhythm development. Additionally, our data indicate feeding rhythms likely emerge due to circuit connections forming between s-LNvs and SIFa neurons at the L3 stage. Finally, we show that reductions in signaling between s-LNv and SIFa neurons interfere with normal behavioral rhythm development in L3. Specifically, RNA interference-induced reductions in pigment-dispersing factor (pdf) signaling and SIFa signaling disrupt diurnal differences in both feeding and sleep, resulting in a loss of behavioral rhythms. Altogether, our findings identify for the first time the clock and clock output circuitry responsible for feeding rhythm regulation in developing organisms.

推荐理由：该论文仅聚焦于小鼠皮层发育中Fezf2剂量效应的分子机制，属于典型的模式生物发育神经生物学研究，缺乏演化视角、跨物种比较及细胞类型同源性分析，与该研究者核心的脑演化、跨物种比较及单细胞图谱研究高度不符。
背景：皮层-脑干投射神经元是大脑运动指令输出的关键细胞类型，其发育依赖于转录因子Fezf2的精确调控。Fezf2剂量异常与多种神经发育障碍相关，但其细胞类型特异性和时序性的分子机制尚不明确。已有研究构建了小鼠皮层发育的单细胞图谱，为系统解析Fezf2的功能提供了数据基础。
研究方法：作者重新分析了Di Bella等人（2021）公开的20个单细胞RNA-seq样本和3个scATAC-seq样本，涵盖胚胎期E10至出生后P4。采用Harmony和scVI进行批次整合，通过consensus doublet removal去除双细胞，以bootstrap 95%置信区间评估基因型效应，并利用WT重复样本估计生物学噪声零分布。进一步进行regulon活性分析推断转录调控网络变化。
研究结果：研究共获得121,889个高质量细胞，鉴定17种转录 distinct 细胞类型。最显著的基因型效应为P1时皮层下投射神经元比例从WT的2.21%降至杂合子0.18%和敲除型0.15%。在成熟型、皮层-丘脑型和胼胝体投射神经元中分别鉴定出947、1,119和838个差异表达基因。敲除型细胞中翻译和核糖体生物合成相关基因显著下调，regulon分析提示Six4、Atf5、Myt1活性增加而Hdac7、Rest、Kdm5a活性降低。
研究意义：该研究揭示了Fezf2剂量依赖性调控皮层发育的细胞类型特异性机制，为神经发育障碍的分子病理提供了候选靶点。然而，研究完全局限于小鼠单一物种，未涉及跨物种比较、细胞类型同源性推断或演化分析，对理解皮层神经元类型的演化起源缺乏直接贡献。其方法论（单细胞重分析、批次整合）虽具参考性，但不足以弥补研究视角的局限性。
中文全摘要：Fezf2是皮层-脑干投射神经元特化所需的剂量敏感性转录因子，其变异与神经发育障碍相关。本研究重新分析小鼠皮层发育的公开单细胞RNA-seq数据（E10-P4，20个样本），比较野生型、杂合子和敲除型，鉴定121,889个细胞和17种细胞类型，发现Fezf2缺失导致P1时皮层下投射神经元减少约13-15倍，并鉴定出翻译/成熟相关基因的汇聚性下调及Six4、Rest等调控因子变化。
英文摘要（原文）：Background. Fezf2 (Forebrain Embryonic Zinc Finger 2) is a dosage-sensitive transcription factor required for the specification of corticofugal projection neurons, and rare predicted-deleterious Fezf2 variants are associated with human neurodevelopmental disorders. The cell-type-specific and temporal manner in which Fezf2 dosage shapes cortical development remains incompletely resolved at single-cell resolution. This study aims to quantify the temporal and cell-type-specific transcriptional response to progressive Fezf2 loss by re-analyzing a public single-cell atlas of mouse cortical development. Methods. We re-analyzed 20 single-cell RNA-seq samples spanning embryonic day (E) 10 to postnatal day (P) 4 across wild-type (WT), Fezf2-heterozygous (Het), and Fezf2-knockout (KO) genotypes, together with 3 wild-type single-cell ATAC-seq samples, from Di Bella et al. (2021) (GSE153164). After quality control, consensus doublet removal, and batch integration with two complementary methods (Harmony and scVI), we annotated cell types and quantified genotype effects. Because the source design includes a single library per genotype at most stages, we report effect sizes with bootstrap 95% confidence intervals and calibrate candidate dysregulation against a biological-noise null estimated from the two WT replicates at P1, rather than reporting formal inferential p-values. Results. We recovered 121,889 high-quality cells and 20,982 genes, resolving 17 transcriptionally distinct cell types. The most prominent genotype effect was a dosage-dependent depletion of subcerebral projection neurons (SCPNs) at P1 - the only timepoint at which all three genotypes were sampled: SCPNs comprised 2.21% of WT cells (mean of two replicates) but 0.18% of Het and 0.15% of KO cells, an approximately 13 -15-fold reduction. Using the WT-replicate null (99th percentile |log2 fold-change| = 0.34), we identified 947, 1,119, and 838 genes exceeding this threshold in Maturing, Corticothalamic, and Callosal projection neurons, respectively. Genes downregulated in KO projection neurons were convergently enriched for cytoplasmic translation and ribosome biogenesis (adjusted P {approx} 8.8*10^-30; in Corticothalamic Neurons). Regulon-activity analysis nominated Six4, Atf5, and Myt1 as increased and Hdac7, Rest, and Kdm5a as decreased in KO cells. Conclusions. Progressive Fezf2 loss is associated with a dosage-dependent, cell-type-specific disruption of cortical development, most notably near-elimination of the SCPN compartment by birth and a convergent downregulation of translational/maturation-associated genes across projection neurons. These associations nominate candidate downstream programs for experimental follow-up; they do not by themselves establish a mechanism.

Sex peptide-SPR-serotonin signaling regulates mating-induced Sex peptide replenishment in Drosophila, suggesting an ancestral role in males
果蝇交配诱导的性肽补充受性肽-SPR-5-羟色胺信号调控，提示其在雄性中的祖先功能

英文摘要（原文）：Seminal fluid not only assists successful fertilization but also induces changes in female post-mating behavior and physiology. Consequently, males with depleted or inadequate seminal fluid produce fewer offspring. Males of iteroparous species therefore increase seminal fluid production to replenish exhausted supplies. However, it remains unclear how mating-induced replenishment of seminal fluid components is regulated at the molecular and cellular levels. Here, we show that replenishment of Sex peptide (SP) in male Drosophila melanogaster requires both SP and its receptor SPR. Mechanistically, mating-induced SP replenishment in the male accessory gland (MAG) is regulated by MAG-projecting serotonergic neurons, and this regulation depends on SPR, which is likely activated by SP as it passes through the reproductive tract during mating. This inter-tissue signaling pathway is not limited to SP but also coordinates the post-mating upregulation of an additional seminal fluid protein gene. While SP-SPR signaling is a well-known regulator of female post-mating responses, we demonstrate that it also modulates male physiology. Furthermore, comparative analyses across 12 drosophilid species are consistent with the hypothesis that SP-SPR signaling acquired a role in males before its role in canonical female post-mating responses evolved. We therefore propose that the role of SP-SPR signaling as a mating signal in males is ancestral to its more familiar but evolutionarily derived role in females.

中文全摘要：麻醉导致无意识的机制之一，可能是脑区操控彼此活动的能力发生改变。作者基于JacobianODE，开发结合delay-coordinate embedding、体积保持可逆编码器和潜在动力学学习的深度学习框架，从部分观测的多脑区神经记录中恢复有向非线性控制。方法在Lorenz系统和工作记忆循环神经网络上验证后，应用于两只非人灵长类PPC、STG、FEF和vlPFC的局部场电位数据。结果显示，丙泊酚麻醉广泛降低区际驱动与稳定控制能力，主要由区际耦合减弱所致。
英文摘要（原文）：Anesthetic-induced unconsciousness may arise partly from a change in how brain areas can manipulate each other's activity. To quantify this change, we use control-theoretic tools that can precisely characterize how easily subsystems in complex networks can control each other. These tools rely on the Jacobian of the dynamics, an object that fully specifies how inputs to a function affect the outputs. We built on JacobianODE, a method for data-driven Jacobian learning, to enable its application to neural recordings. We developed a deep learning framework that recovers directional, nonlinear control from partially observed multi-area recordings by combining delay-coordinate embedding, a volume-preserving invertible encoder, and latent dynamics via JacobianODE. We validated our framework on the Lorenz system and a partially observed working-memory recurrent neural network. We then applied it to local field potential recordings from posterior parietal (PPC), superior temporal gyrus (STG), frontal eye fields (FEF), and ventrolateral prefrontal cortex (vlPFC) in two non-human primates, comparing wakefulness with propofol anesthesia. Anesthesia pervasively reduced the ability of areas to control each other, both in terms of driving towards novel states and stabilizing along existing trajectories. This change in ease of control was driven by a decrease in the magnitude of interareal coupling. Directional ease of driving control from PPC to vlPFC and FEF to vlPFC was increased under anesthesia, providing a potential mechanism for paradoxical excitation observed during propofol infusion. Together, these results recast anesthetic unconsciousness as a directional breakdown of cortical control.
