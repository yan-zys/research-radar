Whole-brain mapping of spinal-projecting neurons in larval zebrafish.
幼体斑马鱼全脑脊髓投射神经元图谱绘制
为揭示脊髓投射神经元（SPNs）的组织原则，作者利用光敏GFP光学逆行标记技术在透明幼体斑马鱼中全脑绘制了投射至脊髓的神经元图谱，发现了远多于以往报道的SPNs，并基于哺乳动物等脊椎动物的描述对斑马鱼SPN核团进行了同源性推测，为理解脊椎动物下行运动通路的组织结构提供了参考。

作者：Carbo-Tano M, Fidelin K, Welch T, Narayan S, Ahrens MB, Dubuc R, Wyart C
DOI: 10.7554/eLife.112062 · PubMed 链接: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42955830/
命中关键词：brain, neuron, zebrafish
推荐理由：该论文为脊椎动物（斑马鱼）特异性的神经解剖学研究，缺乏单细胞转录组技术、无跨物种比较或演化视角，不符合研究者核心兴趣。
背景：脊髓投射神经元（SPNs）是脑向脊髓发送指令信号的关键长程下行神经元，对自主运动和整合性反射至关重要。由于其广泛分布和复杂的长距离连接，SPNs的解剖学组织及其与特定功能关联的映射一直是神经科学中的挑战。斑马鱼幼体因其光学透明性和遗传可操作性，成为研究SPN组织的理想模型。
研究方法：研究者利用基于光敏GFP的光学逆行标记技术，在幼体斑马鱼中实现全脑SPNs的标记与成像；结合细胞位置、形态学和轴突投射模式的多模态信息，对SPN核团进行分类和定位，并与哺乳动物及其他脊椎动物的已知SPN核团进行同源性比较。
研究结果：该研究揭示了远多于以往常规策略所描述的SPNs，涵盖了分布于后脑、中脑和间脑的大量脊髓投射核团；基于解剖位置、细胞形态和投射模式的综合信息，作者对斑马鱼SPN核团提出了与哺乳动物及其他脊椎动物潜在同源的命名建议，建立了详细的SPN全脑图谱。
研究意义：该研究为理解脊椎动物下行运动通路的组织原则提供了高分辨率的解剖学参考，但其意义主要局限于单一物种（斑马鱼）的神经解剖学描述，未涉及跨物种比较的系统演化分析，也未采用单细胞转录组学等该研究者关注的技术手段。对于研究神经回路发育和功能具有价值，但对神经演化和细胞类型同源性的直接贡献有限。
中文全摘要：为绘制脊髓投射神经元（SPN）的全脑解剖图谱，作者利用幼体斑马鱼的透明性和遗传可操作性，采用基于光敏GFP的光学逆行标记方法，揭示了比以往策略更多的SPNs。该图谱涵盖了分布于后脑、中脑和间脑的脊髓投射核团，作者结合细胞位置、形态和投射模式信息，对斑马鱼SPN核团进行了基于哺乳动物及其他脊椎动物描述的同源性推测。
英文摘要（原文）：To elicit voluntary movements and integrative reflexes underlying behavior, the brain sends command signals to the spinal cord via specialized long-range descending neurons, known as spinal-projecting neurons (SPNs). The vast and widespread distribution of SPNs, combined with their complex long-distance connectivity, poses a significant challenge for mapping their anatomical organization and associating specific populations with distinct functions. Here, we took advantage of the transparency and genetic accessibility of larval zebrafish to uncover the fundamental principles of SPN anatomical organization in a teleost. Using an optical backfilling method relying on photoactivable GFP, we generate a whole-brain map of all neurons sending axons toward the spinal cord. This approach reveals far more SPNs than previously described through conventional strategies, offering an unparalleled opportunity to revisit distinct spinal-projecting nuclei distributed across the hindbrain, midbrain, and diencephalic structures. Combining information on cell location, morphology, and projection patterns, we propose tentative homological designations for zebrafish of SPN nuclei based on established descriptions in mammals and other vertebrates.

背景：人类新皮层的独特之处在于其延长的出生后发育窗口，在此期间感觉经验活动依赖性地塑造神经环路，这一过程对人类高级认知能力至关重要。然而，人类特异性遗传创新是否参与调控了这一活动依赖性的环路组装过程，此前尚不清楚。鉴定这类基因并解析其分子机制，有助于理解人类大脑可塑性的遗传基础。
研究方法：研究者通过基因共线性分析和系统发育分析鉴定FRMPD2B的人类特异性起源；利用原位杂交、单细胞RNA测序和报告基因实验分析其表达模式；通过体外神经元培养、电生理记录和异种移植（xenotransplantation）实验评估其功能；采用染色质构象捕获和启动子分析解析其调控机制。
研究结果：FRMPD2B通过部分基因重复起源，其启动子因逆转录转座子插入而获得活动响应性；该基因主要在出生后及儿童期的皮层-皮层投射神经元中表达，促进树突、轴突和突触发育；FRMPD2B敲低导致异种移植人皮层神经元形态发生缺陷；其表达受神经活动和经验双向调控，形成人类特有的活动-经验-连接扩张耦合机制。
研究意义：该研究揭示了人类特异性基因如何通过调控序列创新获得新的表达特性，进而参与塑造人类大脑特有的发育可塑性程序。然而，该工作完全局限于人类-小鼠比较，缺乏更广泛的跨物种演化分析，无法直接为两侧动物脑起源与演化、细胞类型同源或祖先状态重建等核心演化问题提供证据。
中文全摘要：人类新皮层以独特的延长出生后发育期为特征，期间经验塑造神经环路，但人类特异性遗传创新是否塑造了活动依赖性环路组装机制仍不清楚。作者鉴定了人类特异性基因FRMPD2B，其表达受神经活动和经验双向调控，通过部分基因重复和逆转录转座子插入重塑启动子而获得此特性；该基因促进树突、轴突和突触发育，异种移植人皮层神经元中其缺失导致形态发生异常。
英文摘要（原文）：The human neocortex is characterized by its uniquely prolonged postnatal developmental period during which experience sculpts neuronal circuits, a process essential to human cognitive abilities. Yet whether human-specific genetic innovations have shaped the activity-dependent mechanisms regulating circuit assembly remains unknown. Here, we identify the human-specific gene FRMPD2B as an activity- and experience-regulated modifier of cortical connectivity. FRMPD2B is primarily expressed in cortico-cortical projection neurons postnatally and throughout childhood. It promotes dendritic, axonal and synaptic development, and is required for proper morphogenesis in xenotransplanted human cortical neurons. FRMPD2B expression is bidirectionally regulated by neuronal activity and experience. 英文摘要（原文）：Sleep disturbances compromise cognition, and overall physical and mental health; however, animal models of these effects remain limited, and typically impose more extreme disturbances than those experienced by humans. Here, we use behavioural interventions in mice to model two common types of sleep disruption: (1) sleep displacement associated with shiftwork and (2) sleep fragmentation associated with aging and disease. C57BL6/J mice were subjected to either a sleep deprivation protocol (n = 8, females = 4) or a fragmentation protocol (n = 8, females = 4), in which they were completely deprived of sleep or awoken every 2 min respectively, for 12 hours during their typical rest phase (lights-on) across 7 consecutive days. Age-matched control mice (n = 14, females = 7) remained undisturbed in their home cages. Activity during the 12-hour active phase was recorded via infrared motion sensors. After protocol completion, mice were injected with bromodeoxyuridine (BrdU, 200mg/kg) 2 hours prior to brain and blood collection; hippocampal neurogenesis and plasma corticosterone were then assessed. These protocols provide a foundation for future research into the mechanistic links between shiftwork and sleep fragmentation on health and cognition.

研究方法：研究者采用单细胞RNA测序（scRNA-seq）对发育中的小鼠纹状体进行细胞类型鉴定和发育轨迹分析；利用Foxg1条件性基因敲除小鼠模型（包括Nestin-Cre介导的胚胎期前体细胞敲除和Adora2a-Cre介导的出生后SPN特异性敲除）进行功能缺失研究；通过差异基因表达分析和基因本体富集分析鉴定FOXG1下游转录程序；结合染色质免疫共沉淀和靶基因预测分析鉴定FOXG1直接调控的下游基因。
研究结果：研究发现eSPN与iSPN在发育过程中表现出更高的转录组相似性。Foxg1在侧神经节隆起腹区前体细胞中的表达促进dSPN和iSPN命运，同时抑制eSPN命运；Nestin-Cre介导的Foxg1缺失导致dSPN和iSPN均向eSPN命运转换。出生后Foxg1的Adora2a-Cre条件性敲除破坏SPN的patch/matrix区室化身份，导致神经元异常聚集。研究进一步鉴定了FOXG1驱动的、分别特异性于各SPN亚型和patch/matrix身份的转录程序，并发现FOXG1直接调控细胞迁移和细胞粘附相关分子（如Semaphorin和Plexin家族成员）以控制SPN的精确迁移和定位。
研究意义：该研究揭示了FOXG1作为纹状体SPN亚型命运特化和空间组织的关键调控枢纽，建立了从转录因子调控到细胞迁移行为的分子联系，为理解纹状体发育的精细调控机制提供了新见解。然而，该研究完全集中于小鼠单一模式生物，未涉及跨物种比较或演化分析，对于理解纹状体神经元亚型的演化起源和保守性缺乏直接贡献。

英文摘要（原文）：The striatum consists of three main spiny projection neuron subtypes, direct-pathway neurons (dSPNs), indirect-pathway neurons (iSPNs), and recently identified eccentric striatal neurons (eSPNs) with these SPNs organized into two complementary compartments, the patch and the matrix. However, the cellular basis underlying subtype specification especially that of eSPNs, and mechanisms governing the complementary patterning remain unclear. Here, employing single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) combined with loss-of-function we reveal that eSPNs share more developmental similarities with iSPNs and the transcription factor Foxg1 promotes dSPN and iSPN fates but suppresses eSPN fate in ventral region of the lateral ganglionic eminence lineage. We identify FOXG1-driven transcriptional programs and key transcription factors that specify each striatal SPN subtype and patch/matrix identity, and uncover FOXG1 directly regulated cell migration- and cell adhesion-related molecules that control SPN migration. Our findings provide insights into striatal development.

However, the inflammatory microenvironment that develops after injury poses a significant barrier to effective neural repair. Addressing this challenge requires the development of bioactive scaffolds with potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, as well as reduced implantation-induced damage. In this study, we created a novel composite biomaterial scaffold with high mechanical strength and excellent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant capabilities by dispersing in situ self-assembled tea polyphenol-magnesium nanoparticles (TPs-Mg NPs) into gelatin methacryloyl hydrogel (GelMA) microneedle patches (TPs-Mg MN). 英文摘要（原文）：Resting-state brain signal variability has been found to vary with age and cognitive function. Neural flexibility has been suggested as a neural mechanism underlying cognitive reserve (CR), a construct that describes better than expected cognition given brain status. We examined the associations between age, resting-state brain signal variability, and CR. Analysis was based on resting-state functional neuroimaging data from 470 participants (ages 20-80) from the Reference Ability Neural Network and the CR studies. Brain signal variability was quantified as the log-transformed standard deviation of the time-varying blood-oxygen-level-dependent signal per region. We then derived variability pattern scores related to age, perceptual speed, episodic memory, fluid reasoning, and vocabulary using Scaled Subprofile Modelling principal component analysis.
