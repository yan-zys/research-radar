The role of transposable elements in primate neurodevelopment and human brain evolution.

为揭示人类大脑演化的遗传基础，作者综述了灵长类特异性转座元件作为调控元件在神经发育中的作用机制，重点分析了TE衍生序列与KRAB-ZFP蛋白互作如何塑造谱系特异性调控网络，讨论了重复序列比对、位点特异性扰动等技术挑战，为理解TE在人类大脑演化中的功能提供了理论框架。

推荐理由：该研究仅聚焦于灵长类/人类特有特征，缺乏跨物种比较视角，与研究者核心的双侧动物大脑演化及细胞类型同源性研究目标不符。
背景：人类大脑演化的遗传机制仍是未解之谜。转座元件作为基因组的重要组成部分，其在调控创新中的作用逐渐受到关注。灵长类特异性TE如何参与神经发育调控，以及它们与宿主因子的互作机制，是理解人类大脑特化的重要切入点。
研究方法：本文采用综述形式，整合比较基因组学、单细胞多组学和灵长类干细胞模型等研究证据，分析TE衍生序列的调控功能及KRAB-ZFP蛋白与TE的相互作用，并讨论重复序列感知比对、位点特异性扰动等技术方法的现状与挑战。
研究结果：灵长类特异性TE在神经发育中充当调控元件；TE与KRAB-ZFP的互作塑造神经发育过程；TE景观与宿主抑制因子共同构建谱系和物种特异性调控网络；当前技术仍存在重复序列比对、位点特异性扰动和灵长类基因组资源不完整等瓶颈。
研究意义：该综述为理解TE在人类大脑演化中的贡献提供了理论框架，强调了TE作为调控创新来源的重要性。然而，其研究视角局限于灵长类/人类特有特征，缺乏跨物种比较和演化分析，对探索双侧动物大脑起源及细胞类型同源性等核心问题的直接参考价值有限。
中文全摘要：人类大脑演化的遗传基础尚不明确。转座元件占人类基因组一半，正成为调控创新的重要来源。比较基因组学、单细胞多组学和灵长类干细胞模型显示，进化上年轻的灵长类特异性TE在脑发育中发挥调控元件功能。TE衍生序列与KRAB-ZFP蛋白的相互作用塑造神经发育，支持了进化中的TE景观与宿主抑制因子共同构建灵长类脑内谱系和物种特异性调控网络的模型。
英文摘要（原文）：The genetic basis of human brain evolution remains unclear. Transposable elements (TEs), which comprise half the human genome, are emerging as an important source of regulatory innovation. Comparative genomics, single-cell multi-omics, and primate stem cell models show that evolutionarily young primate-specific TEs serve as regulatory elements during brain development. Here we review evidence that TE-derived sequences and Krüppel-associated box zinc finger protein-TE interactions shape neurodevelopment, supporting a model in which evolving TE landscapes and host repressors build lineage- and species-specific regulatory networks in the primate brain. We also discuss technical barriers to causal inference, including repeat-aware mapping, locus-specific perturbation, and incomplete primate genomic resources. Together, these advances provide a framework for determining whether and how TEs contribute to primate neurodevelopment and the roles these elements played in human brain evolution.

中文全摘要：基底放射状胶质细胞（bRG）是灵长类新皮层扩张的关键神经前体细胞，但其在其他脑区和物种中的分布尚不清楚。研究发现，发育中的小鼠海马齿状回（DG）存在大量在转录组和形态上类似人新皮层bRG的神经前体细胞，且该现象在Boreoeutheria（小鼠、狨猴、雪貂、猪）中保守。比较转录组学揭示了小鼠DG与人新皮层bRG间的共享形态调控基因特征，免疫组化显示DG bRG形态类型多样性跨物种存在，且较新皮层更为多样。祖先状态重建表明该多样性在Boreoeutheria共同祖先即已存在。
英文摘要（原文）：Basal radial glia (bRG) are neural progenitor cells (NPCs) essential for primate neocortical expansion, but their presence in other brain regions and across species remains poorly understood. Here, we show that the developing mouse dentate gyrus (DG), unlike the neocortex, harbors abundant NPCs that transcriptionally and morphologically resemble human neocortical bRG. This striking abundance is conserved across other Boreoeutherian species, including mice, marmosets, ferrets, and pigs. Comparative transcriptomics uncovered a shared morphoregulatory gene signature between bRG in mouse DG and human neocortex. Immunohistochemical analysis showed diversity in DG bRG morphotypes across species. Remarkably, compared to the neocortex, DG bRG consistently exhibit greater morphotype diversity. Ancestral state reconstruction suggests that this diversity was already present in the common ancestor of Boreoeutherians. Together, our findings position the DG as a conserved, evolutionarily ancient niche for bRG, revealing that region- and species-specific adaptations of NPCs extend beyond the neocortex.

英文摘要（原文）：Animals rely on light not only for vision but also to adapt behavior to their environment through non-image-forming pathways. In Drosophila, most rhodopsins mediate retinal image formation, but Rhodopsin 7 (RH7) is widely expressed in the brain and optic lobe, acting as a non-canonical light sensor reminiscent of mammalian melanopsin. We combined expression mapping, behavioral assays, and phylogenetic comparisons to investigate its function. RH7 detects temporal changes in luminance, guiding rapid behavioral responses, and regulating daily activity patterns. Flies lacking RH7 are less active during the dark phase, while strains with constitutively active RH7 show increased activity. 推荐理由：虽有跨物种（小鼠和人）单细胞/空间组学技术，但核心聚焦于脊髓衰老与细胞衰老机制，无演化或跨物种比较视角，不涉及脑进化、神经系统起源、细胞类型同源等该研究者核心议题。
背景：脊髓是连接外周与脑的关键中继结构，负责感觉、运动和自主功能。随着年龄增长，脊髓功能逐渐衰退，但其细胞类型特异性的分子衰老机制，特别是细胞衰老（cellular senescence）在其中的作用，尚缺乏系统性的空间解析。
研究方法：作者采用液相色谱-串联质谱（LC-MS/MS）进行蛋白质组学分析，利用10x Genomics Visium技术进行空间转录组学分析，通过单核RNA测序（snRNA-seq）获取细胞类型分辨率的转录组数据，并结合多色免疫荧光成像进行蛋白验证。
研究结果：研究发现小鼠和人脊髓衰老过程中存在收敛的蛋白质组变化，包括纤维蛋白原、层粘连蛋白和免疫激活相关蛋白的升高。时空分析揭示小鼠脊髓衰老的异质性模式：突触信号、线粒体组织和自噬等程序在灰质和神经元中随年龄下调，而血管生成和免疫反应在白质、内皮细胞和小胶质细胞中上调。这些上调的空间模块富集衰老相关基因。人类脊髓中，内皮细胞和小胶质细胞分别特异性富集经典衰老标志物CDKN1A和CDKN2A。
研究意义：该研究首次以单细胞和空间分辨率系统刻画了脊髓衰老的细胞类型特异性分子程序，揭示了衰老与细胞衰老在空间分布上的关联，为理解中枢神经系统衰老的异质性提供了新的维度。然而，该工作未涉及演化比较或神经系统起源等议题。
中文全摘要：脊髓是外周与脑之间信息传递的主要中继站。尽管其功能重要，脊髓衰老的细胞分子变化及其与细胞衰老的关系仍不清楚。本研究整合液相色谱-串联质谱、Visium空间转录组、单核RNA测序和多色免疫荧光，构建了小鼠和人脊髓衰老的跨物种多组学图谱。发现小鼠和人脊髓中纤维蛋白原、层粘连蛋白和免疫活化蛋白随年龄增加而升高。小鼠中，突触信号、线粒体组织和自噬等程序在灰质和神经元中下调，血管和免疫过程在白质、内皮细胞和小胶质细胞中上调，并与衰老相关基因富集。人脊髓中，内皮细胞和小胶质细胞分别富集衰老标志物CDKN1A和CDKN2A。
英文摘要（原文）：The spinal cord is the primary relay center between the periphery and the brain, playing a critical role in sensory, motor and autonomic function. Despite its functional importance, the cellular and molecular changes underlying spinal cord aging and their relationship to cellular senescence - a hallmark associated with aging - remain incompletely understood. Here, we generated a cross-species, multi-omic atlas of spinal cord aging in mice and humans by integrating a combination of liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry, Visium spatial transcriptomics, single-nucleus RNA-sequencing, and multiplexed immunofluorescence imaging. We identified converging age-associated increases in fibrinogen, laminin, and immune activation-associated proteins across the mouse and human spinal cord. Over the mouse lifespan, we further identified distinct spatiotemporal patterns of aging. Downregulated transcriptional programs, including synaptic signaling, mitochondrion organization, and autophagy, were most highly expressed in grey matter regions and in neurons. Upregulated transcriptional programs, including vascular and immune processes, were enriched in white matter regions and in endothelial cells and microglia, respectively. Notably, these increasing spatial modules were also enriched for senescence-associated genes. In the human spinal cord, endothelial cells and microglia were also most enriched for canonical senescence markers CDKN1A and CDKN2A, respectively. Overall, this study defines the shared, spatially-resolved, cell type-specific features associated with aging and senescence in the mouse and human spinal cord.

中文全摘要：人格特质与DNA关联的稳健性、泛化性及作用路径仍不清楚。作者汇总46个队列、61.1万至114万名欧洲样和非洲样基因组参与者，开展五大人格GWAS，并结合最多50,725人的家系内GWAS。结果鉴定1260个主导变异，其中824个为新发现；常见变异可解释4.8%-9.3%的表型方差。遗传关联在地域、报告者、年龄和量表间总体一致，且较少受共享家庭环境混杂，并与多种行为和生活结局相关。
英文摘要（原文）：Personality traits describe stable differences in how people think, feel and behave, and how they interact with and experience their social and physical environments1,2. Many questions remain unanswered about associations between DNA and personality traits, such as their robustness, their generalizability and the biological and social pathways through which they act. Here we meta-analyse data across 46 cohorts comprising 611,037 to 1.14 million participants with European-like and African-like genomes for genome-wide association studies (GWAS) of the Big Five personality traits (extraversion, agreeableness, conscientiousness, neuroticism and openness to experience), and data from up to 50,725 participants for within-family GWAS. We identify 1,260 lead genetic variants associated with personality, including 824 novel variants3. Common genetic variants explain a moderate 4.8-9.3% of the variance in measures of each trait, and 9.3-13.3% among instruments with typical measurement reliability. Genetic associations with personality are highly consistent but not identical across geography, reporter (self versus close other), age group and measurement instrument, and we find minimal spousal assortment for personality in recent history. In contrast to many other social and behavioural traits4,5, within-family GWAS and polygenic index analyses indicate that genetic associations with personality are minimally confounded by the shared family environment. Polygenic prediction, genetic correlation and Mendelian randomization analyses indicate that personality traits have widespread, potentially causal associations with consequential behaviours and life outcomes. Overall, we find that the genetic architecture of personality is robustly generalizable, minimally confounded and widely relevant to human experience.

英文摘要（原文）：Ambiviruses are fungal-infecting circular RNA viruses that uniquely combine viroid-like and viral features, yet the function of their conserved ORF-B protein and their effects on hosts remain unknown, hindered by the lack of a reverse genetics system. Here, we constructed the first infectious cDNA clone of an ambivirus, Fusarium graminearum ambivirus 1 (FgAV1), using a head-to-tail dimer placed downstream of a fungal promoter. FgAV1 was horizontally transmitted via hyphal anastomosis to virus-free Fusarium graminearum strains. Notably, a reverse-oriented dimer construct was also infectious and transmissible. Targeted mutagenesis revealed that both ORF-A- and ORF-B-encoded proteins and the presence of embedded ribozymes are indispensable for ambivirus replication. Our results further demonstrate that FgAV1 infection triggers a fungal RNAi response, extending the antiviral role of host sRNAs to circular RNA viruses. Furthermore, FgAV1 infection suppressed fungal growth and significantly reduced the virulence of F. graminearum on wheat. These findings provide novel insights into ambivirus replication and their potential in fungal pathogen biocontrol.

中文全摘要：CaMKII是突触棘中高度富集、参与突触可塑性的关键激酶，但其更高阶组织机制尚不明确。作者利用高速原子力显微镜，在5至500纳米介观尺度可视化CaMKIIα全酶间相互作用。结果显示，在可自由扩散条件下其不形成稳定簇；而在空间受限时，可通过激酶结构域相互作用组装为链状簇，并在激活及调控区段解离后进一步扩展。与神经发育障碍相关的P212L突变体在基础状态下也形成广泛簇，提示簇大小调控对突触信号至关重要。
英文摘要（原文）：Calcium- and calmodulin-dependent protein kinase II (CaMKII) is highly enriched in dendritic spines at concentrations comparable to those of cytoskeletal proteins and plays a central role in synaptic plasticity. During long-term potentiation, CaMKIIα further accumulates in spines. However, the mechanisms governing its higher-order organization remain poorly understood. Here, we use high-speed atomic force microscopy to visualize interholoenzyme interaction of CaMKIIα at mesoscopic scales (5 to 500 nanometers). Under freely diffusible conditions, CaMKIIα holoenzymes do not form stable clusters. In contrast, when spatially confined, they assemble into chain-like clusters mediated by kinase-domain interactions. These clusters expand upon activation, concomitant with the dissociation of the regulatory segment. Notably, the CaMKIIα Pro212→Leu (P212L) mutant associated with neurodevelopmental disorders forms extensive clusters even in the basal state. Together, our findings demonstrate that CaMKIIα-CaMKIIα interactions drive mesoscale cluster formation and that precise regulation of cluster size and activation-dependent growth might be critical for synaptic signaling.
