A single-cell transcriptomic atlas of paper wasp brains highlights Kenyon cells' role in social behavior

为探究社会行为的细胞与分子机制，作者对纸胡蜂（Polistes fuscatus）四种表型（蜂后、工蜂、雌性蜂、雄蜂）的96,310个脑细胞进行单细胞测序，发现Kenyon细胞神经元构成脑的主要部分且其组成与基因表达随性别、品系和季节变化，并揭示社会互动及社会观察均可诱导Kenyon细胞的转录组响应和神经激活特征，为理解昆虫社会行为的神经基础提供了细胞级图谱。
纸胡蜂（Polistes fuscatus）脑单细胞图谱；96,310细胞；单细胞RNA测序；有脑区/细胞类型区分（Kenyon细胞多亚群、其他神经元、非神经细胞）。

作者：Behrens, C.; Chatterjee, M.; Jernigan, C. M.; Reed, R. D.; Toth, A.; Sheehan, M. J.
DOI: 10.64898/2026.11.05.760234 · bioRxiv 链接: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.05.760234
命中关键词：brain, neuron, neural, transcriptomic atlas, Cellular diversity
推荐理由：新发布的昆虫（纸胡蜂）脑单细胞图谱，属于最高优先级读物；虽聚焦社会行为而非直接演化比较，但为节肢动物神经演化比较提供关键参考数据。
背景：社会昆虫是研究社会行为的经典模型，但其神经细胞的分子编码机制仍不清楚。Kenyon细胞是昆虫蕈形体（mushroom body）的主要神经元类型，在学习、记忆和社会行为中起关键作用。节肢动物脑的细胞类型多样性与社会行为的关联，以及其演化保守性，是神经演化领域的重要问题。建立社会性昆虫的脑单细胞图谱有助于跨物种比较神经元类型的同源性与功能演化。
研究方法：作者采用单细胞RNA测序技术，对纸胡蜂（Polistes fuscatus）四种表型（foundresses、workers、gynes、males）的中央脑组织进行单细胞转录组分析，共获得96,310个细胞的转录组数据。通过无监督聚类和标记基因注释鉴定细胞类型，包括Kenyon细胞、其他神经元和非神经细胞的亚群。对社会互动实验组（直接互动、观察互动、对照）进行差异表达分析，结合神经激活标志物评估细胞类型特异性响应。
研究结果：研究鉴定了纸胡蜂脑的细胞多样性，发现Kenyon细胞神经元构成脑实质的显著部分，并存在多个功能各异的亚群。Kenyon细胞的组成和基因表达谱随性别、品系和季节发生显著变化。社会互动和观察社会互动均能诱导Kenyon细胞亚群的转录组重编程，表现为即刻早期基因和神经激活标志物的上调；重要的是，观察互动与直接互动的激活特征高度重叠，提示存在共享的'社会认知'神经程序。其他神经细胞和非神经群体也表现出社会状态依赖性变化。
研究意义：该研究首次建立了社会性昆虫的脑单细胞图谱，为节肢动物神经系统的细胞类型演化比较提供了重要资源。Kenyon细胞在社会行为中的可塑性揭示了昆虫脑功能与社会的关联，其数据可与果蝇、蚂蚁、蜜蜂等物种进行跨物种细胞类型同源性分析。虽聚焦行为而非直接演化问题，但该图谱是验证'独立起源'与'共同起源'假说、重建祖先细胞类型的关键数据基础，对理解两侧动物神经系统的演化创新具有重要参考价值。
中文全摘要：社会昆虫为研究社会互动提供独特机会，但其行为的细胞和分子机制尚不清楚。本研究对纸胡蜂（Polistes fuscatus）四种表型的96,310个脑细胞进行单细胞测序，发现Kenyon细胞神经元是脑的主要组成部分，其组成和基因表达随性别、品系和季节变化。社会互动甚至观察社会互动均可诱导Kenyon细胞的显著转录组响应和神经激活特征，且观察与直接互动的转录特征高度重叠，提示共享神经程序介导两种社会认知。
英文摘要（原文）：Social insects provide unique opportunities for studying social interactions, but the cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying these behaviors are poorly understood. Here, we performed single-cell sequencing to profile the transcriptomes of 96,310 cells across four phenotypes of Polistes fuscatus paper wasps: foundresses, workers, gynes, and males, to determine how social states and social interactions are encoded in the physiology of the central brain. We characterized the cellular diversity of the Polistes brain and demonstrate that a substantial portion of the brain is composed of diverse populations of Kenyon cell neurons. The composition and gene-expression profiles of these Kenyon cells, as well other neural and non-neural cell populations, vary across sex, caste, and season. We also demonstrate that social interactions, and even the mere observation of a social interaction, induce significant transcriptomic responses, including signatures of neural activation, across populations of Kenyon cell neurons. Notably, the transcriptional and activational signatures elicited by observing a social interaction frequently overlapped with those induced by direct social interaction, suggesting that a shared neural program underlies both direct social interactions and social observation.

英文摘要（原文）：Human pluripotent stem cell-derived models are increasingly used to recapitulate aspects of human corticogenesis, including the emergence of diverse cortical cell types and their progression through developmental states. These systems offer an opportunity to dissect the molecular mechanisms regulating transitions between cell states, but methods for perturbing them systematically at scale remain limited. Here, we developed a semi-automated culture and high-throughput arrayed screening platform for human cortical organoids. Using a library of 1,280 small molecules designed to systematically target major signaling and regulatory pathways, we screened for modulators of EOMES, which encodes for the transcription factor TBR2, and identified Emetine, an inhibitor of translation elongation, as the top hit. 英文摘要（原文）：Sex reversal, when the environment overrides genotypic sex determination, may hasten or delay climate-driven extinction in ectothermic animals, depending on sex-reversed individuals' reproductive ability and offspring viability. Here, we present a comprehensive dataset of wild frogs to compare sex-reversed and sex-concordant males for fertility, attractiveness to females, intrasexual competitiveness, and offspring survival in various environments. 推荐理由：空间转录组动力学重建方法虽具技术创新性，但缺乏演化或跨物种比较视角，且未涉及动物脑/神经系统单细胞图谱构建。
背景：构建虚拟细胞和组织需要重建相互作用细胞群体在时空中的演化过程，但现有的空间组学技术只能提供这一过程的稀疏离散快照，无法直接获得连续的细胞动态信息。如何从这些离散观测中推断出连续的、具有生物学意义的细胞状态和组织结构变化，是当前计算生物学和空间组学领域面临的重要挑战。
研究方法：作者开发了CytoBridge方法，基于联合转录组-空间空间，采用非平衡平均场薛定谔桥（unbalanced mean-field Schrödinger bridge）数学框架，学习细胞状态、空间位置和群体大小的随机轨迹。方法通过空间邻近性和配体-受体先验信息构建时变互作图，将细胞速度分解为基于邻域观测和配体-受体特征的互作分量，以及捕捉内在调控过程和未测量环境影响的内在-环境分量。
研究结果：CytoBridge在五种跨空间转录组平台数据集上进行了验证，涵盖发育（小鼠胚胎发生）、再生和神经退行性疾病等生物学过程。该方法能够准确生成未测量时间点的组织动态，追踪测量和未测量阶段的配体-受体信号通路，并支持假设驱动的计算扰动实验，展示了从异质性时空转录组测量重建连续演化虚拟组织的能力。
研究意义：CytoBridge为将离散的、异质性的时空转录组测量转化为连续演化的虚拟组织提供了通用计算框架，有助于弥合空间组学实验的稀疏采样与生物学过程连续性之间的鸿沟。该方法在发育生物学、再生医学和疾病机制研究中具有应用潜力，但其价值主要体现在计算方法学层面，对于关注演化比较和跨物种细胞类型同源性分析的研究者而言，需进一步适配和扩展才能直接应用。
中文全摘要：空间组学实验仅能提供细胞群体时空演化的稀疏快照。作者提出CytoBridge计算方法，基于联合转录组-空间空间，通过非平衡平均场薛定谔桥框架学习细胞状态、位置和群体大小的随机轨迹，显式整合细胞-细胞相互作用作为动态驱动。由空间邻近性和配体-受体先验构建的时变互作图将速度分解为邻域互作分量和内在调控分量。应用于五个跨平台数据集（涵盖发育、再生和神经退行性疾病），CytoBridge能准确生成未测量时间点的组织动态，追踪配体-受体信号，并支持计算扰动。
英文摘要（原文）：Building virtual cells and tissues requires reconstructing how interacting cell populations evolve in space and time, yet spatial omics experiments provide only sparse snapshots of this process. Here, we introduce CytoBridge, a computational method that reconstructs continuous virtual cell dynamics from discrete spatial transcriptomic data. Starting from a joint transcriptomic-spatial space, CytoBridge learns stochastic trajectories of cell states, positions, and population sizes through an unbalanced mean-field Schrodinger bridge formulation that explicitly incorporates cell-cell interaction as a dynamical driver. A time-varying interaction graph built from spatial proximity and ligand-receptor priors allows the velocity to be decomposed into an interaction component estimated from observed neighborhoods and ligand-receptor features, and an intrinsic-context component that captures intrinsic regulatory processes and unmeasured contextual influences. Applied to five datasets across three spatial transcriptomics platforms and spanning development, regeneration, and neurodegeneration, CytoBridge accurately generates tissue dynamics at unmeasured time points, follows ligand-receptor signaling through measured and unmeasured stages, and supports hypothesis-generating in silico perturbation. CytoBridge provides a general method for turning heterogeneous spatiotemporal transcriptomic measurements into continuously evolving virtual tissues.
