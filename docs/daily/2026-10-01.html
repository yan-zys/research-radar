英文摘要（原文）：Motivated by the successes of large language models (LLMs), we hypothesized that semantic information in the brain may be expressed in distributed patterns of neural activity. We recorded responses of hippocampal neurons while individuals listened to narrative speech. Here, using an encoding model approach, we find robust encoding of semantics, even after controlling for phonemics and grammar. Individual words had complex selectivities that included multiple words in multiple semantic categories. Similar to embedding vectors in LLMs, distance between neural population responses correlated with semantic distance. Ultimately, these results provide a neurocomputational account for understanding how hippocampal neurons encode word meaning during language listening.

英文摘要（原文）：Previous adversity increases sensitivity to subsequent stressful events1-8, but the causal underlying changes in brain circuitry are poorly understood. Here we harnessed unbiased whole-brain activity mapping to identify circuits that are functionally remodelled by previous adversity to promote heightened stress sensitivity. The anterior hypothalamic nucleus (AHN)-a region that has received little attention until now in the context of stress-displayed heightened stress reactivity in previously stressed mice.
