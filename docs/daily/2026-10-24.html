Retroviral insertions contributed to the divergence of human and chimpanzee brains.
逆转录病毒插入贡献人类与黑猩猩大脑的差异分化
为探究人类与黑猩猩脑分化的分子机制，作者对黑猩猩神经类器官中PTERV1内源性逆转录病毒进行表观基因组分析，利用CRISPR敲除实验验证其功能，发现chr19上的一株黑猩猩特异性PTERV1通过DNA甲基化沉默LINC00662并影响神经发育，揭示了逆转录病毒插入在人类-黑猩猩脑演化中的作用，但该研究局限于单一灵长类支系，缺乏跨物种演化比较视角。

DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.aeg5791 · PubMed 链接: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42913018/
命中关键词：brain evolution, brain development, brain, neural
推荐理由：该研究仅聚焦于人类-黑猩猩这一单一灵长类支系的脑分化机制，缺乏跨物种比较演化视角，且未使用单细胞转录组学技术，不符合该研究者核心研究范式。
背景：人类与黑猩猩作为近缘物种，在脑结构和认知能力上存在显著差异，但其蛋白编码基因高度相似。内源性逆转录病毒等转座元件可能通过改变基因调控驱动物种特异性状演化，但此类元件在神经系统发育和演化中的功能尚待深入阐明。
研究方法：研究者采用表观基因组学方法（DNA甲基化测序、异染色质分析）检测黑猩猩神经类器官中的PTERV1插入位点；利用CRISPR-Cas9技术删除黑猩猩iPSC中特异性PTERV1插入；通过神经类器官培养、神经元分化实验及功能 assays（轴突生长、神经元成熟等）验证LINC00662的功能。
研究结果：黑猩猩PTERV1插入在神经类器官中高度DNA甲基化，形成超过150个物种特异性异染色质域。chr19上的PTERV1插入通过DNA甲基化蔓延沉默lncRNA LINC00662；CRISPR删除该插入可恢复LINC00662表达。LINC00662在人类中特异性表达，促进神经元成熟、轴突生长和神经类器官发育。
研究意义：该研究揭示了内源性逆转录病毒插入通过调控lncRNA表达参与人-猩脑分化的机制，为理解转座元件在神经系统演化中的调控作用提供了新视角。然而，该研究局限于单一灵长类支系内部比较，缺乏跨物种演化框架，未涉及细胞类型水平分析，也未能整合多物种比较数据以推断祖先状态或趋同/趋异演化模式。
中文全摘要：过去500-700万年间，人类与黑猩猩在脑大小、结构和认知方面发生分化，尽管蛋白编码基因高度保守。宿主基因组中的逆转录病毒感染引入了众多物种特异性调控元件。黑猩猩携带人类缺失的谱系特异性PTERV1内源性逆转录病毒，但其在脑演化中的作用不明。作者对黑猩猩神经类器官进行PTERV1插入位点的表观基因组分析，发现其高度DNA甲基化，形成150余个物种特异性异染色质域。鉴定到chr19上一株黑猩猩特异性插入通过甲基化蔓延沉默lncRNA LINC00662；CRISPR删除该PTERV1可恢复LINC00662表达。LINC00662在人类脑发育中表达，参与神经元成熟、轴突生长和神经类器官发育。该研究揭示了内源性逆转录病毒插入贡献人-猩脑分化的机制。
英文摘要（原文）：Over the past 5 to 7 million years, humans and chimpanzees have diverged in brain size, structure, and cognition despite high conservation of protein-coding genes. However, retroviral infections within host genomes have introduced numerous species-specific regulatory elements. Chimpanzees harbor lineage-specific PTERV1 endogenous retroviruses that are absent in humans, but their role in brain evolution has remained unclear. We performed an epigenomic analysis of PTERV1 insertions in chimpanzee neural organoids and found they are heavily DNA methylated, forming over 150 species-specific heterochromatin domains with the potential to influence gene regulation. We identified a chimpanzee-specific insertion on chromosome 19 that silences the long noncoding RNA LINC00662 through DNA methylation spreading. CRISPR-mediated deletion of this PTERV1 restored LINC00662 expression in chimpanzee induced pluripotent stem cells. We show that LINC00662, a human-specific transcript expressed during brain development, plays a role in neuronal maturation, axon outgrowth, and neural organoid development. These findings reveal a mechanism by which endogenous retroviral insertions contributed to human-chimpanzee brain divergence.

推荐理由：该论文聚焦小鼠早期胚胎发育的力学-转录组耦合，无跨物种比较或演化视角，仅涉及少量成年神经干细胞数据，与研究者核心的脑演化、跨物种细胞类型比较方向不符。
背景：多细胞组织的发育与维持需要细胞状态与其局部环境（包括几何和力学信号）紧密耦合，但在完整组织中研究这种耦合关系一直面临挑战，因为现有测量技术无法同时评估力学特性和分子定义的细胞状态。
研究方法：作者开发了统一转录组与力学图谱（UTMM）方法，该技术包含两项创新：（1）针对完整3D胚胎的靶向原位RNA测序方法（3DISS）；（2）利用高频细胞内细胞器波动提供细胞级粘弹性柔顺性的相对、非侵入性在体读数的策略。随后将UTMM应用于小鼠从受精卵到桑椹胚阶段的胚胎，并拓展至成年脑的神经干细胞。
研究结果：在小鼠早期桑椹胚中，滋养外胚层与内细胞团谱系的转录和形态学差异逐渐显现，同时所有细胞从2细胞期到桑椹胚期表现出细胞内柔顺性（软化）的逐渐增加。早期谱系偏倚与不同的力学状态相关，反映了正在出现的发育程序。通过渗透压延迟该软化过程会阻碍胚胎进展，支持细胞内物质状态与发育时序之间的功能关系。在成年脑神经干细胞中，增殖的神经干细胞比静息的神经干细胞表现出更大的细胞内柔顺性。
研究意义：UTMM能够桥接多细胞系统中的分子与力学动态，为研究分子状态、几何结构和细胞内力学如何在多细胞发育中协调一致提供了有力框架。但该研究主要聚焦小鼠早期胚胎发育，缺乏跨物种比较或演化视角，对理解脑演化、细胞类型同源性等核心问题的直接贡献有限。
中文全摘要：多细胞组织的发育与维持要求细胞状态与局部环境（包括几何与力学信号）紧密耦合，但现有技术难以在完整组织中同时测量力学特性与分子定义的细胞状态。作者开发UTMM方法，通过靶向原位RNA测序（3DISS）和高频细胞器波动分析，实现完整3D结构中全转录组与细胞粘弹性的同步测量。应用于小鼠植入前胚胎发现，从2细胞期至桑椹胚期细胞内柔顺性逐渐增加，且早期谱系偏倚与不同力学状态相关；延迟软化会阻碍发育。该方法亦可区分成年脑内增殖与静息神经干细胞的力学差异。
英文摘要（原文）：The development and maintenance of multicellular tissues require that cell states be closely coupled to their local environment, including geometric and mechanical cues. However, studying this coupling in intact tissues has been challenging, because existing measurement technologies cannot simultaneously assess mechanical properties and molecularly defined cell states. To address this gap, we introduce the Unified Transcriptome and Mechanics Map (UTMM), a method for concurrent measurement of the transcriptome and cytoplasmic stiffness (high-frequency intracellular mechanical compliance) within intact 3D multicellular structures. UTMM relies on two innovations: (1) a targeted in situ RNA sequencing approach for intact 3D embryos (3DISS), and (2) a strategy that leverages high-frequency intracellular organelle fluctuations to provide a relative, non-invasive readout of cell-level viscoelastic compliance in situ. We applied UTMM to mouse embryos from the zygote through the morula stage to characterize how RNA expression and intracellular mechanical state becomes coupled during early lineage specification. Our data reveal that, in the early morula, transcriptional and morphological distinctions emerge between trophectoderm and inner cell mass (ICM) lineages, coinciding with a gradual increase in intracellular compliance (softening) across all cells from the 2-cell through morula stages. Furthermore, we observe that early lineage biases associated with distinct mechanical states, reflecting emerging developmental programs. When we osmotically delayed this softening, embryonic progression was impeded, supporting a functional relationship between intracellular material state and developmental timing. Extending this approach to neural stem cells in the adult brain (NSCs), we further show that proliferated NSCs exhibit greater intracellular compliance than quiescent NSCs, NSC state-dependent differences in the mechanical properties and that the analytical framework can resolve biologically associated mechanical differences in a distinct tissue context. Together, these results demonstrate UTMM's ability to bridge molecular and mechanical dynamics in multicellular systems, providing a powerful framework for investigating how molecular state, geometry, and intracellular mechanics become coordinated during multicellular development.

研究结果：CardamomOT在模拟数据集的GRN和轨迹重建上表现优于现有方法。在实验数据集上，重建的细胞轨迹、速度场和潜在蛋白质水平相互一致，GRN结构与已知生物学一致。改进后的校准机械模型可作为生成式模型，生成对未观测扰动的细胞响应预测。该方法将机械GRN推断、轨迹重建和真实数据集模拟整合为统一的scRNA-seq时间序列分析框架。

英文摘要（原文）：A key challenge in inferring gene regulatory networks (GRNs) governing cellular processes such as differentiation and reprogramming from experimental data lies in the impossibility of directly measuring protein dynamics at the single-cell level, which prevents establishing causal relationships between regulator activity and target responses. In earlier work, we introduced CARDAMOM, an algorithm that uses temporal snapshots of scRNA-seq data to calibrate a GRN-driven mechanistic model of gene expression. However, this method had several limitations: it could only rely on the relative ordering of time points rather than their exact labels, imposed restrictive quasi-stationary assumptions on protein dynamics, and depended on multiple hyperparameters. Here, we present CardamomOT, a new method based on the same mechanistic model that jointly reconstructs the GRN and unobserved protein trajectories from the data within a mechanistic optimal transport framework. By incorporating exact time labels and priors on protein kinetic rates from the literature, and substantially reducing the number of required hyperparameters, our approach addresses these limitations and substantially improves the accuracy and robustness of GRN calibration. We validate our framework on both in silico and experimental datasets, demonstrating computational scalability and consistently improved performance over state-of-the-art methods in both GRN and trajectory reconstruction on simulated datasets, and, on experimental datasets, reconstruction of cellular trajectories, velocity fields and latent protein levels that are mutually consistent, together with GRN structures consistent with known biology. We also show that these improvements make the calibrated mechanistic model suitable to be used as a generative model to generate testable predictions of cellular responses to unseen perturbations. To our knowledge, this is among the first methods to explicitly integrate mechanistic GRN inference, trajectory reconstruction, and simulation of realistic datasets into a unified framework for scRNA-seq time series analysis.
