章鱼脑发育中胚胎胶质细胞的动态作用
为揭示头足类大型神经系统发育与演化的细胞分子机制，研究者以普通章鱼为模型，整合单细胞多组学与空间表达分析，鉴定出一群早期胚胎胶质细胞群体，其特征为nr4a、dlx和pros调控网络，并发现这些细胞在神经元分化前即参与细胞迁移、轴突导向和脑区模式化；跨物种比较揭示该细胞类型在软体动物中的保守性及其在头足类中的功能创新，为理解两侧动物神经系统独立演化与胶质细胞功能多样化提供了关键证据。
普通章鱼（Octopus vulgaris）胚胎神经系统单细胞多组学图谱，涵盖单细胞RNA-seq、单细胞ATAC-seq和空间表达分析；明确区分了神经祖细胞、早期胚胎胶质细胞及多种神经元和胶质细胞类型，包含脑区空间分布信息。

作者：Lassnig MA, Geslain E, Nguyen B, Abaffyová D, Styfhals R, Spanier K, Patel A, Gattamraju A, Poovathingal S, Wojno M, Van Dijck M, Almansa E, Rogers TF, Simakov O, Accorsi A, Seuntjens E
DOI: 10.1016/j.cub.2026.09.068 · PubMed 链接: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42906557/
命中关键词：brain development, brain, neuron, neural, glia, Drosophila, octopus
推荐理由：章鱼脑发育的单细胞多组学图谱，结合跨物种比较揭示胚胎胶质细胞的演化起源与功能创新。
背景：头足类动物是研究神经系统复杂性独立演化的重要非模型系统，其大脑在结构复杂性和功能上可与脊椎动物比拟，但起源和发育机制长期未知。胶质细胞在神经系统发育中的早期作用，特别是在神经元回路建立前的模式化功能，是理解脑扩张和演化的关键。然而，由于缺乏单细胞分辨率的发育图谱，头足类胶质细胞的类型、起源和功能几乎一无所知。
研究方法：研究者以普通章鱼（Octopus vulgaris）为对象，采用单细胞RNA测序、单细胞ATAC测序等多组学技术，结合空间转录组学分析，构建章鱼胚胎神经系统的单细胞图谱；通过跨物种转录组比较，分析章鱼、另一种头足类、福寿螺（Pomacea canaliculata）及黑腹果蝇（Drosophila）胚胎胶质细胞的分子特征与演化关系。
研究结果：研究鉴定了一群独特的早期胚胎胶质细胞群体，其分子特征为nr4a、dlx和pros调控网络活性，空间分布和动态转录谱表明这些细胞在神经元标志物表达前即参与细胞迁移、轴突导向和脑模式化。跨物种比较揭示该细胞类型在软体动物中的保守性：nr4a、dlx和pros在软体动物胚胎胶质细胞中共同表达，而果蝇胚胎胶质细胞缺失nr4a表达。头足类胚胎胶质细胞的整体基因表达谱已与腹足类福寿螺发生显著分化，后者胶质细胞丧失导向和模式化基因表达。
研究意义：该研究首次在单细胞分辨率上揭示了章鱼神经系统的发育蓝图，鉴定了在软体动物中保守但功能演化的胚胎胶质细胞类型，为理解头足类大型复杂脑的独立演化提供了细胞和分子基础。发现强调了胶质细胞在神经系统发育早期的模式化功能，挑战了胶质细胞仅作为支持细胞的经典观点，并为跨两侧动物门的神经系统比较和细胞类型同源性研究提供了重要资源。
中文全摘要：头足类动物（章鱼、鱿鱼、墨鱼）演化出神经元数量可与小哺乳动物媲美的大型神经系统，但其发育机制、神经细胞形成的遗传程序及脑扩张的演化因素尚不清楚。本研究结合单细胞多组学与空间表达分析，在普通章鱼中鉴定出由孤儿核受体4a（nr4a）、远端同源框（dlx）和prospero同源框（pros）调控网络定义的早期胚胎胶质细胞群体，其动态转录组特征表明这些细胞在表达神经元标志物或分化为成熟胶质细胞前，即可能协调细胞迁移、轴突导向和脑模式化。跨物种比较显示，nr4a、dlx和pros在软体动物胚胎胶质细胞中共享表达，而果蝇胚胎胶质细胞表达pros、dlx、repo、ttk和gcm但不表达nr4a；头足类胚胎胶质细胞的整体表达谱已与福寿螺分化，后者胶质细胞不表达导向或模式化基因。
英文摘要（原文）：Coleoid cephalopods, such as octopus, squid, and cuttlefish, have evolved remarkably large nervous systems with neuron numbers comparable to those of small mammals. Despite this, little is known about how these nervous systems develop, which genetic programs drive the formation and diversification of their neural cells, and what factors contribute to evolutionary brain expansion. This study combined single-cell multi-omics with spatial expression analysis to uncover the cell types and the regulatory mechanisms underlying nervous system development and evolution in Octopus vulgaris. We discovered an early embryonic glial cell population that is distinct from neurogenic progenitors and defined by orphan nuclear receptor 4a (nr4a), distal-less homeobox (dlx), and prospero homeobox (pros) regulons. Their spatial distribution and dynamic transcriptional profile during embryogenesis indicated that these cells might orchestrate cell migration, axon guidance, and brain patterning before expressing neuronal markers or differentiating into mature glia. Comparison of embryonic glial transcriptional signatures across two cephalopods, a gastropod, and Drosophila revealed shared expression of nr4a, dlx, and pros in mollusks, while embryonic glia in Drosophila expressed pros, dlx, repo, ttk, and gcm but not nr4a. Intriguingly, the overall gene expression profile of cephalopod embryonic glia has diverged from that of Pomacea canaliculata (golden apple snail), whose glia do not express guidance or patterning genes. Together, these findings identified a conserved embryonic glial cell type in mollusks that might have acquired new functions in coleoid cephalopods to organize neuronal migration and wiring in their expanded brain.

Tubulin autoregulation factors recruit γ-tubulin to non-centrosomal MTOCs for neuronal microtubule nucleation.
微管蛋白自调控因子招募γ-微管蛋白至非中心体微管组织中心以促进神经元微管成核
为揭示神经元中非中心体微管阵列的形成机制，作者以C. elegans和大鼠海马神经元为模型，利用条件性单细胞降解等位基因和内源标记技术，发现scaper和ttc5通过招募γ-微管蛋白至非中心体微管组织中心调控微管成核，该机制在轴突再生中起关键作用并具跨物种保守性。

作者：Xie Y, Comincini A, Feng C, Davison CA, Hammarlund M, Bartolini F, Yogev S
DOI: 10.1016/j.cub.2026.09.013 · PubMed 链接: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42976846/
命中关键词：neuron
推荐理由：该论文聚焦于神经元微管细胞骨架的分子机制，缺乏演化比较视角、单细胞组学或跨物种分析，与该研究者核心的细胞类型演化与比较基因组学方向不符。
背景：神经元具有独特的非中心体微管阵列，这些阵列对于维持神经元形态、轴突运输和突触功能至关重要。微管成核主要由γ-微管蛋白介导，但神经元中γ-微管蛋白如何被招募至非中心体微管组织中心的机制长期不明。微管蛋白自调控是一种通过调控微管蛋白mRNA稳定性来维持微管蛋白平衡的机制，但其与微管成核之间是否存在关联尚不清楚。
研究方法：研究者主要使用模式生物C. elegans进行遗传学分析，包括构建scaper和ttc5的条件性单细胞降解等位基因、内源蛋白标签化、RNA干扰以及荧光标记的微管成像。同时利用大鼠原代海马神经元培养体系验证保守性，并通过药物诱导的神经元活化和微管成核检测实验评估功能相关性。
研究结果：研究发现scaper和ttc5缺失导致C. elegans神经元微管数量显著减少，表型类似于γ-微管蛋白缺失。机制上，SCAPER和TTC5对于将γ-微管蛋白招募至非中心体微管组织中心是必需的，且SCAPER具有指导性功能——其过表达可增加成核位点γ-微管蛋白水平和微管密度。功能上，这些因子对发育期轴突生长非必需，但在轴突再生中发挥关键作用：SCAPER将γ-微管蛋白招募至生长锥定位的微管组织中心以定向微管成核。此外，SCAPER在大鼠海马神经元活性依赖性微管成核中亦为必需。
研究意义：该研究揭示了神经元中非中心体微管成核的分子机制，建立了微管蛋白自调控与微管成核之间的功能联系。研究为理解神经元细胞骨架的动态调控提供了新视角，并为神经损伤后再生修复研究提供了潜在靶点。然而，该研究未涉及演化比较框架，物种范围限于C. elegans和大鼠，缺乏对微管成核机制演化起源的探讨。
中文全摘要：神经元功能与存活依赖于特征性的非中心体微管阵列，但其形成机制尚不清楚。作者发现两个此前参与微管蛋白mRNA周转的因子scaper和ttc5通过γ-微管蛋白依赖性成核调控神经元微管含量。在C. elegans神经元中，scaper、ttc5或两者缺失可降低微管数量，且二者对于将γ-微管蛋白招募至非中心体微管组织中心不可或缺。SCAPER过表达可提高成核位点γ-微管蛋白水平并增加微管密度。这些因子对发育期轴突生长非必需，但对再生至关重要，其中SCAPER将γ-微管蛋白招募至生长锥定位的微管组织中心以定向微管成核。此外，SCAPER在大鼠海马神经元活性依赖性微管成核中亦为必需，提示其功能保守性。
英文摘要（原文）：Neuronal function and survival depend on stereotyped non-centrosomal microtubule (MT) arrays. How these arrays form remains poorly understood. Here, we found that two factors previously implicated in tubulin mRNA turnover control neuronal MT content through γ-tubulin-dependent nucleation. In C. elegans neurons, loss of scaper, ttc5, or both reduced MT numbers to a similar degree as depletion of γ-tubulin, the main MT nucleator. Using conditional single-cell degradation alleles and endogenous tagging, we find that scaper and ttc5 are required to recruit γ-tubulin to non-centrosomal MT organizing centers (MTOCs). SCAPER is also instructive, as its overexpression raises γ-tubulin levels at nucleation sites and increases MT density. SCAPER, TTC5, and γ-tubulin are dispensable for developmental axon growth but play essential roles during regeneration, where SCAPER recruits γ-tubulin to a growth-cone-localized MTOC to orient MT nucleation. Last, SCAPER is required for activity-dependent MT nucleation in rat hippocampal neurons, suggesting a conserved role. These findings reveal mechanisms governing neuronal cytoskeleton assembly and function and suggest potential crosstalk between tubulin autoregulation and MT nucleation.

英文摘要（原文）：The origin of the avian wishbone (furcula)-present since theropod dinosaurs-remains unsolved. Its homology is still debated, with competing hypotheses proposing derivation from the clavicles, the interclavicle, or both. To obtain comparative developmental evidence, we studied ossification and pre-ossification mesenchymal condensations in a broad sample of birds and non-avian reptiles. Relative positions of paired condensations and their ossification centers support homology between the lateral arms of the furcula and reptilian clavicles. However, in the palaeognathous bird Nothoprocta perdicaria, we observed a separate interclavicle-like medial condensation. 英文摘要（原文）：Progesterone is a critical reproductive hormone that acts via progesterone receptor transcriptional regulators. The short PGR-A isoform lacks a 164-amino acid amino-terminal region present in PGR-B that markedly enhances transcriptional activity. Isoform-specific mutants inactivating either PGR-A (PRAKO) or PGR-B (PRBKO) indicated that PGR-A is specifically essential for female fertility. This study revises that interpretation by showing that an inadvertent frameshift mutation in the PRAKO caused ablation of both isoforms, not the intended PGR-A isoform-specific mutation. A true PGR-A-specific mutant generated through CRISPR-Cas9 editing with a complete lack of PGR-A but retained PGR-B expression showed a phenotype indistinguishable from wild type. Thus, while the short PGR-A isoform has distinct function, it is not independently essential for female reproduction in mice, and the physiological role of these highly conserved isoforms must be reconsidered in light of this new information.

英文摘要（原文）：Resistance-nodulation-cell division (RND) efflux pumps are the major cause of multidrug resistance in Pseudomonas aeruginosa. The inner membrane protein MexK from P. aeruginosa is a narrow-spectrum RND transporter. It assembles with membrane fusion protein MexJ and outer membrane proteins to form a tripartite efflux complex that efficiently exports drugs like triclosan. Here, we resolve the cryo-EM structures of apo-MexK at 3.4-angstrom resolution and the triclosan-bound MexJK complex at 2.6-angstrom resolution.
