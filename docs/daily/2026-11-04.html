DPYD links glial lipids and myelination to sleep duration

为探索睡眠调控的遗传机制，作者以DPYD基因为靶点，采用跨物种比较方法结合遗传学与药理学操作，发现DPYD及其直系同源基因在胶质细胞中通过调控脑脂质促进睡眠，其缺失导致髓鞘化减少且睡眠缩短，并在果蝇中发现该基因在包裹胶质中通过形成脂质滴促进睡眠，揭示了胶质脂质在睡眠调控中的深层保守作用。

作者：Zimmerman, A. J.; Deal, S.; Almeraya Del Valle, E.; Donnelly, J.; Kroll, F.; Guo, X.; Tsundupalli, T.; Shetty, Z.; Doldur-Balli, F.; Keenan, B.; Grant, S. F.; Rihel, J.; Sehgal, A.; Pack, A. I.
DOI: 10.64898/2026.10.31.763190 · bioRxiv 链接: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.31.763190
命中关键词：brain, glia, cross-species, Drosophila, zebrafish
推荐理由：虽涉及跨物种比较（小鼠、斑马鱼、果蝇）且含胶质细胞/髓鞘演化线索，但核心为睡眠调控的生理机制研究，缺乏演化假说检验、细胞类型同源分析或基因调控网络比较等该研究者核心关注点。
背景：睡眠作为一种基本的生理状态，其调控机制在进化上高度保守，但背后的遗传和细胞机制远未阐明。胶质细胞，特别是少突胶质细胞及其形成的髓鞘，近年来被认为在神经环路功能和调控中扮演重要角色，但胶质细胞与睡眠之间的因果联系尚缺乏分子水平的证据。此外，跨物种比较为识别保守的生物学机制提供了有力工具，但如何将分子功能与演化上的细胞类型创新（如髓鞘的出现）相联系，仍是神经演化领域的重要课题。
研究方法：研究者采用跨物种比较策略，在小鼠（体内基因敲除）、斑马鱼（遗传和药理学操作结合行为学分析）和果蝇（遗传操作结合睡眠行为检测）中功能验证DPYD及其直系同源基因；利用单细胞/组织水平表达分析定位基因表达细胞类型；通过脂质组学分析、髓鞘染色和电镜观察评估髓鞘化程度；结合人类GWAS数据与顺式调控元件分析，将遗传变异与细胞类型特异性基因调控关联。
研究结果：DPYD在小鼠和斑马鱼中富集于少突胶质细胞谱系，其缺失导致髓鞘化减少和睡眠缩短，且不依赖于尿嘧啶分解代谢功能。斑马鱼中改变髓鞘化足以改变睡眠时长。果蝇DPYD直系同源基因在包裹胶质中通过促进脂质滴形成来调控睡眠，提示胶质脂质调控的深层保守性。人类睡眠相关GWAS变异定位于少突胶质细胞特异性的DPYD顺式调控元件。
研究意义：该研究建立了DPYD作为连接髓鞘生物学与睡眠的分子纽带，揭示了胶质脂质在睡眠调控中的深层保守作用，为理解胶质细胞功能提供了新视角。然而，该研究主要聚焦生理机制而非演化假说检验，其"跨物种"设计服务于功能保守性验证而非系统性的细胞类型同源比较或演化创新分析，对该研究者核心关注的脑演化、细胞类型起源与基因调控网络演化的直接参考价值有限。
中文全摘要：睡眠调控具有遗传性且跨物种保守，但遗传机制不明。本研究探究DPYD基因（编码二氢嘧啶脱氢酶）在睡眠中的作用。通过跨物种方法，发现DPYD及其直系同源基因特异性地在胶质细胞中通过调控脑脂质促进睡眠。在小鼠和斑马鱼中，DPYD直系同源基因富集于少突胶质细胞谱系，其缺失减少髓鞘化并缩短睡眠，且独立于其在尿嘧啶分解为β-丙氨酸中的已知功能。斑马鱼中遗传和药理学操纵髓鞘显示改变髓鞘化足以扰动睡眠时长。在缺乏髓鞘的果蝇中，DPYD直系同源基因在包裹胶质中通过形成胶质脂质滴促进睡眠——后者被认为是髓鞘的演化前体。此外，人类GWAS鉴定的睡眠变异位于保守顺式调控元件中，该元件选择性接触少突胶质细胞内DPYD启动子，将人类遗传关联与少突胶质细胞对DPYD的特异性调控相连。
英文摘要（原文）：The regulation of sleep is heritable and conserved across species1,2, but the governing genetic mechanisms remain poorly understood. Here, we investigate the role of DPYD--which encodes the pyrimidine catabolic enzyme, dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase--in sleep. Using a cross-species approach, we show that DPYD and its orthologs act specifically in glia to promote sleep through the regulation of brain lipids. In mouse and zebrafish, we find that DPYD orthologs are enriched in oligodendrocyte lineage cells, and their loss reduces myelination and sleep independent of DPYDs established role in uracil catabolism to {beta}-alanine. Genetic and pharmacological manipulations of myelin in zebrafish show that altering myelination is sufficient to perturb sleep duration. In Drosophila, which lack myelin, we find that the DPYD ortholog acts in ensheathing glia to promote sleep through formation of glial lipid droplets, a proposed evolutionary precursor to myelin. Furthermore, a human GWAS-identified sleep variant2 resides in a conserved cis-regulatory element that selectively contacts the promoter of DPYD within oligodendrocytes, connecting the human genetic association to oligodendrocyte-specific control of DPYD. Together, these results identify a deeply conserved role for glial lipids in sleep regulation and establish DPYD as a mechanistic link between myelin biology and sleep.

英文摘要（原文）：Large-scale cortical dynamics during natural behavior are difficult to measure because head-mounted imaging systems must balance field of view, spatial resolution, weight, and optical complexity. Existing wide-field miniature microscopes often rely on compound or custom-corrected optical assemblies, which increase device complexity and limit accessibility for laboratories without specialized optics expertise. Here, we present the Miniature Integrated Microlens Array System (MIMAS), an open-source head-mounted fluorescence microscope that replaces conventional multi-element optics with a 4 × 5 microlens array as its sole imaging element. By combining modular subfield acquisition with computational registration and stitching, MIMAS achieves cellular-resolution imaging across an approximately 4 × 4.5 mm2 field of view while weighing only 2.31 g. In freely behaving mice with sparse labeling of layer 2/3 cortical neurons, MIMAS enabled simultaneous calcium imaging across multiple dorsal cortical regions, with signals extracted from thousands of neurons. This broad sampling captured distributed cortical activity related to locomotor state and sparse position-modulated responses during open-field exploration without detectable disruption of locomotion or loss of signal quality over 30 min of recording. Beyond neuronal imaging, MIMAS also supported wide-field vascular imaging and resolved vessel caliber-dependent hemodynamic responses to isoflurane. These results establish MIMAS as a lightweight, accessible, and versatile platform for large-scale fluorescence imaging in freely behaving mice and demonstrate an alternative strategy for wide-field cortical imaging that lowers the optical complexity barrier for individual laboratories.

In the whisker system, individual whisker identity is preserved along the whisker-brainstem-thalamus-cortex pathway, culminating in distinct cortical domains: barrels and septa. Using simultaneous in vivo recordings from barrel and septal domains, we identify a progressive divergence in spiking activity during repeated single- and multi-whisker stimulation. While the multi- to single-whisker response ratio remains stable in barrels, it increases progressively in septa, suggesting recruitment of local inhibitory circuits. Together, these findings support a model in which Elfn1-dependent recruitment of SST+ interneurons contributes to preferential multi-whisker integration and functional specialization within the mouse somatosensory cortex.

英文摘要（原文）：Urination, a vital and conserved process of emptying urine from the urinary bladder in mammals, requires precise coordination between the bladder and external urethral sphincter (EUS) that is tightly controlled by a complex neural network. However, the specific subpopulation of neurons that accounts for such coordination remains unidentified, limiting the development of target-specific therapies for certain urination disorders, for example, detrusor-sphincter dyssynergia. Here, we find that cells expressing estrogen receptor 1 (ESR1+) in the pontine micturition center (PMC) initiate voiding when activated and suspend ongoing voiding when suppressed, each at 100% reliability. Thus, we identify a cell type in the brainstem that controls the bladder-urethra coordination for urination.

推荐理由：研究聚焦昆虫嗅觉受体蛋白的结构与功能，虽有跨物种比较，但无单细胞组学、无神经系统细胞类型演化分析，与研究者核心方向差距较大。
背景：昆虫嗅觉依赖于 odorant receptor (OR) 与 odorant receptor co-receptor (Orco) 形成的异源复合体，但OR家族在昆虫中经历了广泛的序列分歧和谱系特异性扩张，使得跨物种大尺度比较变得复杂。尽管此前已对个别物种的OR功能有所研究，但缺乏系统性的类群范围分析框架来整合序列、结构和功能信息。此外，Orco作为OR复合体中的通用共受体，其演化起源和结构特化机制尚不清楚，尤其是从同源受体到异源复合体的关键演化转变过程。
研究方法：研究者整合了三种互补策略：基于序列相似性的聚类分析、AlphaFold2预测的结构相似性比较，以及大规模分子对接模拟。开发了'trunk-branch'网络框架对OR进行分类，该框架首先识别核心保守结构特征（trunk），再捕获谱系特异性变异（branch）。对115种代表性昆虫的ORs进行了系统分析，并构建了跨物种的比较对接谱。
研究结果：OR库大小与成虫食性、幼虫食性及栖息地显著相关，但与昼夜节律无关。尽管序列和结构群落组成差异显著，几乎所有被抽样目均含有归属于全部六种对接谱群落的ORs。全变态昆虫表现出更高的OR序列群落多样性。二叠纪末大灭绝前起源的目与后起源的目之间，对接谱组成存在显著差异。Orco蛋白中存在一个高度保守的胞外β-折叠结构，该结构物理性限制配体进入结合口袋，可能解释其减弱的气味分子结合能力。
研究意义：该研究建立了首个整合序列、结构和功能信息的昆虫OR类群范围比较框架，为后续实验和演化研究提供了可检验的假说。对Orco结构特化的发现揭示了从同源到异源受体复合体这一关键演化转变的分子机制，深化了对昆虫嗅觉系统起源和多样化的理解。'主干-分支'网络方法也为其他快速演化的膜蛋白家族的比较研究提供了可借鉴的方法学范式。
中文全摘要：昆虫嗅觉由异源四聚体OR-Orco复合体介导，但序列分歧和谱系特异性扩张阻碍了大尺度比较。研究整合序列/结构相似性与分子对接分析115种昆虫ORs，发现OR库大小与成虫/幼虫食性及栖息地相关。新开发的'主干-分支'网络框架将ORs按序列和结构相似性分类，大尺度对接揭示几乎所有目均包含全部六种对接谱群落。全变态昆虫OR序列群落多样性高于非全变态类群，且二叠纪末大灭绝前后的谱系间对接谱组成存在差异。关键发现为Orco中高度保守的胞外β-折叠限制配体进入结合口袋，可能导致其对天然气味分子结合能力降低。
英文摘要（原文）：Insect olfaction is facilitated by a heterotetrameric odorant receptor-odorant receptor co-receptor (OR-Orco) complex. However, extensive sequence divergence and lineage-specific expansion have complicated large-scale comparisons of OR repertoires across insects. Here, we integrated sequence similarity, structural similarity, and molecular docking to analyze ORs from 115 insect species. OR repertoire size was associated with adult diet, larval diet, and habitat, but not with circadian rhythm. We developed a 'trunk-branch' network framework to classify ORs according to sequence and structural similarity and used large-scale molecular docking to derive comparative docking profiles. Despite marked differences in sequence- and structural-community composition, almost all sampled orders contained ORs assigned to all six docking-profile communities. Holometabolous insects showed higher OR sequence-community diversity than non-holometabolous insects, and docking-profile composition differed between extant lineages whose order-level origins predate or postdate the end-Permian mass extinction. Most notably, we identified a highly conserved extracellular β-sheet in Orco that restricts ligand access to the binding pocket and may contribute to its reduced capacity to bind natural odorants. The emergence of Orco represents a key evolutionary transition from homomeric receptors to heteromeric OR-Orco complexes, and our results suggest that this transition may have been accompanied by pronounced specialization of the extracellular domain and binding pocket. Together, these analyses provide a class-wide framework for comparing insect OR diversity and generate testable hypotheses for future experimental and evolutionary studies.
