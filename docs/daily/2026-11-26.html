背景：囊性棘球蚴病是一种严重的人畜共患寄生虫病，由细粒棘球绦虫（Echinococcus granulosus）感染引起。寄生虫的原头节（protoscolex）具有复杂的细胞组成和发育可塑性，但其细胞异质性和谱系分化机制尚不清楚。理解原头节的细胞组成与发育调控对于控制寄生虫增殖和发现新型抗寄生虫靶点具有重要意义。
研究方法：研究者利用单细胞RNA测序技术构建了细粒棘球绦虫原头节的单细胞转录组图谱，通过聚类分析鉴定主要细胞群体；采用RNA速度分析推断细胞分化轨迹；结合差异基因表达分析和功能注释筛选候选发育关键基因。
研究结果：研究鉴定出6种主要细胞群体：生发细胞、肌肉细胞、神经元、储存细胞、皮层细胞和纤毛细胞。生发细胞表现出显著异质性，部分维持未分化转录特征，部分已启动谱系相关程序。RNA速度分析提示4条潜在分化轨迹，包括未分化群体内部的自我更新与稳态维持轨迹，以及向3个不同谱系亚群分化的趋势。研究还鉴定出p53同源基因和苏氨酸脱氢酶tdh作为候选发育关键基因。
研究意义：该研究首次提供了细粒棘球绦虫的单细胞转录组图谱，揭示了原头节的细胞异质性和潜在分化轨迹，为理解寄生虫发育生物学提供了重要资源。鉴定出的候选发育关键基因可为后续功能研究及抗蠕虫药物靶点探索提供基础，但研究完全聚焦于寄生虫学问题，与演化生物学或比较基因组学无直接关联。

英文摘要（原文）：Cystic echinococcosis is a zoonotic parasitic disease caused by infection with Echinococcus granulosus. Progressive cyst growth can compress host tissues and elicit immune responses. Characterizing the cellular composition and lineage differentiation of E. granulosus protoscoleces may provide insight into the regulation of parasite proliferation and development and facilitate the identification of potential intervention targets. We generated a single-cell transcriptomic atlas of E. granulosus and identified six major cell populations: Germinative cell, Muscle, Neuron, Storage, Tegument,and Cilium. Germinative cells were heterogeneous: some retained an undifferentiated transcriptional profile, whereas others expressed genes associated with differentiated cell types, consistent with the initiation of lineage-associated programs. RNA velocity analysis suggested four potential trajectories, one involving transitions within the undifferentiated population that may contribute to self-renewal and homeostasis, and three showing trends toward distinct lineage-associated subpopulations. A p53 homolog and threonine dehydrogenase tdh were identified as candidate developmental key genes that may contribute to larval growth and development. This study provides a single-cell atlas of E. granulosus protoscoleces and identifies key candidate genes involved in larval development, providing a foundation for future functional studies and the exploration of potential anthelmintic targets.

推荐理由：仅局限于小鼠新皮层神经元发育与功能研究，无跨物种比较或演化视角，与该研究者核心的跨物种细胞类型同源性和神经系统演化研究方向不符。
背景：哺乳动物新皮层中，皮质外端脑投射神经元（ET）是重要的输出神经元，其发育起源包括直接从放射状胶质细胞产生的ETd和经中间祖细胞间接产生的ETi。尽管已知这两种神经发生路径存在，但它们是否导致ET在分子特性、环路连接和行为功能上的系统性差异，此前缺乏系统性研究。理解这一问题的关键在于区分发育程序本身与转录组分类体系之间的关系。
研究方法：研究者采用遗传策略（利用特定Cre品系）分别标记ETd和ETi神经元群体；运用单细胞RNA测序技术在全异皮层范围内分析两种起源ET的转录组特征；结合行为学实验（reach-grasp-drink任务）和钙成像或活动成像技术，记录运动皮层中两类ET神经元的活动模式；通过对比分析效应基因家族表达谱和神经活动特征，建立神经发生起源与功能特化之间的联系。
研究结果：研究发现ETd和ETi均参与所有转录组定义的ET亚型，但在效应基因家族（涉及转录调控、神经元连接和生理学）的协同表达上存在显著差异。在抓握饮水任务中，运动皮层ETd形成小而异质、广泛调谐的活动原型池；ETi则分化为空间组织的多个亚群，分别追踪连续行为的不同阶段。这一发现表明神经发生起源构成了一个独立于现有转录组分类方案的发育性神经多样化轴。
研究意义：该研究揭示了神经发生起源作为神经多样性的独立组织原则，挑战了单纯基于转录组相似性进行神经元分类的框架，为理解发育程序如何塑造神经环路功能提供了新视角。然而，该工作完全集中于小鼠新皮层的固有次级特征，未涉及跨物种比较或演化分析，对理解神经系统演化的普适性机制贡献有限。
中文全摘要：皮质外端脑神经元（ET）由放射状胶质细胞直接神经发生（ETd）或经中间祖细胞间接产生（ETi），但其神经发生起源是否及如何贡献于ET多样性与功能尚不清楚。利用差异靶向ETd和ETi的遗传策略，作者在全异皮层分析其转录组，并在运动皮层成像其在抓握饮水任务中的活动。发现ETd和ETi虽贡献于所有转录组定义的ET亚型，但在塑造转录调控、神经元连接和生理的效应基因家族协同表达谱上显著分歧。功能上，运动皮层ETd形成小而异质的广泛调谐活动原型，ETi则包含追踪RGD连续动作的空间组织亚群。神经发生起源定义了垂直于现有转录组分类方案的神经多样化发育轴。
英文摘要（原文）：Cortical extratelencephalic neurons (ET) derive from either direct neurogenesis from radial glia cells (ETd) or indirect neurogenesis through intermediate progenitors (ETi), but whether and how neurogenic origin contributes to ET diversity and function remains unknown. Using genetic strategies that differentially target ETd and ETi, we profiled their transcriptomes across the isocortex and imaged their activity in motor cortex during a reach-grasp-drink (RGD) task. While both ETd and ETi contribute to all transcriptomically-defined ET subtypes, they diverge significantly in the concerted expression profile of effector-gene families that shape transcriptional regulation, neuronal connectivity and physiology. During the RGD task, motor cortex ETd formed a small yet heterogeneous pool of broadly tuned activity archetypes, while ETi included a diversified set of spatially organized subpopulations that tracked successive actions of RGD. 背景：全基因组加倍（WGD）通过多倍化在真核生物基因组演化中发挥重要作用。自体多倍化与异源多倍化不同，其重复染色体最初源自同一物种，保持高度序列相似性。WGD的主要后果之一是基因调控网络的重构，部分由基因表达剂量的选择驱动。在多细胞生物中，剂量平衡选择的证据主要依赖于重复基因保留和表达的比较模式，直接考察WGD后调控架构的研究较少。
研究方法：作者利用大西洋鲑（Salmo salar）的大规模eQTL数据集，该物种在约8000-1000万年前经历了鲑鱼特异性自体多倍化WGD。通过分析反式调控连接的保留模式、WGD衍生共线区域间的三维基因组接触频率，以及共享eQTL的重复基因对，来评估剂量平衡假说的预测。
研究结果：研究发现反式调控连接在WGD来源的共线区域间优先保留，且共线反式eQTL对表现出升高的三维基因组接触，提示重复染色体区域间空间邻近性的部分保留。总体而言，230对重复基因（5%）共享eQTL，表明调控控制的保守性。此外，16对基因显示由共同调控因子介导的基因型依赖性补偿表达效应，与剂量平衡假说的预测一致，这些基因对显著富集于后期再二倍化区域。
研究意义：结果表明，WGD后少数重复基因长期保留了剂量平衡的调控架构。该研究为理解WGD后调控网络的重塑机制提供了实证，但局限于单一脊椎动物物种的基因组加倍事件，缺乏跨物种比较视角，且未涉及神经系统或细胞类型演化的相关内容。

英文摘要（原文）：Whole-genome duplication (WGD) through polyploidization has played a major role in genome evolution across eukaryotes. While many well-studied WGDs involve allopolyploidization, autopolyploidization provides a distinct context because duplicated chromosomes initially originate from within the same species and retain high sequence similarity. A major consequence of WGD is the rewiring of gene regulatory networks, partly driven by selection on gene expression dosage. In multicellular organisms, evidence for dosage balance selection has relied largely on comparative patterns of duplicate gene retention and expression, with few studies directly examining regulatory architecture after WGD. Here, we analyzed a large-scale eQTL dataset from Atlantic salmon (Salmo salar), which experienced a salmonid-specific autopolyploid WGD approximately 80 to 100 million years ago. We found that trans-regulatory connections were preferentially retained between WGD-derived syntenic regions and that syntenic trans-eQTL pairs showed elevated three-dimensional genomic contact, suggesting partial preservation of spatial proximity between duplicated chromosomal regions. Overall, 230 duplicated gene pairs (5%) shared eQTLs, suggesting conserved regulatory control. Moreover, 16 gene pairs showed genotype-dependent compensatory expression effects mediated by a common regulator, consistent with predictions of the dosage balance hypothesis. These gene pairs were significantly enriched in late-rediploidizing regions. Our results indicate that a small subset of duplicated genes have retained dosage-balanced regulatory architecture long after WGD.

英文摘要（原文）：The ongoing global decline of coral reefs underscores the need to identify key mechanisms driving reef deterioration. The Flower Garden Banks (FGB) reefs in the northern Gulf are widely regarded as some of the healthiest in the continental United States, but recent disease outbreaks in the FGB have raised concerns about their long-term resilience. As human and agricultural expansion intensified across the Mississippi River basin, the river has become the dominant source of nutrients to the northern Gulf; however, the evolution of this influence is poorly understood. Here, we reconstruct nitrogen inputs to the FGB using annually resolved coral-bound nitrogen isotopes (CB-δ15N) from two coral cores that collectively span the period from 1753 to 2023. Our results show that anthropogenic nitrogen began to rise around 1850, coinciding with the early use of fertilizers, and increased sharply after the 1960s with the onset of the Green Revolution and the use of synthetic fertilizers, suggesting the Mississippi discharge as the source. Fractional endmember modeling further reveals that the accumulation and mobilization of legacy nitrogen stored in Midwestern soils and groundwater have amplified modern riverine fluxes, driving Mississippi-derived nitrate to more than 60 to 80% of the nitrogen reaching the FGB in recent decades. The highest riverine contributions of the past 270 years occurred in 2016 and 2022-2023, coinciding with disease outbreaks and thermal stress events. These findings suggest that the combined effects of unprecedented nutrient loading and extreme heat are key synergetic drivers of the recent deterioration in coral health observed for this historically resilient reef system.

DOI: 10.64898/2026.11.24.768995 · bioRxiv 链接: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.24.768995
命中关键词：brain, neuron, Seurat, Spatial transcriptomics
推荐理由：该论文为小鼠脑损伤模型的空间转录组学质量控制方法学研究，无演化或跨物种比较视角，且局限于小鼠单一物种，不符合该研究者核心关注点。
背景：基于成像的空间转录组学技术能在完整组织中获取单细胞分辨率的基因表达信息，为筛选不同实验条件下的细胞群体变化提供了强大工具。然而，在小规模研究中，观察到的细胞类型组成变化可能源于细胞注释错误而非真实生物学差异，且目前研究中很少报告单个细胞类型注释的可靠性，这限制了空间转录组学数据在差异分析中的可靠性。
研究方法：研究者开发了SLUCE工作流，基于R/Seurat框架，采用atlas-informed标记评分对每个细胞进行分类，并记录逐细胞的置信度边际和备选身份。随后应用三类诊断：区域特异性置信度阈值、边缘分配敏感性分析以及结合基因共检测、备选身份和空间位置的单基因驱动检查。该方法应用于6只小鼠（3只创伤性脑损伤，3只假手术）的海马和视束Xenium Prime 5K数据集。
研究结果：研究发现不同组织区域间置信度阈值存在近7倍差异（海马0.17 vs 视束0.026），表明固定阈值无法跨组织转移。驱动检查识别出基于边际过滤未能排除的错误注释群体，包括海马中被错误标注为脉络丛的Klotho表达神经元，以及视束中被错误标注为中性粒细胞的Csf3r表达髓系细胞。排除中性粒细胞标志物面板后，视束中组间差异方向改变的类别数从5个降至2个（其中一个是未分类类别），而主要胶质细胞群体在所有分析中保持稳定。
研究意义：SLUCE工作流能够区分稳健与脆弱的组成变化，帮助研究者在投入时间和组织进行实验验证前识别值得跟进的变化，为空间转录组学数据的质量控制提供了重要工具。然而，该研究完全集中于小鼠单一物种的病理模型，缺乏演化或跨物种比较视角，对理解脑演化、细胞类型同源性或神经系统起源等核心问题无直接贡献。
中文全摘要：成像空间转录组学平台如10x Xenium可在完整组织中实现单细胞分辨率转录组测量，但小样本研究中细胞类型组成变化可能源于注释错误而非真实生物学差异，且单个细胞注释的可靠性很少被报告。作者开发了SLUCE工作流，通过atlas-informed标记评分记录逐细胞置信度和备选身份，并结合区域特异性置信度阈值、边缘分配敏感性分析及单基因驱动检查三类诊断，在小鼠创伤性脑损伤模型中发现海马与视束间置信度阈值差异近7倍，并修正了Klotho神经元和Csf3r髓系细胞等错误注释群体，为空间组学数据的质量控制提供了标准化流程。
英文摘要（原文）：Imaging-based spatial transcriptomics platforms such as 10x Xenium measure thousands of genes in intact tissue at single-cell resolution, making them attractive for screening which cell populations change between experimental conditions. In small studies, however, apparent changes in cell-type composition can arise from misannotated cells rather than biology, and the reliability of individual cell-type calls is rarely reported. We present SLUICE (Screening Label Uncertainty to Identify Composition Effects), an R/Seurat workflow that classifies each cell by atlas-informed marker scoring and records a per-cell confidence margin and runner-up identity. Each group difference is then tested against three diagnostics: region-specific confidence thresholds, sensitivity analyses that reassign borderline calls, and single-gene driver checks that combine gene co-detection, runner-up identity, and spatial location. Applied to Xenium Prime 5K data from six mice (three traumatic brain injury, three sham) in two contrasting regions, the hippocampus and the optic tract, confidence thresholds differed almost sevenfold between regions (0.17 vs 0.026), showing that a fixed cutoff does not transfer between tissues. Driver checks identified mislabelled populations that margin-based filtering left in place, including Klotho (Kl)-expressing neurons called choroid plexus in the hippocampus and Csf3r-expressing myeloid cells called neutrophils in the optic tract. Excluding the neutrophil panel reduced the number of optic tract categories whose group difference changed direction from five to two (one of which was the Unclassified category), while the major glial populations were stable across all analyses. SLUICE separates robust from fragile composition changes, identifying which shifts merit experimental follow-up before time and tissue are committed to them. Code is available at https://github.com/rebeccasakko/hippocampus-xenium-analysis.
