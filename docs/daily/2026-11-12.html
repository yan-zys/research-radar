Tri-Modal Single-Cell Atlas of Developing Human Brain
发育中人脑的三模态单细胞图谱
为揭示表观遗传修饰在人脑发育中的调控作用，作者利用ME-seq技术对40例人类脑样本的34万余个细胞核进行基因表达、染色质可及性和DNA甲基化三模态联合分析，发现DNA甲基化在主要转录和染色质可及性转变后持续重塑，并与神经精神疾病遗传风险相关，但该研究仅局限于人类单一物种，缺乏跨物种比较视角。

推荐理由：该研究仅聚焦于人类单一物种的脑发育表观遗传学，无跨物种比较或演化视角，属于脊椎动物特有特征研究，与该研究者核心的跨物种演化比较方向不符。
背景：表观遗传修饰（如DNA甲基化）在发育和衰老过程中动态变化，为基因调控提供多样性，并与神经精神疾病病因相关。然而，这些调控层在人类脑发育过程中如何相互作用仍不明确。构建多模态单细胞图谱有助于揭示发育中的调控机制。
研究方法：研究者采用ME-seq技术，同时检测基因表达、染色质可及性和DNA甲基化三种模态。对40例人类脑标本（覆盖胎儿发育至青春期）的340,000余个单核进行分析，并构建模态特异性调控网络，整合神经精神病遗传风险数据。
研究结果：研究发现染色质可及性与DNA甲基化捕获部分重叠的顺式调控关联，但具有不同的发育轨迹和时间动力学：DNA甲基化在主要转录和染色质可及性转变后继续重塑。不同模态的发育时钟产生模态依赖的年龄估计，模态特异性调控网络汇聚于发育转录因子，甲基化定义的调控区域为神经精神病遗传风险提供互补注释。
研究意义：该研究建立了DNA甲基化作为发育调控的持续且互补维度的认识，将长期重塑与人类脑成熟和疾病易感性联系起来。然而，该工作仅局限于人类单一物种，缺乏跨物种比较或演化视角，对理解神经系统的演化起源和细胞类型同源性贡献有限。

英文摘要（原文）：Epigenetic modifications, such as DNA methylation, evolve throughout development and aging, providing remarkable regulatory diversity and contributing to disease etiology, including neuropsychiatric disorders. However, how these regulatory layers interact across human brain development remains unclear. Leveraging ME-seq, we simultaneously mapped gene expression, chromatin accessibility, and DNA methylation in over 340,000 single nuclei from 40 human brain specimens spanning fetal development to adolescence. Chromatin accessibility and DNA methylation captured partially overlapping cis-regulatory associations. Developmental trajectories revealed distinct temporal kinetics: DNA methylation continued remodeling beyond major transcriptional and accessibility transitions, while developmental clocks yielded modality-dependent age estimates. Modality-specific regulatory networks converged on developmental transcription factors, whereas methylation-defined regulatory regions provided complementary annotations of neuropsychiatric genetic risk. Together, these findings establish DNA methylation as a persistent and complementary dimension of developmental regulation, linking prolonged remodeling to human brain maturation and disease susceptibility.

Input-specific deployment of the active zone scaffold RIM tunes synaptic release probability and plasticity.

研究结果：RIM蛋白在不同突触类型中的功能存在显著差异：在相位性突触中RIM是传递必需的，而在紧张性突触中大部分非必需；RIM仅参与相位性突触的急性稳态增强，不参与紧张性突触的慢性增强；超分辨率成像显示RIM在相位性突触中与Cav2通道距离更近，并在可塑性过程中协调其纳米级空间重组以扩增可立即释放池。
研究意义：该研究揭示了RIM蛋白功能的突触特异性，挑战了RIM作为通用活性区组织者的传统观点，为理解突触异质性的分子机制提供了新视角，并提示不同突触可能采用差异化的分子策略来调控神经递质释放。
中文全摘要：RIM蛋白作为突触前活性区支架分子的功能具有输入特异性：在果蝇神经肌肉接点中，RIM对高释放概率的相位性突触传递至关重要，却对邻近的低释放概率紧张性突触非必需；这种特异性同样体现在可塑性调控中，RIM参与相位性突触的急性稳态增强但不参与紧张性突触的慢性增强。超分辨率成像显示RIM在相位性突触中更接近Cav2通道，并在急性可塑性期间协调其纳米级重排以快速扩增可立即释放池。
英文摘要（原文）：The multidomain scaffold RIM (Rab3-interacting molecule) is widely viewed as a broadly required organizer of the presynaptic active zone, clustering voltage-gated Ca2+ (Cav2) channels, priming synaptic vesicles, and supporting neurotransmitter release. Whether this reliance is uniform across synapses with distinct release probabilities, however, has been difficult to resolve at convergent inputs. Here, using neurotoxin-based silencing to isolate transmission at the Drosophila neuromuscular junction, we show that the requirement for RIM is highly input-specific: RIM is essential to empower transmission at a high-release probability phasic synapse, yet is largely dispensable at a neighboring low-release probability tonic synapse. This specificity extends to plasticity: RIM is required for acute homeostatic potentiation at the phasic input, but not for chronic potentiation at the tonic input. Super-resolution imaging reveals that RIM is positioned closer to Cav2 channels at phasic synapses and, during acute plasticity, coordinates their nanoscale reorganization to drive the rapid expansion of the readily releasable pool.

英文摘要（原文）：We evaluate deep survival analysis frameworks for patent licensing prediction, systematically comparing cure rate models against standard approaches and quantifying the benefit of temporal modeling over static classification to address unresolved methodological questions following recent applications of neural survival models to patent contexts. We conducted experimental validation on 624,129 United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) patents (2016-2017), comparing classical Cox regression, deep neural network baseline, DeepSurv (neural Cox proportional hazards), and Cox cure rate models. Performance was evaluated using Recall@K metrics across 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year prediction horizons, with statistical significance assessed through patent-level bootstrap resampling (B = 1,000). All deep learning approaches achieve high performance (Recall@10% 89.8-91.9%), substantially outperforming classical Cox regression (70.6-72.3%). Among neural architectures, DeepSurv-a standard neural Cox proportional hazards model-offers the optimal balance of simplicity and performance. More complex cure rate extensions provided no clear additional benefit over standard DeepSurv within the scope of this study: although bootstrap resampling detected a small but statistically significant difference (0.70 percentage points), this gap is practically negligible for patent screening, suggesting that explicit population heterogeneity modeling offers limited practical gains when deep feature learning is employed. Survival modeling provides statistically detectable but modest gains over static binary classification (Recall@10% differences of 0.5-1.6 percentage points), indicating that the primary value stems from capturing complex patent characteristics rather than explicit temporal modeling. This study provides, to the best of our knowledge, the first systematic evaluation of cure rate models and temporal modeling benefits for patent licensing prediction, demonstrating that architectural simplicity (DeepSurv) achieves near-optimal performance (Recall@10% 91%) when deep feature learning is prioritized. The findings offer actionable guidance for patent portfolio management: invest in feature learning infrastructure rather than architectural sophistication.

A leading model posits that procrastination reflects an imbalance between task aversiveness and the pursuit of positive task outcomes, yet this theoretical framework has neither been validated in real-world settings nor effectively applied to guide interventions. To address this gap, we conducted a double-blind, randomized, sham-controlled trial. Adults with chronic procrastination received seven sessions of high-definition transcranial direct current stimulation (HD-tDCS) to the left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC). Using the intensive experience sampling method, we assessed the effect of anodal HD-tDCS on real-world procrastination behavior at offline after-effect (2-day interval) and long-term after-effect (6-month follow-up).
