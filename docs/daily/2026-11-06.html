命中关键词：neural, ancestral, ATAC-seq, Regulatory evolution
推荐理由：该研究聚焦单一鱼类物种的胚胎发育染色质可及性，无跨物种比较视角，无单细胞组学，且未涉及神经系统细胞类型演化或同源性问题。
背景：基因组调控变化如何驱动适应性辐射是进化发育生物学的核心问题。马拉维湖慈鲷在不到一百万年内产生数百种物种，是研究此问题的理想系统。A. calliptera作为广义河流型谱系，可代表该辐射的祖先群体，为比较研究提供基线。
研究方法：研究者采用ATAC-seq技术对A. calliptera胚胎3、7和12天三个发育阶段进行全基因组染色质可及性分析，结合差异可及性分析、转录因子足迹分析和网络拓扑分析，构建调控网络并鉴定阶段特异性调控元件。
研究结果：研究鉴定到550,876-701,889个阶段特异性开放染色质峰，早期以染色质开放为主，后期出现特化性关闭。7天时期调控谱最广，12天时期神经和运动行为基因富集。TF-启动子网络围绕SOX2、FOXG1、PTF1A、SOX10等保守枢纽调控因子保持稳定拓扑结构。
研究意义：该研究建立了慈鲷首个胚胎表观基因组资源，揭示了发育染色质景观的动态变化规律，为理解调控进化如何塑造慈鲷多样化提供了比较框架，但缺乏跨物种比较视角，主要贡献限于单一物种发育表观遗传学。
中文全摘要：马拉维湖单倍型慈鲷是最快速的脊椎动物适应性辐射之一，但其调控区域如何塑造胚胎发育尚不清楚。作者对A. calliptera胚胎三个发育阶段进行ATAC-seq，发现染色质可及性随发育逐步特化：早期广泛开放，后期神经和运动行为相关基因富集。转录因子足迹分析显示发育重要TF具有阶段特异性调控活性，但TF-启动子网络拓扑稳定，符合沙漏模型。
英文摘要（原文）：Background Lake Malawi's haplochromine cichlids are one of the most rapid and species-rich vertebrate adaptive radiations known, generating hundreds of species within < 1 million years, yet how regulatory regions shape their embryonic development remains poorly understood. Astatotilapia calliptera, a generalist riverine lineage and proxy for the ancestral stock that seeded this radiation, offers a developmental chromatin baseline against which regulatory divergence in specialised lineages can be compared. Results We generated genome-wide ATAC-seq profiles of A. calliptera embryos across three developmental stages (3, 7 and 12 days post-fertilisation, dpf), identifying 550,876-701,889 accessible chromatin peaks per stage. Accessible chromatin was enriched at gene promoters and at both highly conserved and accelerated conserved noncoding elements at every stage. The functional repertoire of accessible-promoter genes shifted across development, from early patterning at 3 dpf to the broadest regulatory repertoire at 7 dpf, and an increasingly neural- and locomotory-behaviour-enriched repertoire by 12 dpf, coinciding with post-hatching motor maturation. Differential accessibility showed that chromatin opening, rather than closing, dominates the earliest transitions (3 vs 7 and 3 vs 12 dpf), driven mostly by general metabolic genes, whereas the smaller, later 7 vs 12 dpf transition combined targeted gains at tissue-remodelling and apoptosis genes with losses at genes governing early neural and retinal patterning; almost all of these losses (96%) were at promoters that had opened between 3 and 7 dpf, indicating transient rather than sustained opening. Transcription factor (TF) footprinting identified stage-specific regulatory activity among developmentally important TFs, while the broader TF-promoter network remained topologically stable across all three stages, built around conserved hub regulators of core neural and sensory patterning genes e.g., SOX2, FOXG1, PTF1A, SOX10. This suggests that wiring is established early and largely maintained, following the hourglass model, with developmental progression driven by changes in activity of this conserved programme. Conclusions Together, these findings establish the first embryonic epigenomic resource for A. calliptera, revealing a developmental chromatin landscape of broad early opening and progressive functional specialisation, and providing a comparative framework for understanding how regulatory evolution has shaped Lake Malawi cichlid diversification.

英文摘要（原文）：Current memristive circuits for biological decision-making only consider single-dimensional stimulus feedback and synaptic weight regulation without considering the influence of multiple factors interacting. This article designs a biological multibrain collaborative Fogg's behavior model (FBM) neural network circuit based on memristors and considers the influence of emotions on decision-making. The circuit includes the thalamus, dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, ventral tegmental area, hippocampus, amygdala, striatum system, and anterior cingulate cortex module. The circuit adopts a brain-like partitioned modular design, which provides a structural basis for its functional implementation, and it also has the advantages of low power consumption and high integration. PSpice simulations show that the integration of more brain region coordination mechanisms exhibits adaptive decision-making characteristics that are closer to the biological brain.

英文摘要（原文）：Pseudo-random number generation often requires trade-offs among quality, power consumption, and bandwidth to produce unpredictable sequences of numbers. The brain, on the other hand, efficiently generates unpredictable output complex network dynamics occurring in a high-dimensional state. This state, which is hypothesized to be chaotic, relies on the balance between excitation and inhibition. Here, we investigated if computational models of these chaotic balanced states can be harnessed for Neuromorphic Pseudo-Random Number Generators (NPRNGs) in low power hardware. We successfully constructed a balanced spiking neural network model consisting of leaky-integrate-and-fire neurons that could be readily implemented in low power FPGAs and used as a NPRNG. In both hardware and software instantiations, NPRNGs produce high-quality random numbers as validated by standard metrics for testing RNG quality.

背景：尼古丁作为广泛使用的精神活性物质，通过激活烟碱型乙酰胆碱受体影响中枢神经系统功能。既往关于尼古丁效应的分子研究主要集中于中脑-边缘多巴胺奖赏回路，且多采用 bulk 组织分析，缺乏对特定细胞类型响应及其空间组织特征的精细解析。嗅皮层作为胆碱能调节的重要区域，参与嗅觉联想编码，但其对急性尼古丁暴露的细胞特异性转录反应尚不明确。
研究方法：研究采用雄性C57BL/6小鼠，通过单次腹腔注射尼古丁（1 mg/kg）或生理盐水建立急性暴露模型。暴露24小时后取材，制备覆盖前嗅核、梨状皮层及相邻等皮层的冠状切片，使用Visium HD空间基因表达平台进行高分辨率空间转录组测序。采用基于细胞核分割的方法进行差异表达分析，结合通路富集分析解析分子程序，并通过空间邻近性分析评估神经元-胶质细胞相互作用。
研究结果：差异表达分析显示尼古丁响应主要集中于嗅兴奋性神经元，涉及钙离子处理、突触组装和细胞外基质相关通路，同时观察到即时早期基因Egr1的持续表达降低。空间定位发现梨状皮层浅层的锥体神经元连续群体特异性响应尼古丁，该群体特征性表达Cartpt和Penk，其突触组装、受体调节和蛋白聚糖相关程序显著上调，但细胞类型比例无显著变化。邻近分析揭示该神经元群体周围一个细胞直径范围内星形胶质细胞分泌蛋白hevin的编码基因Sparcl1表达降低。

Most molecular studies of its effects, however, have focused on reward circuitry and have used bulk tissue, which cannot show which cells respond or how responding cells are arranged relative to one another. We previously found that a single nicotine exposure activates hippocampal astrocytes through the astrocyte-expressed kinase AKT2. Here we asked which cells respond to nicotine in the anterior olfactory cortex, a cholinergic-modulated region that encodes odor associations, and whether their astrocytic surroundings change. Male mice received a single intraperitoneal injection of nicotine or saline, and coronal sections spanning the anterior olfactory nucleus, piriform cortex and adjacent isocortex were sequencing 24 h later using Visium HD. Differential expression on nucleus-based segmentation was concentrated in olfactory excitatory neurons. Pathways centered on calcium-handling, synapse-assembly and extracellular-matrix programs, together with a lasting reduction of the immediate-early gene Egr1. Within the piriform region, the response localized to a continuous population of superficial pyramidal neurons expressing Cartpt and Penk. Nicotine also reduced expression of Sparcl1, which encodes astrocyte-secreted synaptogenic protein hevin, within one cell diameter of these neurons. Our study shows that a single nicotine exposure leaves a spatially organized transcriptional program on olfactory circuitry, centered on one neuronal population and the tissue at its border. These results identify candidate cells, genes and tissue locations for testing how nicotine alters sensory cue circuits.

英文摘要（原文）：Lead (Pb) exposure remains a global health concern, particularly because of its adverse effects on neurodevelopment during fetal development and childhood. Early-life exposure to Pb during neuronal differentiation can impair neurodevelopment, although the underlying molecular mechanisms remain unclear. In this study, we investigated the cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying Pb-induced neurotoxicity during neural differentiation using SH-SY5Y cells. Our results demonstrated that Pb exposure caused severe injury to differentiating neural cells, leading to cell death with membrane disruption. These findings indicate that selenium protects differentiating neural cells against Pb-induced injury through mechanisms involving peroxide-reducing activity as well as additional selenium-dependent pathways. Collectively, our findings provide new insights into the molecular basis of Pb-induced neurotoxicity and support a protective role for selenium-dependent stress resistance against Pb-induced cellular injury.
