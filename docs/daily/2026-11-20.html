中文全摘要：全球约有1500万名HIV暴露未感染儿童（CHEU），其死亡、患病和发育迟缓风险更高。作者在津巴布韦农村SHINE试验的223名2岁儿童中（36名CHEU、187名CHU），测量身高、膝跟长、体重、围度、皮褶厚度及生物电阻抗，并用广义估计方程比较体成分差异。结果显示，CHEU的长度年龄别、体重年龄别、上臂围和小腿围更低，阻抗指数更低提示瘦体重减少，肱三头肌和肩胛下皮褶更薄提示皮下脂肪减少，且这一模式不能完全由低出生体重或生长迟缓解释。
英文摘要（原文）：Globally, 15 million children were born HIV-exposed uninfected (CHEU) from mothers living with HIV. CHEU have increased mortality, morbidity and stunting compared to children HIV-unexposed (CHU). Our objective was to compare body composition between CHEU and CHU, which may underlie this vulnerability. In a substudy among 223 children (36 CHEU and 187 CHU) enrolled in the Sanitation Hygiene Infant Nutrition Efficacy (SHINE) trial in rural Zimbabwe, we measured height, knee-heel length, weight, calf circumference, skinfold thicknesses (triceps, subscapular and calf), and bio-electrical impedance at 2 years of age. Generalised estimating equations were used to compare body composition between CHEU and CHU, and interactions with linear growth and birthweight. CHEU had significantly lower length-for-age, knee-heel length, weight-for-age, mid-upper arm circumference and calf circumference compared to CHU. CHEU had a lower impedance index (adjusted mean difference -0.49, 95% CI -0.87, -0.11), reflecting less lean mass, and lower skinfold thicknesses (subscapular Z-score adjusted mean difference -0.49 (95% CI -0.97, -0.02); triceps Z-score -0.48 (95% CI -0.83, -0.13)), indicating reduced subcutaneous fat. In early-life, CHEU had lower birthweight. However, no significant interaction between stunting or birthweight and HIV exposure was observed for body composition outcomes. CHEU have a distinct pattern of body composition by 2 years of age, characterised by reduced lean and fat mass compared to CHU which was not explained by reduced linear growth or lower birthweight. This pattern of early-life growth may contribute to the increased vulnerability of CHEU to mortality, morbidity and reduced neurodevelopment.

英文摘要（原文）：Systemic type I interferon (IFN-I) and pro-inflammatory cytokine responses are critical for limiting mosquito-borne RNA virus viral replication and disease, yet mechanisms initiating these responses and their linkage to viral tropism for lymphoid tissues are poorly understood. Here, we reveal that virus-cell interactions and specific host signaling pathways in lymphoid tissues are determinants of systemic cytokine induction. Using Venezuelan equine encephalitis virus (VEEV) mutants with defined lymph node (LN) tropism in mice, we show that VEEV LN infection positively associates with systemic IFN-I and other proinflammatory cytokine induction. Maximal responses require coordinated activation of multiple IRF7-dependent Toll-like receptor and RIG-I-like receptor pathways. Combining targeted cell depletion and Visium HD spatial transcriptomics viral gene quantitation, we uncover a spatiotemporal, biphasic innate immune response to VEEV infection, with initial cytokine induction driven by VEEV replication in dendritic cell and monocyte/macrophage subtypes within the LN subcapsular sinus. Surprisingly, active virus replication is curtailed in this LN compartment before 16hpi, when cytokine induction becomes dominated by non-VEEV-replicating bystander cells including plasmacytoid dendritic cells. These findings have direct implications for intervention against viral diseases characterized by lymphoid infection and subsequent spread to sites of terminal disease.

中文全摘要：髓鞘是脊椎动物神经系统的重要特征，但其中枢轴突功能仍不明确。作者利用雌雄小鼠皮层灰质中Layer 5锥体神经元细轴突的光学与电学记录，并结合计算建模，发现髓鞘可将动作电位传播的代谢成本降低约一半，而对传导速度影响很小。模型表明，更高速度和能效虽理论可达，却会损害复极以及结间电压门控通道和离子泵功能，提示皮层髓鞘偏向能量效率与离子稳态而非最大传导速度。
英文摘要（原文）：Myelin is a hallmark of vertebrate nervous systems, yet its roles in central axons remain elusive. Using optical and electrical recordings from thin axons of Layer 5 pyramidal neurons in murine cortical gray matter of animals of either sex, and computational modeling, we argue that myelination halves the metabolic cost of spike propagation with little effect on conduction velocity. Modeling indicates that, although greater speed and energy efficiency are theoretically possible, these would compromise repolarization and the function of internodal voltage-gated channels and pumps. We further suggest that, in contrast to peripheral axons, cortical myelin segregates current flow within periaxonal nanodomains. High-frequency currents, key for the rising phase of the action potential, traverse the myelin sheath to facilitate propagation, whereas low-frequency currents leak through paranodal junctions, supporting repolarization and ion homeostasis. These results suggest that cortical myelin adopts a structural trade-off that favors metabolic efficiency and ionic homeostasis over maximal velocity.

英文摘要（原文）：Sensory inputs are rich with temporal patterns that unfold across multiple timescales. Uncovering these regularities is essential for anticipating future events and navigating the environment efficiently. Numerous models have been proposed to account for learning at specific temporal scales; however, they are often designed in isolation and rely on narrowly tuned statistical measures, limiting their generalizability to other paradigms. In contrast, humans typically learn without prior knowledge of the underlying structure or the relevant timescale at which regularities occur. Here, we present a unifying account of statistical learning that spans a wide range of temporal dependencies, from adjacent and nonadjacent transitions to complex network structures. This model, long-horizon associative learning, offers a biologically grounded implementation of the successor representation, or equivalently, the free energy minimization model. Reanalyzing data from 11 previously published studies, we show that a single neural mechanism captures both local statistical regularities and higher-order structural properties. This mechanism rests on graded temporal overlap of associative traces and is governed by a single free parameter (β). This initial domain-general associative learning process, emerging from the graded structure of associations, may later scaffold to higher-level operations such as grouping, categorization, rule abstraction, and memory formation. Overall, this framework offers a conceptual synthesis that bridges disparate strands of the statistical learning literature and reframes apparent paradigm-specific effects as different expressions of a common underlying computation.

中文全摘要：为识别脓毒症中细胞周期相关诊断标志物并解析其作用，作者分析GEO转录组数据，将差异表达基因与细胞周期相关基因取交集，并结合机器学习筛选标志物，再进行GSEA、免疫浸润、药物预测及scRNA-seq整合分析。结果确定UPP1、DRAM1、GADD45A和MAPK14四个标志物，AUC均大于0.9；共有65条富集通路，14类免疫细胞浸润改变；scRNA-seq显示CD16+与CD14+单核细胞为关键细胞，且标志物沿拟时序分化上升。
英文摘要（原文）：Sepsis is a life-threatening organ dysfunction arising from a dysregulated host response to infection. Cell-cycle disturbance is increasingly recognized as a driver of sepsis-associated immune dysfunction. This study aimed to identify cell cycle-associated diagnostic biomarkers and clarify their roles in sepsis. Transcriptomic profiles from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) database were analyzed to identify candidate genes by overlapping differentially expressed genes (DEGs) between sepsis and control samples with cell cycle-related genes (CCRGs). Biomarkers were subsequently screened via machine learning algorithms, followed by expression level validation and receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curve analysis. Furthermore, gene set enrichment analysis (GSEA), immune infiltration analysis, and drug prediction were performed.Finally, single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) data were integrated for cell annotation and biomarker expression analysis, enabling the identification of key cells and the reconstruction of pseudotime trajectories. UPP1, DRAM1, GADD45A, and MAPK14 were selected as biomarkers and were significantly upregulated in sepsis samples (area under the curve (AUC) > 0.9). Additionally, GSEA revealed 65 pathways that were shared across all biomarkers, such as toll-like receptor signaling and antigen processing and presentation. Immune analysis revealed altered infiltration of 14 cell subsets in sepsis, including increased neutrophil numbers and decreased CD8+ T cell numbers. Drug prediction analysis identified 19 potential drugs, including doxorubicin hydrochloride and cisplatin with dual-targeting capacity. Finally, scRNA-seq confirmed CD16+ and CD14+ monocytes as key cells among the six cell types, with all biomarkers showing increasing expression trends during their differentiation. This study identified four cell cycle-associated biomarkers for sepsis and provided computational evidence linking them to sepsis-related pathways and monocyte differentiation. These findings may provide useful clues for future experimental validation and biomarker development.

中文全摘要：尖峰序列记录神经元放电模式，但其内在随机性与电极导致的错位会削弱模型在跨会话、跨个体场景中的泛化能力。作者提出概率神经表征Transformer（PNRT），将可变尖峰活动建模为一致的潜在概率分布，并设计基于活动的神经元重排序方法，使模型摆脱物理电极位置约束、缓解错位问题。在3个跨受试者数据集上，PNRT在神经一致性建模和行为解码方面均优于确定性基线，显示出更强的稳健统一表征能力。
英文摘要（原文）：Spike train data encapsulate precise information about neuronal firing patterns and serve as the primary modality for modeling neural dynamics. However, the variability of spike data impairs model ability to generalize across sessions and subjects. To address this gap, spike representation models are designed to extract unified neural manifolds from large-scale recordings. While recent attention-based models have demonstrated feasibility, they are constrained by deterministic architectures that are incapable of capturing the intrinsic stochasticity of neural activity and electrode-induced misalignment. To overcome these limitations, we propose the Probabilistic Neural Representation Transformer (PNRT), a framework that models variable spike activities into a consistent latent probabilistic distribution. It also implements an activity-based neuronal reordering method that decouples the model from physical electrode positions to mitigate misalignment. Validated on three cross-subject datasets, PNRT outperforms deterministic baselines in both neural consistency modeling and behavior decoding, demonstrating its capability to model unified neural representation and robustness across spike variability.

The transcription factor BCL11A restores differentiation potential to aged oligodendrocyte progenitor cells.

为揭示恢复衰老OPC分化潜能的关键因子，作者开发了gSWITCH计算流程以捕获动态基因表达模式并鉴定'开关'基因，发现Bcl11a在衰老OPC中表达降低且作为多开关基因的潜在主调控因子，证实Bcl11a过表达可促进衰老OPC向OL分化并在脊髓脱髓鞘损伤模型中增强髓鞘再生，同时发现Bcl11a在脊椎动物进化中受到广泛的纯化选择。

详情（作者 / 链接 / 命中关键词 / 推荐理由 / 背景 / 研究方法 / 研究结果 / 研究意义 / 中英文摘要）
作者：Ghosh T, Baror R, Zhao C, Sharma A, Au WH, Lakatos A, Goldman N, Franklin RJM
DOI: 10.7554/eLife.110038 · PubMed 链接: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42930359/
命中关键词：vertebrate evolution
推荐理由：该论文聚焦于小鼠OPC衰老与髓鞘再生的分子机制，虽提及Bcl11a的脊椎动物进化保守性，但缺乏跨物种比较、细胞类型演化或神经系统演化等核心视角，属于典型的哺乳动物衰老/再生医学研究，与研究者关注的两侧动物脑演化、细胞类型同源性及跨物种单细胞图谱等大尺度演化问题关联甚微。
背景：少突胶质细胞祖细胞（OPC）是中枢神经系统中持续存在的干细胞样细胞，在年轻动物中能够高效分化为髓鞘化少突胶质细胞（OL），但在衰老过程中其分化能力显著下降。这种年龄相关的功能衰退导致脱髓鞘损伤后的髓鞘再生障碍，以及与学习相关的适应性髓鞘形成受损。目前对于如何逆转衰老OPC的分化潜能衰退仍缺乏有效的分子干预靶点。
研究方法：研究者开发了名为gSWITCH的计算流程，用于捕获谱系进展和有序生物学转变中的动态基因表达模式，包括时间序列实验数据。利用该工具鉴定OPC特征性'开关'基因，并比较年轻与衰老OPC之间的表达差异。通过转录因子结合位点富集分析筛选潜在主调控因子，结合体外OPC分化实验和体内脊髓脱髓鞘损伤模型（使用溶血卵磷脂诱导），验证Bcl11a的功能。此外，通过跨物种序列比较分析Bcl11a的进化选择压力。
