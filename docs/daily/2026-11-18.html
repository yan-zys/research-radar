Transposons contribute to splice-isoform diversity in the Drosophila brain.
转座子促进果蝇脑部剪接异构体多样性
为回应Azad等人对转座子-基因嵌合剪接事件的质疑，作者基于改进的TEChim软件对果蝇脑部转座子外显化进行定量分析，证实内含子转座子插入可频繁被招募为可变外显子，其使用频率从罕见事件到近完全包含不等，并通过优化实验条件的RT-PCR验证了先前报道的7个位点，表明转座子剪接异构体是果蝇脑转录组可测量且具有生物学相关性的特征。

作者：Choucri M, Rainey T, Treiber CD
DOI: 10.7554/eLife.110625 · PubMed 链接: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43041067/
命中关键词：brain, neuron, neural, glia, transposable element, Drosophila
推荐理由：该研究虽涉及果蝇脑部转录本多样性，但聚焦于转座子剪接机制的技术验证与方法论争论，缺乏演化或跨物种比较视角，不符合研究者的核心关注点。
背景：mRNA异构体的细胞类型特异性表达是脑部复杂性的重要来源。此前研究表明内含子转座元件(TE)可剪接入神经转录本，扩展神经元和胶质细胞的剪接异构体库，但这一发现受到质疑，亟需系统验证与定量分析。
研究方法：基于改进的TEChim软件对果蝇脑部TE外显化进行定量分析；采用RT-PCR在优化实验条件下验证TE-基因剪接事件；与Azad等人的TIDAL分析流程进行方法学比较。
研究结果：内含子TE插入频繁被招募为可变外显子，外显子使用频率从罕见事件到近完全包含不等；在Azad等人测试的7个位点均检测到TE-基因剪接证据；识别出Azad等人研究中的实验与计算设计缺陷。
研究意义：证实TE剪接异构体是果蝇脑转录组中具有生物学相关性的可测量特征，而非罕见人为假象，为理解转座子对脑部分子复杂性和功能适应性的贡献提供了支持，但该研究局限于单一物种的技术验证层面。
中文全摘要：转座子(TE)可剪接入神经转录本并多样化神经元和胶质细胞的剪接异构体库。Azad等人近期研究未能重复该发现。本研究通过定量分析TE外显化，证实内含子TE插入频繁被招募为可变外显子，使用频率变异大；改进TEChim软件后，在Azad等人测试的7个位点中均获得TE-基因剪接的清晰证据，并指出其实验与计算设计缺陷，证明在适当条件下TE-基因剪接可被RT-PCR检测。
英文摘要（原文）：The extraordinary complexity of the brain depends in part on the vast diversity of mRNA isoforms it expresses, often in a cell-type specific manner. In a recent study, we found that intronic transposable elements (TEs) are spliced into neural transcripts and diversify the splice isoform repertoire of neurons and glia (Treiber and Waddell, 2020). A recent paper by Azad et al. revisits these findings using their TIDAL analysis pipeline applied to our published data (Azad et al., 2024). Their analysis did not find any of the splicing reads we reported and although they used RT-PCR to test seven of the 264 TE-gene pairs we had previously reported, they failed to validate TE-gene splicing in any of them. Here, we conduct a quantitative analysis of TE exonisation and show that intronic TE insertions are frequently recruited as alternative exons, with exon usage ranging from rare events to near-complete inclusion in transcripts. We implement this analysis in an improved version of our TEChim software and present clear support for TE-gene splicing at the seven loci tested by Azad et al. We also identify methodological issues in the experimental and computational design of the Azad et al. study that likely explains their failure to detect TE-gene chimeras, while demonstrating that TE-gene splicing can be detected by RT-PCR under appropriate experimental conditions. Together, our data demonstrates that TE splice isoforms are not rare artefacts but measurable and biologically relevant features of the Drosophila brain transcriptome that may contribute to the molecular complexity and functional adaptability of the brain.

A radial glial regulatory program bridges progenitor maintenance and neuronal identity
放射状胶质细胞的调控程序连接祖细胞维持与神经元身份
为揭示早期神经祖细胞的分子差异如何影响其神经元产出，作者结合转录组与染色质图谱分析及体内扰动实验，鉴定了SOX9高表达放射状胶质细胞（S9RGs）中连接祖细胞维持与神经元身份的分子程序，发现Notch-SOX9轴调控祖细胞特性并影响上层神经元命运决定，为理解皮层神经发生中祖细胞多样性的起源提供了机制性见解。

作者：Fabra-Beser, J.; Planells, J.; Tran, L. L.; Turskan, D.; Marin-Garnes, A.; Veintimilla-Escot, L.; Mateos-Martinez, C. M.; Gibson, T. J.; Franco, S. J.; Gil-Sanz, C.

命中关键词：neurogenesis, neuron, glia
推荐理由：仅聚焦小鼠大脑皮层发育，无跨物种比较或演化视角，属于典型的模式生物发育神经生物学研究。
背景：大脑皮层的神经元多样性源于连续的神经发生过程。早期神经祖细胞群体内部的分子异质性如何转化为不同的神经元产出，是发育神经科学中的核心问题。放射状胶质细胞（RG）作为皮层主要的神经祖细胞，其多样性是否与神经元类型的产生存在因果关系，以及这种多样性是否在物种间具有保守性或特异性，尚待阐明。
研究方法：研究者结合单细胞/群体转录组测序（RNA-seq）、染色质可及性分析（ATAC-seq）以及体内基因扰动（包括Sox9条件性敲除和Notch通路激活），在小鼠胚胎皮层发育过程中对S9RGs进行分子特征刻画；并通过跨物种比较分析，将小鼠S9RGs的分子特征与雪貂高增殖RG及人皮层类器官外层RG进行比对。
研究结果：研究鉴定了S9RGs特有的分子程序，涵盖细胞外信号、细胞周期、祖细胞维持和神经发生相关基因；SOX9直接调控该程序的部分组分，而Notch信号位于Sox9上游并能重现S9RGs的多项转录和染色质特征。S9RGs在染色质可及性层面表现出与后期RG不同的模式，尤其在与祖细胞维持和神经元身份相关区域。Notch激活可部分重现这些变化，包括影响上下层神经元命运决定相关基因的差异。跨物种比较显示，尽管空间位置不同，S9RGs与雪貂高增殖RG及人oRGs存在分子特征重叠。
研究意义：该研究提出了早期放射状胶质细胞的分子差异可同时影响其增殖持续时间和后续神经元身份的新机制，为理解皮层神经发生中祖细胞多样性的产生提供了框架。然而，该工作完全局限于小鼠单一模式生物的发育过程，未涉及跨物种演化比较，也未探讨这些分子程序在更广泛物种中的保守性或变异性，因此对研究两侧动物神经系统演化的直接参考价值有限。
中文全摘要：大脑皮层通过连续的神经发生波次获得神经元多样性。早期祖细胞间的分子差异如何与其神经元产出相关联尚不清楚。本研究鉴定了早期SOX9高表达放射状胶质细胞（S9RGs）中的分子程序，该程序将祖细胞维持与神经元身份调控基因相连接。通过结合转录组和染色质分析以及体内扰动实验，作者发现涉及细胞外信号、细胞周期调控、祖细胞维持和神经发生的程序；SOX9调控该程序的部分内容，Notch则作用于Sox9上游。S9RGs表达部分后期RG特征，并在祖细胞维持、神经发生和神经元身份相关基因上呈现独特的染色质可及性模式。值得注意的是，偏好产生上层神经元的S9RGs与雪貂高增殖性RG群体及人皮层类器官的外层RG存在分子特征共享。
英文摘要（原文）：The cerebral cortex acquires its neuronal diversity through successive waves of neurogenesis. How molecular differences among early progenitors relate to their neuronal output remains unclear. Here, we identify a molecular program in early SOX9-high radial glia (S9RGs) that links progenitor maintenance with the regulation of genes associated with neuronal identity. Combining transcriptional and chromatin profiling with in vivo perturbations, we identify a program involving extracellular signaling, cell-cycle regulation, progenitor maintenance, and neurogenesis. We show that SOX9 regulates part of this program, including receptors that contribute to progenitor maintenance, while Notch acts upstream of Sox9 and reproduces several transcriptional and chromatin features of S9RGs. S9RGs express selected later-stage radial glial features and show distinct chromatin accessibility patterns at genes involved in progenitor maintenance, neurogenesis, and neuronal identity. Notch activation reproduces part of these changes, including differences at genes associated with lower- and upper-layer neuronal specification. Interestingly, S9RGs, which preferentially generate upper-layer neurons, share molecular features with the highly proliferative RG population in ferrets and with human outer RGs (oRGs) from cortical organoids, despite their different positions within the developing cortex. Together, our findings suggest that molecular differences among early radial glia could influence both how long they remain as progenitors and the neuronal identities they subsequently generate.

英文摘要（原文）：Mitochondria drive cellular reprogramming by integrating metabolism and signaling. In macrophages, mitochondria are central to immunometabolic responses to external cues, but the extent to which they are remodeled and participate in macrophage reprogramming remains unclear. Here, we integrate transcriptomics with whole-cell and purified mitochondrial proteomics to profile lipopolysaccharide (LPS)/interferon-γ (IFN-γ)- and interleukin-4 (IL-4)/IL-13-stimulated macrophages. Together, we identify mtDNA expression, intramitochondrial translation, and mitochondrial membrane potential as critical, drug-sensitive determinants of the IL-4/IL-13 response.

英文摘要（原文）：Ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate carboxylase/oxygenase (rubisco) is an important but challenging protein engineering target. Fast and selective rubiscos could enhance photosynthesis in plants and accelerate biobased production processes. To facilitate engineering of rubisco, we applied an in vivo screen that couples rubisco activity to growth rate of the photoautotrophic cyanobacterium Synechocystis sp. PCC 6803. We screened a barcoded mutagenesis library of the form II rubisco from Gallionella sp. containing 15,000 single-site and multi-site variants. Exchanges in loop 6 near the active site, at the dimer interface, and in potential gas tunnels improved rubisco fitness. This large labeled fitness dataset, containing examples of epistasis, can be useful for benchmarking computational models of rubisco.

Uncoupling GDT is promising for breeding of elite cultivars with strong growth and immunity. We previously identified that AP2 transcription factor TARGET OF EARLY3 (TOE3) promotes both growth and antiviral defense. However, the mechanism underlying this GDT uncoupling remains unknown. Here, we find that the amino-terminal domain of TOE3 (T3N) inhibits abscisic acid (ABA) signaling. Mechanistically, T3N binds to an ABA receptor PYL4 to interfere with PYL4-PP2C4 interaction. These findings reveal how TOE3 uncouples GDT to provide strategies for breeding crops with strong growth and antiviral immunity.

英文摘要（原文）：Multiomic profiling provides a comprehensive physiological overview at the molecular level, but understanding of its spatiotemporal dynamics remains limited in human populations. We profiled longitudinal whole-blood gene expression and metabolite levels in 335 females over 8 years. Longitudinally variable genes showed cell type specificity and enrichment for aging-relevant pathways, including cardiometabolic and neurodegenerative disorders. Integrative analyses revealed extensive static and time-variable cross-omic connectivity.
