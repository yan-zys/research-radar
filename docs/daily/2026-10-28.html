背景：转录因子foxQ2II在多数动物前部神经外胚层图式形成中保守，但已在脊椎动物中丢失。尽管已知该因子参与早期神经发育，但其在成体脑中的功能及所标记神经元的特性长期不明。昆虫蕈形体和中心复合体作为高级整合中枢，其神经元类型的分子标记与同源性研究对理解泛甲壳动物乃至两侧动物神经系统演化具有重要意义。
研究方法：作者利用赤拟谷盗增强子陷阱品系标记foxQ2II表达细胞，结合免疫组织化学和新建立的甲虫脑bow（brainbow）谱系示踪系统，对Tc-foxQ2II阳性神经元进行形态学分类和神经递质表型鉴定；通过比较神经解剖学方法将甲虫多巴胺能神经元簇与黑腹果蝇已知神经元簇进行同源推断。
研究结果：Tc-foxQ2II阳性神经元可被分为9个具有不同投射模式的簇，均定位于蕈形体和/或中心复合体及其相关神经纤维网。所有簇均含有乙酰胆碱能和谷氨酸能的快速激活神经元，未见GABA能或血清素能神经元。鉴定出多个多巴胺能神经元群为Tc-foxQ2II阳性，并将其与果蝇的PPL2c、PPM1和PPL1簇神经元进行同源匹配。
研究意义：该研究将foxQ2基因的功能认知从早期感觉/神经分泌细胞规格化拓展至成体高级脑中枢的中间神经元标记，为昆虫神经系统的细胞类型分子标记提供了新工具。然而，该工作缺乏多物种比较框架和组学层面的系统分析，对理解foxQ2在更广泛演化尺度上的功能保守性与创新性贡献有限。
中文全摘要：foxQ2II转录因子在多数动物前部神经外胚层图式形成中保守，但在脊椎动物中丢失，其在成脑中的功能尚不清楚。作者利用增强子陷阱、免疫染色和新建立的甲虫脑bow系统，将赤拟谷盗Tc-foxQ2II阳性神经元分为9个投射模式不同的簇，发现所有簇均含快速激活的乙酰胆碱和谷氨酸能神经元，无GABA能或血清素能神经元；多数多巴胺能神经元为Tc-foxQ2II阳性，并与果蝇PPL2c、PPM1、PPL1簇神经元同源，提示foxQ2标记参与高级脑中枢的快速反应中间神经元。
英文摘要（原文）：The brain is one of the most complex animal organs, and the development of the many different neuron types remains enigmatic. A set of brain-specific transcription factors is involved in brain patterning, but their specific contributions mostly remain to be elucidated, including foxQ2II. This transcription factor is conserved in anterior neuroectodermal patterning of most animals, while it has been lost from vertebrates. The contribution of foxQ2II-positive neurons to the adult brain has remained enigmatic. Here, we use our enhancer trap, immunostainings, and our newly established beetle brainbow system to categorize Tc-foxQ2II-positive neurons into nine clusters with different projection patterns. All clusters contain neurons with the fast-activating neurotransmitters acetylcholine and glutamate, while no Tc-foxQ2II-positive neuron is GABA-ergic or serotonin-positive. Interestingly, we found that many dopaminergic neurons were Tc-foxQ2II positive and homologize them with dopaminergic neurons of the PPL2c, PPM1, and PPL1 cluster described in the Drosophila brain. Our results show that Tc-foxQ2II marks subsets of fast-acting interneurons contributing to the higher-order brain centers mushroom bodies and central complex. Taken together, our work expands the known functional range of foxQ2 genes from sensory and neurosecretory cell specification to interneurons involved in the function of higher-order brain centers.

英文摘要（原文）：SOX2 is a transcription factor and central regulator of neural progenitor identity, yet the downstream molecular networks through which it supports human brain development remain incompletely characterized. Here, we applied CRISPR-lineage tracing at cellular resolution in heterogeneous tissue (CRISPR-LICHT) to human cerebral organoids to functionally interrogate SOX2-dependent regulatory networks. Using a SOX2-focused library targeting SOX2 together with conserved neurodevelopmental regulators expressed in neural progenitor cells, we showed that CRISPR-LICHT detects reproducible lineage- depletion phenotypes across genes already known to be involved in progenitor maintenance, fate specification, glial differentiation and neuronal maturation. This extends the application of CRISPR-LICHT beyond the severe neural progenitor phenotypes previously characterized for microcephaly-associated genes and demonstrates its ability to detect perturbation effects across a broader set of neurodevelopmental regulators, involving SOX2 targets previously defined in mouse and now open to further understanding in a human model setting. We further screened 120 evolutionarily conserved, poorly characterized SOX2-dependent genes classified as Tdark within the Illuminating the Druggable Genome framework and identified 28 candidate hits whose disruption impaired neural progenitor lineage expansion and/or survival. Individual downregulation of CDC42EP4, a poorly characterized CDC42 effector linked to cytoskeletal organization, revealed altered distribution of targeted progenitors between ventricular- and subventricular- like compartments, with changes consistent with altered progenitor-state progression. Together, these findings expand the functional landscape of SOX2-dependent regulation in human neural progenitors and identify cellular architecture as a potential component of SOX2-associated developmental programs.

英文摘要（原文）：Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) can provide new insights into the regulation of developmental processes by revealing unknown or overlooked genes. We identified a novel gene called beer belly (bebe) as a marker of a segment addition zone (SAZ) associated cell cluster in a single-cell atlas of the spider Parasteatoda tepidariorum. Here we show that bebe is an ancient toolkit gene encoding a previously uncharacterised protein predicted to be secreted, which likely plays an important role in the development of most animal phyla. However, we could not identify a bebe ortholog in any mammal or holometabolous insect, suggesting it has been lost in these lineages and explaining why it has been overlooked to date. Knock-down of bebe expression in P. tepidariorum demonstrates that it regulates SAZ size and consequently segment size by interacting with Wnt8. Our findings also suggest that bebe may regulate segmentation in other invertebrates and play additional developmental roles in bilaterian and non-bilaterian animals, likely ancestrally, through modulation of Wnt signalling. This study emphasises the promise of applying single-cell sequencing across a diverse range of species to better understand the regulation of development beyond candidate genes from traditional models, allowing us to better characterise the animal developmental toolkit and its evolution.

英文摘要（原文）：Deep learning architectures comprise hierarchies in modern machine learning studies, and these are not only full of semantic depth; they are also structured systematically by a sequence of nonlinear transformations. In particular, in convolutional neural networks, the activation functions are the canonical nonlinear construction blocks that control the expressiveness of the model, gradient flow in the process of back-propagation, and hierarchical feature abstraction. The canonical activation functions, which are the rectified linear unit (ReLU), the sigmoid, and the hyperbolic tangent (tanh) have non-adaptive and non-dynamic operation properties. As a result, they limit the capacity of a network to learn the complex nonlinear dynamics of a variety of data distributions and maximize the multi-level dependencies that occur among hierarchical layers. To overcome the above constraints, the current proposal proposes the implementation of Symmetric Modifiable Slope Activation (SMSA), which is a method that enables the dynamically calibrated neural layers through simultaneous modification of the slope, rang and the curvature of the activation space. SMSA is implemented in three different forms, first is slope parameter (n) is defined manually, a globally adjustable one with a learnable coefficient β, and (iii) a layer-wise adaptive regime allowing each convolutional layer to learn its coefficient β). The strategies listed above were evaluated on the benchmark datasets MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, HAM10000 and COVID-19, using both lightweight and conventional convolutional neural networks architectures, including Custom-CNN, SBNet-CNN, ResNet18, VGG16 and DenseNet121. The empirical findings also support the idea that SMSA(x, n) is more suitable to increase convergence rates and to improve the learning of discriminative features in data-specific regimes, whereas SMSA(x, β) its adaptive counterpart is more suitable to provide better optimization stability, faster convergence, and better predictive performance. Indeed, using layer-wise SMSA in the SBNet- CNN model on the Fashion-MNIST data, the test accuracy of 97.52% was reached, which is, in fact, better than what ResNet18(94.18%) and VGG16(94.13%) had done. In addition, the layer-wise adaptation provided more robustness with imbalanced classes by achieving a classification test accuracy of 98.59% in HAM10000 and having a 4% positive change in the accuracy of COVID-19 classification with DenseNet121. These findings support the importance of the adaptive activation mechanisms which have a critical influence on the regulation of gradient dynamics, the development of nonlinear representations, and the process of generalization. Future studies will focus on generalizing SMSA to graph-based neural networks, transformer models, and self-supervised paradigms, which is complemented by a comprehensive theoretical analysis of gradient flow and representation geometry, which will eventually bring the state of the next-generation neural network design.
