To address these issues, we propose scGFormer (Single-cell Multi-scale Graph Transformer), a unified framework that integrates: (i) Performer-based Global Attention (PGA) to capture long-range dependencies, (ii) Graph-based Local Attention (GLA) to model neighborhood structures, and (iii) a Squeeze-and-Excitation Gene Reweighting module (GeneSE) to enhance gene-level representations. 对研究者而言，这项工作提示可从跨脑区输入、祖细胞微环境和黏附分子信号三个层面重新审视皮层发育问题。方法上，融合类器官结合单核RNA测序适合用于追踪细胞来源、连接建立与命运变化；应用上，可进一步用于神经发育疾病中丘脑-皮层通讯异常的机制研究。
