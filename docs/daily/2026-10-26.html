推荐理由：该论文为纯人类婴儿发育神经影像学研究，无演化或跨物种比较视角，与研究者核心方向完全无关。
中文全摘要：现有神经影像研究多依赖严格控制范式，掩盖了驱动发育的脑-行为动态。本研究建立自然主义fNIRS范式，通过连续视频编码婴儿自发行为并结合FIR建模，解析reach-to-grasp、hand-to-mouth及发声行为的特异性血氧反应。结果显示：6-13月龄期间，reach-to-grasp和发声频率增加，三种行为时长缩短；发育中皮层激活呈行为特异性——reach-to-grasp时双侧后顶叶活动随年龄降低，而hand-to-mouth和发声时感觉运动与前运动区活动随年龄增强。皮层活动与行为频率、时长的个体差异相关，提示皮层募集与行为变异耦合。该框架将发育神经影像扩展至自然交互情境。
英文摘要（原文）：A central premise of developmental neuroscience is that mature behaviour emerges through reciprocal interactions between infants spontaneous actions, neural activity, and environmental experience. Yet, much human neuroimaging research relies on highly controlled, repetitive paradigms designed to minimize behavioural variability, obscuring the very brain-behaviour dynamics that drive developmental change. Here, we introduce a naturalistic functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) paradigm that exploits, rather than suppresses, natural variability in infants behaviour to investigate cortical changes associated with behavioural development. Continuous video coding of spontaneous infant behaviour was combined with finite impulse response (FIR) modelling to estimate behaviour-specific hemodynamic responses associated with reach-to-grasp, hand-to-mouth, and vocalization behaviours while accounting for temporally overlapping hemodynamic responses by treating extraneous behaviours as nuisance regressors. From 6 to 13 months of age, reach-to-grasp and vocalizations increased in frequency, while all three behaviours shortened in duration. Critically, developmental changes in cortical activation were behaviour-specific. During reach-to-grasp movements, bilateral posterior parietal activity decreased with age. In contrast, during hand-to-mouth and vocalizations, somatomotor and premotor activity increased with age. Cortical activity was also related to individual variation in the frequency and duration of these behaviours, indicating that cortical recruitment remained coupled to behavioural variation independent of age. These findings reveal behaviour-specific patterns of cortical reorganisation during infant development and establish a methodological framework for linking spontaneous behaviour to cortical activity in freely behaving infants. More broadly, this approach enables direct investigation of cortical development as it unfolds through naturalistic interactions among behaviour, neural activity, and experience. Significance StatementContinuous self-generated interactions with the environment play a critical role in driving infant brain development, yet neuroimaging research often examines infants while sleeping or in highly constrained and repetitive tasks. Here, we show that spontaneous infant behaviour can instead be leveraged to provide the temporal variability needed to resolve behaviour-specific cortical responses in freely behaving infants. By combining continuous behavioural coding with data-driven finite impulse response (FIR) modelling of overlapping hemodynamic responses, we identified behaviour-specific developmental differences in cortical recruitment without tightly constraining the infants behaviour. This novel approach expands developmental neuroimaging beyond sleeping or trial-based paradigms and demonstrates how naturally occurring behavioural variability can become a useful feature of fNIRS experimental design. More broadly, it generates new opportunities to directly test theories of neurobehavioural development in more ecologically valid settings.

英文摘要（原文）：The brown marmorated stink bug, Halyomorpha halys (BMSB), is an invasive polyphagous pest causing major losses in fruit and field crops in Europe and the Americas. With insecticide options increasingly limited by resistance and regulatory restrictions, RNA interference (RNAi) offers a species-specific alternative. As RNAi is fully functional in BMSB following hemolymph injection, we evaluated neuronal and neuron-associated genes as systemic RNAi targets using dsRNA microinjection. A literature screen of >80 insect RNAi targets identified 12 neuronal/synaptic genes, from which five were selected in BMSB: acetylcholinesterase (HhAChE), alpha-soluble NSF attachment protein (HhAsnap), Shaker (HhSh), tyrosine hydroxylase (HhTh) and Ras opposite protein (HhRop). Previously published transcriptomic data showed HhAChE, HhSh and HhTh were enriched in brain/central nervous system, whereas HhAsnap and HhRop were broadly expressed at higher abundance. Adults injected with dsRNA (334-364 bp; 2 μL, 1000 ng/μL) targeting HhAsnap or HhRop showed strong mortality: 96.8% (30/31) and 100% (34/34) by Day (D)13, respectively (log-rank, P < 0.0001). Feeding assays showed >60% reductions in salivary sheath formation from D4 to D8 after dsAsnap or dsRop treatment. Quantitative real-time (qRT)-PCR at 72 h confirmed ~58% knockdown of HhRop, whereas HhAChE and HhAsnap showed no significant whole-body depletion. Broadly and highly expressed targets (HhAsnap, HhRop) were associated with strong mortality and feeding suppression, whereas brain-enriched targets did not. Expression breadth and transcript abundance appear critical for RNAi responsiveness in BMSB, identifying HhAsnap and HhRop as promising candidates for oral or spray-based RNAi control. © 2026 The Author(s). 英文摘要（原文）：Drosophila ventral nervous system defective (vnd/NK2.2) encodes a homeodomain (HD) transcription factor essential for establishing neuroblast (NB) identity in the ventral nerve cord. However, its direct downstream targets during embryonic neurogenesis remain largely unknown. This study aimed to identify target genes directly regulated by Vnd and elucidate the molecular mechanisms underlying their regulation during the formation of embryonic NBs. Expression profiles and embryonic phenotypes were analyzed in either vnd6/Y or dpn1/dpn1 single mutants and vnd6/Y; dpn1/dpn1 double mutants using immunofluorescence. Direct binding of the Vnd HD to the deadpan (dpn) promoter was assessed via EMSA, and functional binding sites were validated using dual-luciferase reporter assays in S2 cells. The dosage-dependent relationship between vnd and dpn was characterized in primary neural cultures using real-time PCR and quantitative immunofluorescence. Both vnd6/Y and dpn1/dpn1 single mutants exhibited similar developmental defects, including NB mis-specification and central/peripheral nervous system disorganization. Consistently, vnd6/Y; dpn1/dpn1 double mutants displayed severely aggravated, overlapping neurogenic phenotypes, suggesting their functional convergence in a combinatorial regulatory mechanism. 背景：细胞间通讯是组织功能协调的核心机制，单细胞转录组学的发展使得系统解析细胞间信号传递成为可能。然而，现有细胞通讯推断方法（如CellPhoneDB、NicheNet等）大多基于细胞类型水平的平均表达，忽略了细胞间异质性，可能掩盖关键通讯事件或引入系统性偏倚。开发能够在单细胞分辨率上精确推断通讯事件的方法，对于深入理解组织微环境动态具有重要意义。
研究方法：作者开发了HyperCom，一种基于超图的细胞通讯推断框架。该方法从已知配体-受体（LR）相互作用数据库出发，结合单细胞表达数据构建超图，通过图扩散算法计算每个细胞作为信号发送者或接收者的概率得分，最终生成细胞类型无关的通讯强度排序。方法支持全数据集或条件感知两种分析模式，并以R包形式实现。
研究结果：HyperCom在多种数据集上进行了验证，包括基于物理细胞接触的实验数据、空间转录组数据以及多条件单细胞数据集。结果表明，HyperCom能够捕捉传统细胞类型聚合方法无法检测的细微细胞级通讯信息，具有物种无关性、可扩展性和灵活性，适用于不同实验设计和生物学场景。
研究意义：HyperCom为单细胞水平的细胞通讯研究提供了新的计算工具，其细胞类型无关的分析框架有助于减少聚合偏倚，更精细地解析组织微环境中的通讯网络。然而，该方法目前主要应用于一般性细胞生物学问题，尚未与演化生物学、跨物种比较或神经系统研究建立直接联系。
中文全摘要：细胞间通讯是跨条件动态改变细胞状态的基础，但现有方法通常按细胞类型注释聚合信号，可能引入偏倚。作者开发HyperCom，基于已知配体-受体相互作用和单细胞表达数据构建超图，通过图扩散为每个细胞在发送者-接收者连续谱上打分，并生成细胞类型无关的LR对排名。验证显示该方法能捕捉细微的细胞级通讯信息，已作为开源R包发布。
英文摘要（原文）：Cell-cell communication underpins intercellular responses allowing cells to dynamically change states across conditions; however current methods typically aggregate signals by cell type annotations which may introduce biases. We developed HyperCom, a hypergraph-based method to infer cell-resolved cell-cell communication from single cell transcriptomics data. HyperCom constructs a hypergraph from known ligand-receptor interactions and the expression of those ligands and receptors in individual cells and performs graph diffusion on the hypergraph to score cells on a continuous spectrum from sender to receiver for each LR pair. HyperCom then generates a cell-type agnostic ranking of LR pairs by communication either over the whole dataset or in a condition-aware manner. Validation of HyperCom on physical cell-to-cell contact, spatial, and multicondition datasets highlight its flexibility to capture subtle per cell communication information. HyperCom is available as a species-agnostic, scalable, and open source R package: https://github.com/pawelab/HyperCom.
