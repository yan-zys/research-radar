Locus-resolved expression and manipulation of human-specific transposable elements reveal their role in timely progression of early brain development

作者：Deelen, L.; Marlow, S. A.; Urbanaviciute, P.; Richards, T. T.; Mitchell, Z. H.; Demurtas, M.; Koulle, A.; Li, X.; Bowles, K. R.; Berrens, R. V.; Trizzino, M.

命中关键词：brain evolution, brain development, neurogenesis, brain, neuron, glia, transposable element
推荐理由：该论文仅聚焦于人类特异性转座元件在早期皮层发育中的调控作用，局限于人类/灵长类特有特征，无跨物种比较或演化视角，不符合该研究者核心的跨物种细胞类型同源比较与神经演化方向。
背景：人类大脑皮层的扩张是灵长类演化的显著特征，但其背后的调控机制尚不清楚。转座元件作为基因组的重要组成部分，尤其在人类谱系中提供了大量调控新颖性，然而由于技术限制，个体TE位点的功能研究长期受阻。近年来，单细胞长读长测序技术的发展为解析重复序列的位点特异性表达提供了可能。
研究方法：研究者采用单细胞长读长测序技术CELLO-seq，对皮层类器官进行转座元件位点解析表达分析；运用CRISPRi技术特异性沉默SVA和LTR5Hs元件；结合共表达网络和 regulon 分析，以及 motif-to-regulon 汇聚分析，将染色质可及性与转录因子结合程序的变化直接关联。
研究结果：研究发现年轻人类特异性TEs在皮层类器官的所有主要细胞类型中表达，并携带关键神经发生转录因子的结合基序。CRISPRi沉默SVA和LTR5Hs导致放射状胶质细胞向神经元转变的协调失败，表现为前体细胞滞留、转录向腹侧化偏移、突触成熟受损以及中间神经元前体的分化阻滞。分子层面，TE沉默导致背侧皮层身份程序丧失和腹侧化相关转录活性增强。
研究意义：该研究确立了年轻人类特异性转座元件作为调控皮层神经发生 timing 的功能性调控架构组分，为理解人类大脑演化提供了新的分子视角。然而，该工作完全局限于人类特有特征，未涉及跨物种比较或演化分析，对于关注细胞类型同源性和神经演化比较基因组学的研究者参考价值有限。
中文全摘要：人类大脑皮层在灵长类演化中显著扩张，但其特异性神经发育程序背后的调控创新仍不清楚。转座元件约占人类基因组一半，是谱系特异性调控新颖性的重要来源，但由于技术挑战难以分辨近 identical 重复序列，其个体对皮层发育的贡献尚不可知。本研究应用单细胞长读长测序（CELLO-seq）生成皮层类器官中年轻人类特异性TE的首个位点解析表达图谱，发现来自SVAs、LTR5Hs和L1Hs等多个TE家族的单个位点在所有主要皮层细胞类型中表达，并携带关键皮层和神经发生转录因子的结合基序。利用CRISPRi在类器官分化期间沉默SVA和LTR5Hs，证明其联合缺失导致放射状胶质细胞-神经元转变的协调失败。
英文摘要（原文）：The human cerebral cortex has undergone remarkable expansion during primate evolution, yet the regulatory innovations underlying human-specific neurodevelopmental programmes remain poorly understood. Transposable elements (TEs) comprise approximately half of the human genome and represent a major source of lineage-specific regulatory novelty, but their individual contributions to cortical development have been inaccessible due to the technical challenge of resolving near-identical repetitive sequences. Here we apply single-cell long-read sequencing (CELLO-seq) to generate the first locus-resolved atlas of young human-specific TE expression in cortical organoids, revealing that individual loci from multiple TE families, including SVAs, LTR5Hs, and L1Hs, are expressed across all major cortical cell types and carry binding motifs for key cortical and neurogenic transcription factors. Using CRISPRi to silence SVAs and LTR5Hs during organoid differentiation, we demonstrate that their combined loss causes coordinated failure of the radial glia-to-neuron transition, characterised by progenitor retention, transcriptional ventralisation, impaired synaptic maturation and a differentiation block in interneuron precursors. Co-expression network and regulon analyses identify loss of dorsal cortical identity programmes and gain of ventralisation-associated transcriptional activity as the dominant molecular consequences of TE silencing. Motif-to-regulon convergence directly links SVA and LTR5Hs chromatin accessibility to the loss of specific TF binding programmes. These findings establish young and human-specific TEs as functional components of the regulatory architecture governing the timing of cortical neurogenesis, with implications for understanding human brain evolution and neurodevelopmental disease.

作者：Hehmeyer J, Harris D, Lowe CJ, Marlow H
DOI: 10.1016/j.cub.2026.10.071 · PubMed 链接: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43072378/
命中关键词：neuron, neural
推荐理由：该论文直接探讨双侧动物神经元的共同起源与多样化，通过跨物种单细胞比较揭示神经元类型的演化模式，与研究者的核心研究方向高度吻合。
背景：动物细胞类型多样性在神经系统中表现尤为壮观。尽管双侧动物神经元的共同进化起源获得越来越多支持，但首个神经元何时、如何分化为现存谱系中多种神经元类型，以及伴随这一分化的分子机制仍不清楚。现有神经元演化模型主要基于果蝇、线虫和人类的有限观察，缺乏跨整个神经系统和多样化物种的系统级比较，限制了对神经元类型起源与演化模式的理解。
研究方法：研究者对半索动物柱头虫（Saccoglossus kowalevskii）进行单细胞RNA测序，构建其神经系统单细胞转录组图谱；随后开展跨物种细胞类型比较分析，涵盖原口动物和后口动物多个物种；通过比较转录组学方法鉴定神经元类型定义基因，分析神经递质通路、离子通道、突触后受体和转录因子的表达模式；并检测古老神经元调控密码的特征，特别关注同源异形域转录因子的富集情况。
研究结果：研究发现同一物种的神经元类型与不同门类的神经元类型几乎无相似性，此前鉴定的跨门类转录相似性仅为全局分化模式的例外。所有神经元共享与突触前膜和轴突细胞骨架等一般特征相关的基因表达，但远程相关物种已进化出细胞类型定义基因的独特组合使用方式：它们共同表达独特的神经递质通路、离子通道、突触后受体和转录因子组合。研究还发现了以同源异形域转录因子强富集为特征的古老神经元调控密码信号。这些结果表明，尽管神经元在核心转录特征上高度共享，但基因表达的大规模重排支持了主要动物谱系间神经元细胞类型身份的大规模转换。
研究意义：该研究为理解双侧动物神经元类型的起源与演化提供了关键的系统级证据，挑战了基于有限物种的神经元演化模型，揭示了神经元类型在深层演化时间尺度上的快速多样化和大规模转换。研究结果对评估共同起源假说与独立起源假说的相对贡献具有重要启示，为后续利用跨物种单细胞比较方法重建祖先神经元类型、解析基因调控网络演化奠定了基础，对演化神经生物学和比较单细胞组学领域具有深远影响。
中文全摘要：动物拥有多样化的终末分化细胞类型，神经系统尤为突出。尽管双侧动物神经元的共同进化起源日益得到支持，但首个神经元何时分化为现存谱系中的多种类型以及伴随的分子机制仍不清楚。现有神经元演化模型基于蝇类、线虫和人类的有限观察，而非跨整个神经系统和多样化物种的系统级比较。作者对柱头虫进行单细胞RNA测序并跨原口和后口动物比较细胞类型，发现物种间神经元类型相似性极低，先前鉴定的跨门类转录相似性仅为全局分化模式的例外。所有神经元共享突触前膜和轴突细胞骨架等一般特征的基因表达，但远程相关物种进化出细胞类型定义基因的独特组合使用，支持主要动物谱系间神经元细胞类型身份的大规模转换。
英文摘要（原文）：Animals harbor a diversity of terminally differentiated cell types. This cell type diversity is particularly spectacular in the nervous system. While the shared evolutionary origin of the bilaterian neuron has been increasingly supported, it remains poorly understood when the first neuron diversified into the many types present in extant lineages and what molecular mechanisms accompanied this neuron diversification. Existing models of neuronal evolution are based on select observations of flies, nematodes, and humans rather than on systems-level comparisons across whole nervous systems and diverse species. By applying single-cell RNA sequencing in the acorn worm Saccoglossus kowalevskii and performing cell type comparisons across protostome and deuterostome species, we show that neuronal cell types in one species bear little resemblance to those found in different phyla. Previously identified cross-phyletic transcriptional similarities represent exceptions to a global pattern of divergence. Specifically, we observe that all neurons share the expression of genes associated with general features such as the presynapse and axon cytoskeleton, but distantly related species have evolved distinct usages of cell-type-defining genes: they co-express unique combinations of neurotransmitter pathways, ion channels, post-synaptic receptors, and transcription factors. Additionally, we recover signal for an ancient neuronal regulatory code characterized by a strong enrichment for homeodomain transcription factors. Overall, these results suggest that while neurons largely share a core set of transcriptional features, a great deal of reshuffling of gene expression has occurred, and this supports large-scale turnover in neuron cell type identities among major animal lineages.

背景：个体间表型变异是进化发生的原材料，基因表达作为遗传变异与表型变异之间的关键中介，其变异范围受到进化与发育约束的限制。尽管单细胞生物中由生物分子随机性和细胞异质性导致的表达变异已被广泛研究，但对于多细胞生物中不同细胞和组织共享相同遗传背景的情况下，表达变异与基因功能、器官功能及基因调控之间的相互作用仍是未解问题。
研究方法：研究者采用高度多重化的3'端bulk RNA测序技术，对三种辐鳍鱼（斑马鱼Danio rerio、白斑狗鱼Esox lucius和斑点雀鳝Lepisosteus oculatus）的野生型个体进行至少九个器官（包括脑、心脏、肝脏、肌肉、性腺、肾、鳃、皮肤和肠）的转录组 profiling；通过量化个体间基因表达变异独立于平均表达水平，结合蛋白质编码序列进化速率分析，探究表达变异与选择压力的关系。

英文摘要（原文）：Phenotypic variation among individuals provides the raw material for evolution, and gene expression is a key mediator between genetic and phenotypic variation. As for phenotypes, the range of gene expression is limited and biased by evolutionary and developmental constraints. Observed expression variability because of biomolecular stochasticity and cell-to-cell heterogeneity has been well studied in isogenic populations of unicellular organisms. However, for multicellular organisms with a diversity of cells and tissues sharing the same genetic background, the interplay between expression variability, gene and organ function, and gene regulation remains an open question. Here, we use highly multiplexed 3'-end bulk RNA sequencing to generate transcriptome profiles spanning at least nine organs in outbred individuals of three ray-finned fish species: zebrafish, Northern pike, and spotted gar. Per organ, we quantify individual-to-individual gene expression variability independent of mean expression level. Lowly variable genes are enriched in cellular housekeeping functions, whereas highly variable genes are enriched in stimulus-response functions. Furthermore, highly variable genes evolve under weaker purifying selection at the protein-coding sequence, indicating that intraspecies expression variability predicts interspecies protein sequence divergence. Genes that are broadly expressed across organs are both highly expressed and lowly variable, whereas organ-biased genes are typically highly variable within their top organ. Among organ-biased genes, patterns of expression variance are dependent on the top organ. Specifically, brain- and gonad-biased genes have lowly variable expression across different organs, suggesting stabilizing selection. These patterns suggest that gene regulatory mechanisms evolve under organ-specific selective pressures.

聚焦方向
一是转座元件功能化研究：揭示年轻人类特异性TEs从"寄生序列"到发育调控元件的角色转变，SVA和LTR5Hs直接参与神经发生时序控制
