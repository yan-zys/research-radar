Whole-body single-cell atlas of an adult vertebrate, Danionella cerebrum.
成年脊椎动物Danionella cerebrum的全身单细胞图谱
为构建首个完整脊椎动物细胞类型转录组图谱，作者以小型透明硬骨鱼Danionella cerebrum为模型，进行区域分层单细胞RNA测序并结合空间转录组学，在单细胞分辨率上映射全身细胞类型与基因表达；发现空间定位的神经细胞类型、成体分子位置记忆、幼态持续特征及保守的体区与附肢特化程序；通过与脊椎动物比较揭示保守神经细胞类型，并通过再生数据集解析端脑再生中的表达动态。该图谱为生物学和神经科学提供了全面的脊椎动物全身细胞类型资源。
Danionella cerebrum（小型透明硬骨鱼）/ 约300,000细胞 / scRNA-seq + MERFISH空间转录组 / 有脑区及神经细胞类型区分（端脑、小脑等）

作者：Atabay KD, Aoude P, Park C, Kadobianskyi M, Paugois OH, Judkewitz B, Reddien PW
DOI: 10.1126/science.aeg1125 · PubMed 链接: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42956121/
命中关键词：neural, single-cell atlas, Cell atlas, Spatial transcriptomics
推荐理由：该论文构建了首个成年脊椎动物全身单细胞图谱，包含神经细胞的区域特异性区分，为跨物种比较神经演化提供了重要参考资源。
背景：完整的动物细胞类型转录组图谱是理解细胞类型组成、组织原则和演化规律的基础。脊椎动物因其体型较大、结构复杂，构建全身单细胞图谱长期面临挑战。Danionella cerebrum是一种体长仅约10毫米的小型透明硬骨鱼，具有脊椎动物保守的器官系统，且为研究器官再生提供了模型，但其全身体细胞组成尚未在单细胞水平解析。
研究方法：研究者对成年Danionella cerebrum进行区域分层单细胞RNA测序，结合空间转录组学技术（MERFISH）实现单细胞分辨率的基因表达定位；通过跨物种比较分析识别保守神经细胞类型；利用端脑损伤再生模型获取再生过程的时间序列单细胞数据。
研究结果：研究构建了覆盖全身的细胞类型图谱，识别出大量空间定位的神经细胞类型，包括端脑、小脑等脑区特异性神经元；发现成体组织中保留分子位置记忆的细胞群以及幼态持续的形态特征；揭示成年结缔组织中保守的体区与附肢特化基因程序；跨物种比较鉴定出脊椎动物保守的神经细胞类型标志物；端脑再生过程中呈现特征性的基因表达动态变化。
研究意义：该研究首次实现成年脊椎动物的全身单细胞图谱构建，填补了脊椎动物全身体细胞参考图谱的空白。其作为小型脊椎动物模型的高通量、高分辨率数据资源，为跨物种细胞类型比较、神经演化及再生生物学研究提供了重要基准，尤其有助于理解脊椎动物神经系统细胞类型的保守性与多样性起源。
中文全摘要：完整的脊椎动物细胞类型转录组图谱有助于理解动物细胞类型组成、组织与演化。小型化、透明且具再生能力的硬骨鱼Danionella cerebrum使全脊椎动物单细胞分析成为可能。作者进行区域分层的成年Danionella全身单细胞RNA测序，结合空间转录组学在单细胞分辨率上映射细胞类型与基因表达；基于区域基因表达特征界定空间定位的神经细胞类型；揭示携带成体分子位置记忆的细胞类型、幼态持续特征，以及成年结缔组织中保守的体区与附肢特化程序；比较分析发现保守神经细胞类型，再生数据集揭示端脑再生中的表达动态。
英文摘要（原文）：A complete cell type transcriptome atlas of a vertebrate could promote understanding of animal cell type composition, organization, and evolution. The miniaturized, transparent, and regenerative teleost Danionella cerebrum brings whole-vertebrate single-cell profiling experiments within reach. We performed region-stratified single-cell RNA sequencing across the adult Danionella body and mapped cell types and gene expression at single-cell resolution using spatial transcriptomics. We delineated spatially distinct neural cell types on the basis of their regional gene expression signatures. The body-wide atlas elucidated cell types harboring adult molecular positional memory, uncovered paedomorphic features, and revealed conserved body-region and appendage-specification programs in adult connective tissue. Comparative analyses revealed conserved neural cell types, and regeneration datasets uncovered expression dynamics during telencephalon regeneration. This whole-vertebrate transcriptome atlas yields a comprehensive resource for myriad questions in biology and neuroscience.

Spontaneous neural activity in the developing ant olfactory system
发育中蚂蚁嗅觉系统的自发神经活动
为探究蚂蚁嗅觉系统发育是否依赖自发神经活动，作者在克隆突袭蚁Ooceraea biroi的转基因品系中使用GCaMP6s钙成像技术，发现发育中的嗅觉感觉神经元在蛹期即表现自发钙瞬变且随变态发育呈现阶段性变化，晚期出现双侧触角同步的节律性锯齿状活动，提示蚂蚁嗅觉系统发育逻辑可能更接近哺乳动物而非果蝇，为比较昆虫嗅觉系统演化提供了新的发育视角。

推荐理由：该研究聚焦于蚂蚁嗅觉系统的发育神经活动，虽涉及社会性昆虫（蚂蚁）神经系统发育，但缺乏跨物种比较视角、单细胞组学技术及演化分析框架，与该研究者核心的跨物种细胞类型同源比较和神经演化研究关联有限。
背景：自发神经活动在哺乳动物嗅觉系统发育中至关重要，依赖嗅觉受体表达的嗅觉感觉神经元自发活动模式参与轴突向嗅小球的分选。果蝇作为简化的模型系统，其触角叶小球形成不依赖功能性嗅觉受体。蚂蚁作为社会性昆虫具有大幅扩张的嗅觉系统，其发育机制在演化上更接近哺乳动物还是果蝇尚不明确。
研究方法：研究者构建在嗅觉感觉神经元中表达GCaMP6s钙指示剂的转基因克隆突袭蚁Ooceraea biroi品系，利用活体钙成像技术在不同变态发育阶段的透明蛹中进行动态记录，分析自发钙活动的时序特征、细胞间相关性及双侧同步性。
研究结果：发育中的蚂蚁嗅觉感觉神经元从嗅小球形成早期即表现自发钙瞬变。早期阶段活动快速、细胞间变异显著且群体无相关性；中期变态阶段钙事件延长、每秒峰值数下降；接近羽化时出现新的活动模式，表现为节律性、锯齿形的钙事件，且双侧触角的嗅觉感觉神经元同步参与。
研究意义：该研究首次在昆虫中揭示了类似哺乳动物的发育性自发神经活动，提示蚂蚁扩张的嗅觉系统可能采用与哺乳动物更为相似的发育逻辑，为理解社会性昆虫嗅觉系统的演化创新提供了新的发育视角，但研究未涉及跨物种细胞类型比较或演化机制分析。
中文全摘要：自发神经活动是哺乳动物神经发育的重要特征。与果蝇不同，蚂蚁具有大幅扩张的嗅觉系统且需要功能性嗅觉受体才能正常发育。为检验蚂蚁嗅觉感觉神经元是否表现自发活动，作者在表达GCaMP6s的克隆突袭蚁Ooceraea biroi透明蛹中进行活体钙成像，发现发育中的OSN从嗅小球形成早期即表现自发钙瞬变，且活动特征随变态发育而变化：早期活动快速、细胞间变异大、群体无相关性；中期活动减慢、钙事件延长、峰值减少；晚期出现双侧触角OSN同步参与的节律性锯齿状钙事件。
英文摘要（原文）：Spontaneous neural activity is a pillar of mammalian neurodevelopment. In mammals, developing olfactory sensory neurons (OSNs) show spontaneous activity patterns that depend on the olfactory receptor (OR) they express. This activity is necessary for proper sorting of OSN axons into olfactory bulb glomeruli. This contrasts with the simpler olfactory system of the fruit fly Drosophila melanogaster, where antennal lobe glomeruli form without functional ORs. Compared to Drosophila, ants have a vastly expanded olfactory system and, unlike Drosophila, require functional ORs for proper development. This raises the possibility that ants, like mammals, rely on spontaneous neural activity. To test whether developing ant OSNs are spontaneously active, we performed live calcium imaging in translucent pupae of the clonal raider ant Ooceraea biroi, using a transgenic line expressing the genetically encoded calcium indicator GCaMP6s in OSNs. Developing O. biroi OSNs exhibit spontaneous calcium transients from the earliest stages of glomerulus formation, and features of this activity change over the course of metamorphosis. During early stages, activity is faster, varies widely between cells, and is uncorrelated across the OSN population. By mid-metamorphosis, activity has slowed, with longer calcium events and fewer peaks per cell. As pupae approach eclosion, a new mode appears, with rhythmic, sawtooth-shaped calcium events that synchronously engage OSNs in both antennae. This stage-dependent spontaneous activity suggests that the expanded ant olfactory system may use a developmental logic more akin to mammals than to Drosophila.

英文摘要（原文）：Although calcium homeostasis is disrupted in metabolic diseases, its metabolic regulation remains unclear. Here, we identify a mechanism by which fumarate suppresses sarco/endoplasmic reticulum (ER) calcium ion-adenosine triphosphatase (SERCA) activity via succination of a conserved cysteine residue, impairing ER calcium uptake and promoting metabolic dysfunction in Drosophila. In mammalian cells, high glucose or fumarate inhibits SERCA activity and increases ER calcium release and cytosolic and mitochondrial calcium levels. Mechanistically, we show that fumarate covalently modifies SERCA2b at Cys875 and that a Cys875Ser mutant resists fumarate-induced inhibition. In Drosophila, knock-in flies with the corresponding Cys875Ser mutation preserve ER calcium homeostasis and are protected from hyperglycemia, glucose intolerance, and reduced survival on a high-sugar diet. These effects are phenocopied by pharmacological fumarate reduction or allosteric SERCA activation. Collectively, these findings suggest that fumarate-mediated SERCA inhibition provides a mechanistic link between glucose metabolism and calcium homeostasis, with potential relevance to metabolic dysfunction.

英文摘要（原文）：The turquoise karst lakes of China's Jiuzhaigou Nature Reserve harbor among the highest natural calcium concentrations known in freshwater, around threefold higher than adjacent rivers. We show that this calcium tolerance arose through evolutionary loss of vitamin D signaling. Lake and river populations diverged ∼410,000 years ago. 英文摘要（原文）：Forward genetic screening links genotype to phenotype by introducing random genetic perturbations and identifying phenotype-altering mutations. Although genome-scale screens are routine for simple phenotypes in cultured cells, extending them to complex image-based phenotypes remains challenging. Here, we present spatially resolved CRISPR screening (SPARCS), a microscopy-based platform for forward genetic screening on single-cell images. SPARCS physically isolates mutants in situ by automated laser microdissection, enabling image-based screening at unprecedented scale with multimodal hit phenotyping. These results establish SPARCS as a scalable platform for genome-wide genetic screening of complex cellular phenotypes with a proteome-level readout.

研究结果：HAIKA在聚类、降维和形态嵌入任务上优于多数基线深度学习模型和传统形态描述符。膜翅目翅膀形态演化呈现模块化解耦：形状模块和翅脉模块以不同速率演化。翅膀形态演化与关键生态转变（如寄生性、真社会性）不存在一致关联，表明生态适应与形态演化之间存在复杂性。HAIKA提取的连续字符能有效改善基于形态的膜翅目系统发育重建。
研究意义：HAIKA为大规模表型分析提供了可扩展、自动化的图像学工具，可推广至2D和3D数据集及多样分类群。该研究揭示了膜翅目翅膀形态演化的模块化和速率异质性，挑战了形态演化与生态适应简单对应的观点。然而，该工作完全聚焦于昆虫翅膀外部形态，未涉及神经系统、细胞类型或分子调控网络，对脑演化及单细胞比较研究的直接参考价值有限。
中文全摘要：现有方法的可扩展性、主观性和可解释性不足限制了生物形态演化量化及表型数据整合。作者开发HAIKA（层级注意力可解释K维自编码器），从未标注标准化图像中提取连续形态表征并标注贡献区域。应用于广翅目昆虫前翅时，HAIKA在聚类、降维和形态嵌入上优于多数基线深度学习模型和传统描述符。应用于膜翅目时，HAIKA衍生连续字符改善了基于形态的系统发育重建，并揭示翅形和翅脉的演化动力学：两者以解耦速率演化，且与寄生性或真社会性等生态转变不一致。HAIKA为多样本、2D和3D数据集的表型分析提供了可扩展途径。
英文摘要（原文）：Quantifying biomorphological evolution and integrating phenotypic data into large-scale ecological and evolutionary studies are often limited by the scalability, subjectivity, and poor interpretability of existing methods. We introduce HAIKA (Hierarchical Attention-based Interpretable K-dimensional Autoencoder), a self-supervised residual channel-attention autoencoder that extracts continuous morphological representations from unlabeled standardized images and highlights the image regions that contribute to those representations. Applied to insect forewings across broad pterygote lineages, HAIKA outperformed most baseline deep learning models and conventional descriptors in clustering, dimensionality reduction, and morphological embedding. When applied to Hymenoptera (i.e., ants, bees, and wasps), the HAIKA-derived continuous characters improved morphology-based phylogenetic reconstruction and illuminated the evolutionary dynamics of wing shape and venation. Our results reveal that wing morphological evolution in Hymenoptera proceeds at decoupled rates across shape and venation modules and is not consistently aligned with key ecological transitions such as parasitoidism or eusociality. HAIKA provides a scalable, image-based route for phenotypic analyses, broadly applicable across diverse taxa and to both 2D and 3D datasets.
