命中关键词：neural development, neurogenesis, brain, neural, scRNA-seq
推荐理由：该研究局限于小鼠单一物种的视网膜发育，无任何跨物种比较或演化视角，且聚焦于分子机制而非细胞类型演化，不符合研究者的核心关注点。
背景：染色质重塑在神经发育中起关键调控作用，但具体分子机制尚不完全清楚。ChAHP复合体由CHD4、ADNP和HP1蛋白组成，其中CHD4和ADNP的突变均与神经发育障碍相关。尽管该复合体被认为参与神经前体细胞能力状态的调控，但其在视网膜发育中的具体功能及亚基间功能分工仍缺乏深入研究。
研究方法：作者使用Chx10-Cre驱动系统构建小鼠视网膜特异性Adnp和Chd4条件敲除模型。通过免疫组织化学和共免疫沉淀验证蛋白表达和复合体形成。采用单细胞RNA测序（scRNA-seq）分析基因表达变化，并使用cut&run-seq鉴定基因组结合位点，以解析ChAHP复合体的调控机制。
研究结果：研究发现ADNP和CHD4在视网膜各细胞类型中普遍共表达并形成复合体。两种条件敲除均导致发育中细胞死亡升高，但表型存在差异：Adnp敲除引起前体细胞数量减少和增殖缺陷，导致成熟视网膜各细胞类型减少；Chd4敲除则表现为细胞命运失衡和前体增殖延长。scRNA-seq显示两者具有共同转录特征，但Chd4敲除影响更为严重。cut&run-seq表明ADNP和CHD4被招募至相同基因组位点，其中许多位点也被CTCF占据。
研究意义：该研究揭示了ChAHP复合体在视网膜发育中的关键作用，并阐明CHD4具有ChAHP非依赖性的额外功能。研究提示不同脑区对ChAHP的依赖性存在差异，因此对该复合体突变的遗传易感性也不同。这一发现为理解神经发育障碍的区域特异性 vulnerability 提供了新视角，但该研究完全局限于小鼠单一模型，缺乏演化或比较生物学维度。
中文全摘要：表观遗传重塑被认为调控神经前体细胞能力状态的进展。本研究检测ChAHP染色质重塑复合体在视网膜发育中的功能。ChAHP由CHD4、ADNP和HP1蛋白组成。在小鼠视网膜中，ADNP和CHD4共表达并形成复合体。作者构建视网膜特异性条件敲除模型，发现两种敲除均引起细胞死亡增加，但Adnp敲除导致前体细胞数量和增殖缺陷，而Chd4敲除则引起细胞命运失衡和前体增殖延长。scRNA-seq揭示共同转录特征，但Chd4敲除对基因表达影响更严重。cut&run-seq显示两者被招募至相同基因组位点。
英文摘要（原文）：Epigenetic remodelling is thought to regulate the progression of competence states in neural progenitor cells. Here, we examined the functions of the ChAHP chromatin remodelling complex during retinal development. ChAHP consists of the ATP-dependent nucleosome remodeller CHD4, the zinc finger and homeodomain transcription factor ADNP, and the HP1 heterochromatin proteins (Cbx1/3/5). Notably, both CHD4 and ADNP are genetically associated with neurodevelopmental disorders (NDDs), but ChAHP functions in neural development are not fully understood. In the retina, we found that both ADNP and CHD4 proteins were co-expressed ubiquitously in each cell type throughout development, and the two proteins formed a complex in vivo. Next, we generated retina-specific conditional knockouts (cKOs) to both Adnp and Chd4 using the Chx10-Cre driver. We observed phenotypic correspondence and divergence. Developmentally, we observed elevated cell death in both Adnp and Chd4 cKOs, but Adnp cKOs also exhibited deficits in progenitor numbers and proliferation. As a result, mature Adnp cKOs were hypoplastic with reductions in each cell type. We previously described a more complex phenotype in Chd4 cKOs, where cell fates were imbalanced and progenitor proliferation was prolonged, but these features were absent from the Adnp cKO. To understand how ChAHP regulates gene expression, we compared Adnp and Chd4 cKOs via scRNA-seq, which revealed common transcriptional signatures. However, Chd4 cKOs exhibited more severe effects on gene expression in line with its stronger effects on cell fate. We then performed cut&run-seq, which suggested that Adnp and Chd4 are recruited to the same genomic loci - many of which are also occupied by the Ctcf transcription factor. Taken together, our data suggest that ChAHP regulates retinal growth, but that CHD4 has additional ChAHP-independent functions in regulating progenitor fate decisions. These findings point to the idea that during neurogenesis, some brain regions may have greater dependence on ChAHP, and therefore greater vulnerability to genetic mutation.

推荐理由：该研究聚焦于Hedgehog形态发生子的分子信号机制，虽涉及发育保守性但未涉及脑/神经系统演化、跨物种细胞类型比较或单细胞组学，与该研究者核心方向不符。
背景：形态发生梯度是动物发育的核心调控机制，Hedgehog蛋白的双重脂质修饰（棕榈酸酯和胆固醇）使其具有高度疏水性，却能形成长程浓度梯度，这一悖论长期未解。理解脂质修饰如何分别贡献于信号传导与扩散，对揭示发育信号的演化机制具有重要意义。
研究方法：研究者采用活细胞单分子追踪技术直接观察Hedgehog配体的动态扩散行为，并工程化改造合成信号配体以分离两种脂质修饰的独立功能，结合受体结合实验和信号活性测定，系统比较了不同修饰状态下的扩散特性与信号效力。
研究结果：研究发现两种脂质修饰均增强信号效力但机制不同：棕榈酸酯直接促进受体 engagement，而胆固醇通过拓扑限制配体于细胞膜表面实现局部富集，却牺牲了长程扩散能力。胆固醇的功能揭示了一种固有的信号效力-梯度形成权衡，暗示非信号共受体可能通过类似的膜限制机制调控形态发生梯度。
研究意义：该研究首次在分子机制层面阐明了Hedgehog脂质修饰的功能分工，提出了信号效力与梯度范围之间的进化权衡假说。然而，该工作完全聚焦于单一分子的生化机制，未涉及跨物种比较、神经系统发育或细胞类型演化，与该研究者关注的宏观演化问题距离较远。
中文全摘要：Hedgehog形态发生子存在矛盾特性：虽因双重脂质修饰而疏水，却能形成高度保守的空间浓度梯度。通过活细胞单分子追踪和合成信号配体，作者发现棕榈酸酯直接促进受体结合，而胆固醇则将分泌的形态发生子拓扑限制在细胞表面，以脂质膜作为非信号共受体增强局部浓度但限制长程扩散，揭示了信号效力与梯度形成之间的固有权衡。
英文摘要（原文）：Hedgehog family morphogens present an interesting paradox: Despite being hydrophobic because of dual-lipid modifications, they form spatial concentration gradients that are highly conserved and essential for many aspects of metazoan development. Using live-cell single-molecule tracking and engineered synthetic signaling ligands, we isolated the distinct contribution of each lipid modification to Hedgehog diffusion and signaling potency. We found that although both lipid modifications enhance signaling potency, they do so through different mechanisms. Palmitate directly promotes receptor engagement, whereas cholesterol topologically confines secreted morphogens on the cell surface, effectively using the lipid membrane as a nonsignaling co-receptor that enriches ligands locally at the cost of restricting long-range diffusion. Our results on the function of cholesterol point to an intrinsic trade-off between signaling potency and gradient formation, with implications for the evolution and mechanism of nonsignaling co-receptors.

Here, we present a descriptor-guided transfer learning framework, named IonNet, to predict ion mobility for compounds, regardless of structure accessibility. IonNet adopts a multichannel subnetwork architecture that captures universal representations by integrating static and statistical descriptors of compounds. We demonstrate the exceptional performance of IonNet in predicting ion mobility, consistently outperforming 16 ablation study combinations and previous deep learning models. Leveraging the universal adaptability of chemical representations, IonNet not only uncovers 87 FICs among ∼4500 stable perfectly stoichiometric compounds but also efficiently pinpoints ∼63,000 prospective FICs from ∼5 million substituted compounds. This study not only presents a full-chain artificial intelligence tool for identifying FICs but also offers compositional principles governing ion mobility, thereby accelerating the development of energy storage and conversion.

英文摘要（原文）：Regulatory DNA controls when and where genes are expressed, making it a key driver of phenotypic evolution. Yet detecting selection in non-coding regions remains difficult, as most approaches rely on sequence conservation or changes in substitution rate rather than molecular effects. RegEvol bridges this gap by linking machine learning-based predictions of transcription factor binding to explicit evolutionary models. It uses the distribution of predicted mutational effects to infer fitness functions under different evolutionary scenarios including random drift, stabilising selection, and directional selection. Through maximum-likelihood estimation, it identifies the regime that best explains observed changes along a lineage from an ancestral sequence. When substitution numbers are limited, such as along short evolutionary branches, likelihood differences can be aggregated across sets of regulatory elements to increase statistical power. RegEvol corrects biases that affected previous tests based on machine learning of transcription factor binding, while remaining conservative across different levels of divergence. Applied to over 3 million Drosophila melanogaster regulatory regions, we identify 5.1% under directional selection, enriched near reproductive and immune genes. Applying the aggregation strategy to human CTCF binding across tissues reveals enrichment of directional signals in nervous and male reproductive systems. The framework is readily applicable to experimentally detected regulatory elements with alignable ancestral sequences and is flexible to future advances in understanding regulatory function, providing a powerful basis for investigating adaptation in non-coding regions.
