英文摘要（原文）：Subplate neurons (SpNs) are early-born neurons in the mammalian neocortex and play a pivotal role in cortical development. It is widely believed that SpNs are predominantly lost due to postnatal cell death, with only a few remnants contributing to layer 6b (L6b) neurons in the adult neocortex, but the extent to which SpNs persist as L6b neurons remains largely unknown. To address this, we conducted a comprehensive birthdate analysis using multiple ethynyl deoxyuridine (EdU) injections over 24 h, thereby precisely tracking the survival and distribution of SpNs in the adult mouse neocortex. 英文摘要（原文）：Alcohol craving is a key process in alcohol abuse, especially during young adulthood, a period of heightened vulnerability and continued brain development. Although acute physical exercise has been proposed as a potential strategy to reduce craving and modulate affective and cognitive processes, current evidence remains limited regarding its effectiveness and underlying mechanisms. This systematic review examined the effects of brief exercise interventions on alcohol craving in young adults with problematic alcohol use or alcohol use disorder (AUD) and explored available evidence on associated neurobiological responses. Four electronic databases were searched for studies published between 2016 and 2026 following PRISMA guidelines. The review protocol was registered in PROSPERO (CRD420261371036). Eligible studies included participants aged 18-29 years and assessed alcohol craving following a single bout of exercise. Random-effects meta-analyses using Hedges' g were conducted when sufficient data were available, while additional outcomes were synthesized narratively. Six studies met the inclusion criteria. Meta-analysis showed a moderate and statistically significant reduction in alcohol craving following acute exercise compared with baseline or control conditions (g ≈ 0.55), with moderate between-study heterogeneity. Narrative findings indicated that acute exercise was often accompanied by reductions in anxiety and negative affective state, modulation of stress-related physiological markers, improvements in executive functioning, and changes in indices of brain and autonomic activity, although findings varied across studies. Overall, available evidence suggests that a single session of physical exercise may be associated with short-term reductions in alcohol craving in young adults, highlighting the need for further clinically focused and methodologically robust research.

为解决单细胞转录组数据规模化存储与AI兼容性问题，作者开发了GENOAR平台，利用AI智能体和并行计算处理约5400万个人和小鼠细胞数据，实现数据预处理、智能标注与检索；发现AI智能体在数据检索性能上可与人类专家媲美；展示了其在视网膜母细胞瘤祖细胞特征分析中的应用，为构建可扩展的单细胞图谱提供了技术框架，但缺乏演化生物学应用。

推荐理由：该论文为AI驱动的单细胞数据平台工具开发，无演化比较视角，物种仅涉及人和小鼠，且以疾病应用（视网膜母细胞瘤）为案例，不符合研究者核心兴趣。
背景：单细胞转录组学数据呈爆炸式增长，但数据的规模化整合、标准化存储与AI兼容性仍面临挑战。现有数据库和分析工具难以同时满足人类研究者和AI系统的需求，亟需开发新型数据引擎以实现高效导航与智能检索。疾病研究如视网膜母细胞瘤的肿瘤微环境解析，也需要大规模图谱数据的支持。
研究方法：作者开发GENOAR平台，整合并行计算进行单细胞数据预处理加速，设计双AI智能体系统：AI命名师采用智能体技术规范细胞类型标签，AI图书管理员执行数据检索任务。平台整合人和小鼠超5400万细胞的单细胞转录组数据，采用分析就绪格式存储，并应用于视网膜母细胞瘤原发肿瘤及类器官环境的祖细胞转录特征分析。
研究结果：GENOAR在三个关键组件上表现优异：并行计算显著加速预处理流程；AI智能体成功实现数据存储、标注标准化与智能检索，检索性能达到人类专家水平；基于平台分析揭示了视网膜母细胞瘤及其类器官环境中与祖细胞相关的转录特征；平台验证可用于后生动物单细胞图谱的按需构建，但演示物种仅限人和小鼠。
研究意义：GENOAR展示了构建AI兼容、可扩展且可重复的单细胞转录组图谱的可行性，为大规模单细胞数据整合提供了技术基础设施。然而，该工作主要贡献在于计算工具开发，未涉及跨物种比较、细胞类型同源性或神经系统演化等演化生物学核心问题，对专注于脑演化与细胞类型演化的研究者直接价值有限。
中文全摘要：尽管生成模型已革新诸多领域，构建可扩展的AI兼容数据集仍具挑战。本研究开发了GENOAR平台，利用AI智能体和并行计算覆盖约5400万细胞，用于多种下游任务。并行计算加速单细胞数据预处理；AI智能体实现数据存储与探索。GENOAR包含AI命名师（规范非结构化标签）和AI图书管理员（数据检索），呈现人和小鼠超5400万细胞的分析就绪格式。应用方面揭示了视网膜母细胞瘤及其类器官中祖细胞相关转录特征，并验证了其用于后生动物单细胞图谱按需构建的可行性。
英文摘要（原文）：Although generative models have revolutionized many fields, including genomics, building a scalable dataset compatible with artificial intelligence (AI) is essential yet underexplored. Here, we developed a Global Engine for navigating the Omnicell space via Agent-based Research (GENOAR) that covers approximately 54 million cells for diverse downstream tasks using AI agents and parallel computing. GENOAR outperforms in three parts. Parallel computations accelerated the preprocessing of single-cell transcriptomic data; the other components enabled agent-assisted data storage and exploration for both AI models and humans. GENOAR involves two AI agents: an AI nomenclaturist ordered the unstructured labels of the single-cell transcriptomic profiles, and an AI librarian retrieved data. GENOAR presents transcriptomic profiles from more than 54 million cells from humans and mice in an analysis-ready format for humans and AI agents. AI agents in GENOAR exhibited successful retrieval performance compared with human experts. Using GENOAR, we elucidated progenitor cell-associated transcriptional signatures in native retinoblastoma and organoid environments. GENOAR was released and validated for the on-demand construction of single-cell atlases for metazoa. In conclusion, using GENOAR, the development of a single-cell transcriptomic atlas compatible with both AI systems and human researchers is feasible, scalable, and reproducible.
